# What is on the NFX YouTube channel

NFX's [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@nfxinsights/videos) is a back catalog of founder education from
2017 to 2026. FACT: the channel page shows about 36 thousand subscribers and 501 videos, and the Videos tab listed 337
when scrolled to the end on 2026-10-21. Everything is free to watch. A note on a handle: the channel is
`@nfxinsights`; the handle `@NFX` belongs to an unrelated channel.

## The series

| Series | Roughly | Era | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed Masterclass (season two) | 10 episodes | 2026 | See [the episode guide](speed-masterclass). Episode 4, the talk to Stanford students, is the most watched. |
| Network Effects Masterclass (season one) | trailer and clips | 2022 | The full course is on nfx.com behind a free login; see [the episode guide](network-effects-masterclass). |
| The NFX Podcast | about 60 | 2020 to 2025 | Long interviews with founders and investors, plus company stories such as PlanGrid, DocSend, Credit Karma and Waze. |
| Live at Stanford business school | 3 | 2023 to 2024 | Talks by Pete Flint and James Currier on AI and startups. |
| Startup mini-series | about 10 | 2019 to 2021 | Short explainers: what makes a strong CEO, five core metrics, what network effects are. |
| Killer wedge mini-series | 12 clips and an overview | 2023 | See [wedges](wedges-timing-and-idea-screens). |
| Fundraising lessons | 16 clips | 2019 | One-minute pitching tips. |
| Investor pitch advice | about 120 clips | 2017 to 2018 | Short answers from many investors on decks, markets, timing and teams. |
| The Founders' List | about 40 | 2020 | Readings of famous memos and essays, such as an early Amazon shareholder letter. |
| Ask NFX | 2 | 2025 | Partners answering founder questions, such as when to pivot. |

## What was read for this series

FACT: 53 items were read in depth: the ten Speed Masterclass and ten Network Effects episodes from their official
transcripts, 29 other videos from their YouTube transcripts, and four NFX web pages. The rest of the
channel, mostly the short 2017 and 2018 investor clips, most podcast interviews and the memo readings, is cataloged
here but not summarized. Assessment: the investor clips are mainly about pitching to investors, so skipping them loses
little for someone who is not raising money.

## Caveats

- YouTube's automatic captions garble names and numbers, so figures in the videos are the speakers' own claims and
  some speaker names are not confirmed.
- View counts and ages are as shown on 2026-10-21 and will drift.
