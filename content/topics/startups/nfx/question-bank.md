# A question bank for challenging a business idea

These eighteen questions are drawn from the NFX material in this series and phrased to be said out loud to a founder, or
to yourself. Plain English first: they are not a scorecard. Use two or three at a time, and notice which ones are
uncomfortable. Each group names the part of the series it comes from.

## Idea quality
(from [the idea talk](copy-a-lot-but-not-all) and [wedges, timing and idea screens](wedges-timing-and-idea-screens))
1. Which proven business is this modeled on, and what exactly are the two or three obvious changes?
2. Is this a crowded market with real revenue in it, or an empty one where you may be carrying hidden demand risk?
3. What made this possible now and not five years ago?
4. Price times how often times how many people: is the product big enough to matter?
5. If an honest investor sorted it into mediocre, uncertain or fertile soil, which would it be, and why?

## Differentiation
(from [copy a lot, but not all](copy-a-lot-but-not-all) and [the network effects course](network-effects-masterclass))
6. Who is the incumbent, and what would copying your model cost them in their own revenue?
7. If you are ten percent better, why would anyone switch? What would a hundred times better look like?
8. What is the niche inside the niche, and who are the first hundred users by name?
9. Which of the twelve wedges is this, and could it use a second?
10. Which defenses do you have today (network effect, brand, embedding, scale, a cornered resource), and which do you
    need by month twelve?

## Speed and learning
(from [the Speed Masterclass](speed-masterclass))
11. What is the smallest version that could be in front of real people by Friday?
12. What can be done by hand behind the scenes to test demand before anything is built?
13. What is the top bottleneck this week, and what would you pay to remove it?
14. What did you learn since yesterday that changes what you do tomorrow?
15. Which fear, of looking foolish, of failing or of succeeding, is actually slowing this down?

## Structure and focus
(from [AI-era companies](ai-era-companies))
16. Which work here is done by a person, which by AI, and which by AI with a person reviewing it?
17. Which of your projects should be parked or stopped, and what would you do with the freed hours?
18. If you could only keep one thing you are building, which one, and what is the evidence?
