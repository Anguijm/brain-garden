# Shipyards and the maritime industrial base

Standing knowledge about naval ship repair and the industrial base around it, kept
separately from the weekly [Waterfront Brief](projects/waterfront-brief/) because it
outlives any one issue. The brief reports what happened this week; these notes are the
background a story gets read against.

The research that feeds both runs through the same lens: who competes for the trades,
whose hulls arrive at the pier and when, which planning yard or builder is worth talking
to before that work lands, which vendors get consumed upstream, and what method or
qualification path is worth adopting.

## Notes
