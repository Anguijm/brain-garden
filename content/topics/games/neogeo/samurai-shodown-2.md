# Samurai Shodown II (Neo Geo)

**Developer:** SNK | **Publisher:** SNK | **Year:** 1994

---

## Introduction

The best weapon-based 2D fighter of the 1990s, and the entry the series never bettered despite many attempts. Where *Street Fighter* is about combos, *Samurai Shodown* is about **spacing and single decisive hits** — a heavy slash can take a third of a health bar, so the game is fought at the edge of range with enormous tension.

---

## Controls

- **A:** Light slash. **B:** Medium slash. **C:** Heavy slash.
- **D:** Kick. **A+B:** Hard slash. **B+C:** Kick variant.
- **Joystick:** Move; back to block; **down-down** to pick up dropped weapons.

---

## Why it plays differently

**Damage is enormous.** Three or four clean hits end a round. Combos exist but are short; the game is about landing one correct attack, not fifteen.

**Range is everything.** Each character's weapon has a distinct reach, and the whole match is a negotiation about who controls the space between. Whiffing a heavy slash is usually fatal.

**Disarming.** A clash or certain attacks can knock the weapon out of your opponent's hands. **A disarmed fighter is drastically weaker** and must scramble to retrieve it, which turns the match on its head. This is the mechanic people remember.

---

## The Rage gauge

Taking damage fills a **POW meter**. When full, your attack power spikes for a limited time and you can perform a **super special**. Because it fills by being hit, it is a comeback mechanic rather than a reward for winning.

---

## The referee and the delivery man

A referee calls the match and reacts to it. A delivery man occasionally runs across the stage throwing items — bombs, food, weapons. These are not decoration: an item can change a round.

---

## The roster

Fifteen fighters, each with a genuinely distinct weapon and range: Haohmaru's katana, Nakoruru's small blade and hawk, Ukyo's iaido draw, Genjuro's long reach, and Hanzo and Galford's ninja tools.

**Nakoruru** and **Haohmaru** are the usual starting points.

---

## Strategy

**Stay just outside their range.** Most rounds are lost by stepping in too early.

**Do not whiff heavy slashes.** The recovery is long and the punish is severe.

**Retrieve your weapon immediately** if disarmed. Down-down over it.

**Use the POW meter when it fills** — it decays.

**Block low.** Sweeps are the most common opener.
