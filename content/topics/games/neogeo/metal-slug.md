# Metal Slug: Super Vehicle-001 (Neo Geo)

**Developer:** Nazca Corporation | **Publisher:** SNK | **Year:** 1996

---

## Introduction

The most beautifully animated 2D action game ever made, and that is close to a factual claim rather than an opinion. Nazca — a studio formed by ex-Irem staff who had made *In the Hunt* — hand-drew every frame, and the result is a game where soldiers stumble when they run, prisoners scratch themselves while waiting to be rescued, and a tank's treads deform over terrain.

It runs on the Neo Geo, arcade hardware SNK also sold as a home console at a price nobody could justify, and it is a run-and-gun where the joke and the craft are inseparable.

---

## Controls

- **Joystick:** Move; up/down aims, down to crouch.
- **A:** Fire — hold for automatic.
- **B:** Jump.
- **C:** Grenade — limited, and much stronger than the gun.
- **In a Slug:** A fires the cannon, B jumps, and **down + down** ejects you.

---

## The Slug

The SV-001 tank is the title character. It absorbs several hits before exploding, its cannon kills most things in one shot, and it can duck, jump and crush enemies.

**Two things people miss:**

**The Slug is a health pool, not a vehicle.** Hits damage it rather than killing you, so staying in it through a dangerous stretch is worth more than looking cool.

**You can abandon it as a weapon.** Eject from a damaged Slug and it explodes shortly after, doing heavy damage to anything nearby. Deliberately driving a dying Slug into a boss and jumping out is a real tactic.

---

## Weapons

| Pickup | Effect |
|---|---|
| **H** — Heavy Machine Gun | Fast, the reliable default |
| **R** — Rocket Launcher | Huge single-target damage |
| **S** — Shotgun | Devastating close, useless far |
| **F** — Flame Shot | Wide, burns through crowds |

**Ammunition is finite** and you revert to the pistol when it runs dry. The pistol is weak but unlimited.

**Grenades are the real weapon.** Bosses die to grenades far faster than to any gun, and most players hoard them to death.

---

## Prisoners

Bearded POWs are tied up throughout. Freeing them gives points, items and occasionally a weapon. **They can be killed by your own fire**, which costs the reward.

---

## Strategy

**Spend grenades on bosses.** That is what they are for.

**Stay in the Slug** unless you need to reach somewhere it cannot go.

**Crouch to dodge.** Most enemy fire passes over a crouching sprite.

**Free every prisoner** — the item drops carry you through the later stages.

**Learn the boss patterns.** This is an arcade game designed to eat coins; the bosses are memorisation tests.

## Emulation Notes

Runs on Batocera's **FinalBurn Neo** core, which needs `neogeo.zip` as a BIOS in the system directory. Both are installed and every game on this system has been launch-tested.

**Arcade games have no continues limit here.** The original ate quarters; emulated, you can continue indefinitely, which changes the difficulty from brutal to approachable. That is a legitimate way to see a game built to be unfair.

The Neo Geo home console, the AES, sold cartridges for around \$200 each in 1996. Emulation is how most people will ever play this library.
