# Shock Troopers (Neo Geo)

**Developer:** Saurus | **Publisher:** SNK | **Year:** 1997

---

## Introduction

A top-down run-and-gun in the lineage of *Ikari Warriors* and *Mercs*, and one of the Neo Geo's most underrated games. Where [Metal Slug](topics/games/neogeo/metal-slug) is a side-scroller, Shock Troopers is played from above, with rotational aiming and a much larger sense of space.

It is generous where most arcade games of the era were miserly: eight characters, branching routes, and a difficulty curve that is demanding rather than unfair.

---

## Controls

- **Joystick:** Move — **direction of travel is also aim**.
- **A:** Fire. **B:** Grenade / sub-weapon. **C:** Special attack.
- **A held:** Lock facing while moving, which is how you strafe.

**Learning to lock facing is the single biggest skill.** Without it you can only shoot where you walk; with it you circle an enemy while keeping fire on them.

---

## Two ways to play

At the start you choose:

**Solo** — one character, three lives. Harder, and the way the game is balanced for scoring.

**Team Battle** — three characters, and when one dies the next takes over with a fresh health bar. Effectively three lives with different abilities, and the more forgiving choice.

**Pick Team Battle first.** The eight characters play differently enough that the variety is worth it.

---

## The eight

Each has a distinct weapon, movement speed and special: a flamethrower specialist, a heavy gunner, a fast knife fighter, a grenadier. They matter more than in most games of the type.

---

## Branching routes

After certain stages you **choose your next destination** from a map. Routes differ in terrain, enemies and bosses, and no single run sees everything. This is the replay hook.

---

## Weapons

Picked up in the field — spread, laser, flame, homing — each with limited ammunition, reverting to a default rifle when exhausted. **Grenades are separate and plentiful.**

---

## Strategy

**Lock your facing and strafe.** This is the difference between competent and helpless.

**Use grenades constantly.** They restock and they clear crowds you cannot outshoot.

**Take different branches on replays.**

**In Team Battle, order your squad deliberately** — lead with a character you find awkward, so your best is last.
