# Garou: Mark of the Wolves (Neo Geo)

**Developer:** SNK | **Publisher:** SNK | **Year:** 1999

---

## Introduction

SNK's last great fighting game before the company went bankrupt, and the one its competitive scene still plays. *Garou* is technically *Fatal Fury 9*, set ten years after the series' story, with an almost entirely new cast — only Terry Bogard returns, older and in a different jacket.

It is widely considered one of the best-looking and best-playing 2D fighters ever made, and it never got a sequel because SNK collapsed.

---

## Controls

Four buttons:

- **A:** Light punch. **B:** Light kick. **C:** Heavy punch. **D:** Heavy kick.
- **Joystick:** Move; back to block, down-back to block low.

---

## The T.O.P. system

Before the match you choose a **position on your health bar** — the start, middle or end — to place your **Tactical Offense Position** gauge.

When your health falls into that band, three things happen at once: your attack power rises, your health slowly regenerates, and you gain access to a **T.O.P. Attack**, a unique unblockable-ish special.

**This is the game's signature idea.** Placing T.O.P. at the end of the bar gives you a comeback mechanic; placing it at the start makes you dangerous while healthy but leaves you with nothing when losing. It is a real strategic choice made before the fight begins.

---

## Just Defend

Block **at the exact moment** an attack lands and you Just Defend: you take no chip damage, recover a sliver of health, and recover instantly enough to counter.

**This is the mechanic that defines high-level play.** It rewards knowing your opponent's timing precisely, and it turns defence into offence. *Garou*'s Just Defend predates and arguably surpasses the parry systems it is usually compared with.

---

## Feints

Every character can cancel the start of a special move into nothing, baiting a reaction. Feint cancels chain into combos that are otherwise impossible.

---

## The roster

Twelve characters, almost all new. **Rock Howard**, Geese's son raised by Terry, is the protagonist. **Terry Bogard** returns as the veteran. The rest — Hokutomaru, Gato, B. Jenet, Kevin Rian, Tizoc — are original and unusually well designed.

---

## Strategy

**Choose T.O.P. position deliberately.** Defensive players want it at the end; aggressive players at the start.

**Practise Just Defend on predictable attacks first.** The window is tight but the payoff is large.

**Learn one character's feint cancels.** It is where the damage is.

**Block low by default.** Overheads are rarer than sweeps.

## Emulation Notes

Runs on Batocera's FinalBurn Neo core, launch-tested on this box.

Note that the romset here is `garou`, the standard release. Several near-identical sets exist — `garoubl` is a bootleg, `garoup` a prototype with different balance, `garouh` an alternate revision. They look the same in a file listing and are not the same game.
