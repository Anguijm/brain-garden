# The Last Blade 2 (Neo Geo)

**Developer:** SNK | **Publisher:** SNK | **Year:** 1998

---

## Introduction

SNK's other weapon fighter, and the most elegant-looking game on the Neo Geo. Where [Samurai Shodown II](topics/games/neogeo/samurai-shodown-2) is brutal and abrupt, *The Last Blade 2* is fluid and combo-driven, set at the end of Japan's Bakumatsu period with a melancholy tone the genre rarely attempts.

The art and animation are widely considered SNK's finest work.

---

## Controls

- **A:** Light slash. **B:** Heavy slash. **C:** Kick. **D:** Repel.
- **Joystick:** Move; back to block.

---

## Speed and Power modes

Chosen per character before the match, and they are genuinely different games:

**Speed** — attacks chain into combos freely, damage per hit is lower, and you gain a dash-cancel. Fast, aggressive, combo-heavy.

**Power** — no chain combos, but each hit does far more damage and you get a stronger super. Deliberate and punishing.

**Most beginners should pick Speed**, because it forgives imprecision. Power rewards players who land clean single hits.

---

## Repel

The D button **parries**. Time it against an incoming attack and you deflect it entirely, leaving the attacker wide open.

**Repel is the heart of high-level play.** It exists in both modes, it beats almost anything, and it turns the game into a reading contest. It also has a recovery penalty if mistimed, so it is not a free option.

---

## The roster

Fourteen characters, mostly swordsmen, each with a distinct weapon length and rhythm. **Kaede**, **Moriya** and **Hibiki** are the usual entry points; Hibiki in particular became the series' signature character.

---

## Strategy

**Pick Speed to learn, Power to compete.**

**Practise Repel against predictable moves** before relying on it.

**Watch weapon reach.** As in Samurai Shodown, spacing decides more rounds than execution.

**Do not mash in Power mode** — chains do not exist there and you will just be punished.
