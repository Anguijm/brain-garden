# Metal Slug 3 (Neo Geo)

**Developer:** SNK | **Publisher:** SNK | **Year:** 2000

---

## Introduction

The best game in the series and, by common consent, the peak of the run-and-gun genre. Everything from [Metal Slug](topics/games/neogeo/metal-slug) is here and expanded: more Slugs, more transformations, branching paths through every level, and a final mission that runs about half an hour on its own.

It is also **much harder** than the first game, and its later stages are famous for it.

---

## Controls

- **Joystick:** Move, aim, crouch.
- **A:** Fire. **B:** Jump. **C:** Grenade.
- **In a Slug:** A cannon, B jump, **down + down** to eject.

---

## Branching paths

**Every mission has multiple routes.** Take a different fork and you get different terrain, different enemies and sometimes a different vehicle. The branches rejoin at the boss, but the paths in between are genuinely distinct content.

**This is the main reason to replay it**, and most players never see half the game.

---

## Transformations

Getting hit by specific hazards changes your character, and each state has its own rules:

| State | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Zombie** | Slow, cannot jump well, but **vomits a screen-clearing blood attack** and takes reduced damage |
| **Mummy** | Very slow, cannot use most weapons, cured by medicine |
| **Fat** | Slower, larger hitbox, but weapons become enormously more powerful |

**Zombie form is not purely a penalty.** The blood attack does massive damage and you are immune to further zombie infection, so a zombie run through the right section is stronger than a healthy one.

---

## The Slugs

Far beyond the original tank: a camel, an elephant, a submarine, a mech, an ostrich, and the Slug Copter. Each handles differently and several are stage-specific.

---

## Mission 5

The final mission is a **thirty-minute endurance run** with no checkpoints in the arcade sense, escalating from a base assault into a space battle against an alien mothership. It is the single longest and hardest stage in the series and it is the reason the game has the reputation it does.

---

## Strategy

**Take different branches on replays.** It is effectively several games.

**Do not panic in zombie form.** Use the blood attack; it clears rooms.

**Save grenades for bosses**, as always.

**Mission 5 needs continues.** On original hardware this ate a fortune in coins; emulated, use them freely.

**Learn which Slug is coming.** Several sections are far easier if you reach the vehicle before engaging.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs on Batocera's FinalBurn Neo core with `neogeo.zip` in the system directory. Launch-tested on this box.

**Unlimited continues change this game more than any other on the system.** It was tuned so that Mission 5 emptied your pockets. Without that pressure it becomes a long, spectacular set piece rather than a wall.
