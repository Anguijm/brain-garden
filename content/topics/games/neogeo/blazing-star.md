# Blazing Star (Neo Geo)

**Developer:** Yumekobo | **Publisher:** SNK | **Year:** 1998

---

## Introduction

The best-looking shoot-'em-up on the Neo Geo, and the sequel to [Pulstar](topics/games/neogeo/pulstar) by the same studio. Where Pulstar was a hard, memorisation-driven *R-Type* homage, Blazing Star is faster, more colourful and much more generous.

It is also the source of one of gaming's most quoted mistranslations, delivered on the game-over screen: **"You fail it! Your skill is not enough! See you next time! Bye bye!"**

---

## Controls

- **Joystick:** Move.
- **A:** Fire — **tap** for rapid shots, **hold and release** for a charged attack.
- **B:** Bomb.
- **C:** Speed / secondary depending on ship.

---

## The charge shot, and the scoring system

Holding A charges a heavy attack. That much is standard. What is not standard is how scoring works:

**Destroying enemies with the charge shot produces gold multiplier items.** Chaining charged kills raises a multiplier that climbs into the thousands.

**The entire game is a scoring exercise.** Survival is comparatively easy by shooter standards; the depth is in routing each stage so charged shots land on the right enemy groups in sequence.

---

## Six ships

Each has a different shot pattern, charge behaviour, speed and bomb. They are not cosmetic:

- **Windina** — homing lightning, forgiving, the beginner pick
- **Peplos** — spread shot, wide coverage
- **Dino246** — heavy, slow, huge damage
- **Hellhound** — fast, narrow, high skill ceiling
- **Bloody Dragon** and **Empire-57** — specialised patterns

---

## Difficulty

**Blazing Star is approachable for the genre.** Hitboxes are fair, the ships are reasonably durable, and continues are unlimited on emulation. It is a good first bullet-hell-adjacent shooter for that reason.

The pre-rendered CGI sprites were dated within a few years and have come back around to looking deliberate.

---

## Strategy

**Charge constantly.** Tap-firing is for emergencies; the score lives in charged kills.

**Learn one ship.** The routes differ per ship because the shot patterns do.

**Do not bomb defensively at first** — bombs cost score, and survival is rarely the problem.

**Chain multipliers across enemy waves**, not individual enemies.
