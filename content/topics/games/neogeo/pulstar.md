# Pulstar (Neo Geo)

**Developer:** Aicom | **Publisher:** SNK | **Year:** 1995

---

## Introduction

An unashamed *R-Type* homage, made by ex-Irem staff, with pre-rendered CGI graphics that were startling on 1995 arcade hardware. It is the predecessor to [Blazing Star](topics/games/neogeo/blazing-star) and it is considerably harder.

**This is a memorisation game.** Enemy waves arrive in fixed patterns designed so that a first-time player cannot survive them. That is the genre convention it inherits from R-Type, and whether it is a virtue depends entirely on your appetite for learning a route.

---

## Controls

- **Joystick:** Move.
- **A:** Fire — **hold to charge**, release for a heavy beam.
- **B:** Deploy / recall the **Pulse Barrier**.

---

## The Pulse Barrier

A detachable pod that floats near your ship. It **absorbs enemy fire**, deals contact damage, and can be positioned in front of or behind you.

This is lifted directly from R-Type's Force pod, and it works the same way: it is a shield you aim. Learning to reposition it to cover the direction fire is coming from is the core skill.

---

## Charging

Holding fire builds a beam through several tiers. A fully charged shot pierces multiple enemies.

**The tension is that charging means not shooting.** Deciding when to hold and when to tap is most of the moment-to-moment play.

---

## Difficulty, stated honestly

Pulstar is hard in the specific way arcade shooters of its era were hard: **enemies appear where you cannot react to them, only where you can remember them.** Deaths in the first several attempts are not skill failures, they are information.

The eight stages are short. The game is beatable in about half an hour by someone who knows it.

---

## Strategy

**Learn stage by stage.** Do not attempt full runs early.

**Keep the Barrier between you and the threat**, and detach it to clear a lane ahead.

**Charge during quiet stretches**, tap during dense ones.

**Memorise the boss openings.** Most bosses kill you in the first four seconds until you know the pattern.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs on Batocera's FinalBurn Neo core, launch-tested here.

**Unlimited continues transform this game.** In the arcade it was a coin-eater; here you can learn a stage by dying in it repeatedly at no cost, which is the only realistic way to get through it. Save states make it easier still, if you want them.
