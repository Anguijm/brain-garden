# Dragon Warrior Monsters (Game Boy Color)

**Developer:** TOSE / Enix | **Publisher:** Enix | **Year:** 2000 (US)

---

## Introduction

Enix's answer to Pokémon, and a more interesting game than that framing suggests. Known in Japan as *Dragon Quest Monsters: Terry's Wonderland*, it stars Terry — a character from *Dragon Quest VI* — recruiting monsters in a world reached through a chest of drawers.

**The difference from Pokémon is breeding, and it is not a side system.** In Pokémon, breeding produces the same species with better moves. Here, **breeding two different monsters produces a third, distinct species**, often one you cannot recruit any other way. The strongest monsters in the game exist only as breeding results.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move. **A:** Confirm/talk. **B:** Cancel.
- **Start:** Menu.

---

## Recruiting

Monsters are not caught with an item. You **feed them meat** during battle, and they may decide to join. Recruitment odds depend on the monster's disposition, your party's strength, and how battered it is.

**You control your party only indirectly.** Each monster has a personality and an AI tactic you set — fight wisely, focus offence, conserve magic — rather than choosing individual moves. This is a genuine Dragon Quest inheritance and it takes adjustment.

---

## Breeding, in practice

Take two monsters, retire them at the shrine, and receive one offspring.

- The child inherits **skills from both parents** and starts at level 1 but with better growth ceilings.
- The species produced depends on the **families** the parents belong to (slime, beast, dragon, bird, plant, material, undead, ???).
- **Bred monsters keep inherited skills permanently**, so lineage compounds: each generation is stronger than the last.

**This is the actual game.** Someone playing it as Pokémon, recruiting a team and levelling them, will hit a wall in the middle tournaments. Someone breeding deliberately will not.

---

## Structure

The world is a hub with **gates** into randomly generated dungeons. Each gate has a theme, a set of monsters, and a boss at the bottom. Progress is measured through **tournaments** — beat the current tier, unlock the next set of gates.

Because dungeons are generated, grinding is unlimited and the game never runs out of places to look for a specific monster.

---

## Strategy

**Start breeding early.** Waiting until you are stuck means restarting a lineage from scratch.

**Keep a slime line going.** The slime family breeds into some of the strongest late monsters.

**Set tactics deliberately.** "Fight wisely" is the default and it is frequently wrong for a specific fight.

**Save before tournaments.** They are the difficulty spikes.

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Game Boy Color core with **rewind enabled**.
