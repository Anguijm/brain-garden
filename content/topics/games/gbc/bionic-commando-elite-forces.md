# Bionic Commando: Elite Forces (Game Boy Color)

**Developer:** Nintendo Software Technology | **Publisher:** Nintendo | **Year:** 2000

---

## Introduction

A Bionic Commando game made by **Nintendo**, not Capcom — Nintendo Software Technology in Redmond built it under licence, and it is one of the better entries in the series.

The premise is unchanged and still the most distinctive thing about it: **you cannot jump.** Your character has a bionic grappling arm, and every gap, ledge and vertical move is crossed by swinging.

If you have played the [NES Bionic Commando](topics/games/nes/bionic-commando), this is a direct continuation of that design with better level construction.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move; up/diagonals aim the arm.
- **A:** **Fire the bionic arm** in the aimed direction.
- **B:** Weapon.
- **Arm attached:** Hold and use the D-pad to swing; release at the top of an arc to launch.

### Not jumping

The single mechanic that defines the game and the reason people bounce off it. You cross a gap by firing the arm at a ceiling or ledge, swinging, and releasing at the right moment. Momentum carries.

**It takes about twenty minutes to stop reaching for a jump button and roughly an hour to become fluent.** Past that point the movement is genuinely satisfying in a way jumping is not.

---

## Structure

Mission-based. You select an area, complete an objective, and return. Between missions there are briefings and equipment choices.

There are **two playable characters** with slightly different capabilities, and the game has multiple endings depending on choices and completion.

---

## Weapons and upgrades

Collected through missions: spread weapons, homing rounds, armour upgrades. Some doors and routes need a specific tool, so backtracking with new equipment is expected.

---

## Strategy

**Aim the arm diagonally, not straight up.** Diagonal shots convert into swings; vertical ones just hang you in place.

**Release at the apex.** Letting go too early drops you short; too late kills your momentum.

**Use the arm as a weapon.** It knocks enemies back and it is free.

**Explore mission areas fully before finishing the objective.** Some upgrades are only reachable during a specific mission.
