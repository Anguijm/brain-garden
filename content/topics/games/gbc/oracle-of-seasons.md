# The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons (Game Boy Color)

**Developer:** Flagship (Capcom) | **Publisher:** Nintendo | **Year:** 2001

---

## Introduction

The action half of Capcom's two-game Zelda project. Where [Oracle of Ages](topics/games/gbc/oracle-of-ages) is built on puzzles, **Seasons** is built on combat and traversal — more enemies, more direct dungeons, a gentler difficulty curve.

Set in Holodrum. General Onox has buried Din, the Oracle of Seasons, and thrown the land's seasons into chaos. The **Rod of Seasons** lets you change the season wherever you stand.

**Play this one first.** It is the more forgiving introduction, and the password chain works in either direction.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move.
- **A / B:** Two equipped items, assigned freely.
- **Start:** Inventory. **Select:** Map.

Two item slots, constant swapping — the same friction as Ages.

---

## The Rod of Seasons

Stand on a **tree stump**, swing the rod, and the season changes across the region.

Each season rewrites the terrain in a specific way:

| Season | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Spring** | Flowers bloom into bouncy platforms; some vines grow |
| **Summer** | Vines dry and become climbable; water lowers |
| **Autumn** | Mushrooms grow into platforms; leaves pile into bridges |
| **Winter** | Water freezes into walkable ice; snow drifts raise you up |

**You can only change seasons at a stump.** The puzzle is usually locating the stump, not choosing the season — the correct season is generally obvious once you can reach a stump at all.

---

## Animal companions

Same three as Ages — Ricky the kangaroo, Dimitri the Dodongo, Moosh the winged bear — assigned by which secret you find first.

---

## Linked games

Finishing Seasons gives you a password for Ages. Entering it makes Ages a **linked game**: rings carry over, the story acknowledges Holodrum, and the ending runs through to **Ganon** rather than stopping at Veran.

Both games standalone end early. The full story only exists across a linked pair.

---

## Rings

Passive upgrades, dozens of them, found and traded. The **Power Ring** and **Armor Ring** families trade offence against defence; the joke rings do things like turn Link into an octorok. The collection transfers to a linked game, which is a large part of why linking is worth doing.

---

## Strategy

**Learn the stumps.** In every region, find the season stump before exploring. It converts the map from one layout into four.

**Seasons rewards aggression** in a way Ages does not. The sword upgrades matter here.

**Do not hoard bombs and seeds.** They restock cheaply and both games gate progress behind spending them.
