# Game Boy Color Game Guides

The Game Boy Color ran for barely four years before the Advance replaced it, and it spent most of
that time playing backwards-compatible Game Boy games. What it got in its own right, it got late
and it got good.

## Two games that are not what the box says

**Metal Gear Solid** shares a title with the PlayStation game and nothing else. It is an original
story called *Ghost Babel* in Japan, built top-down for the hardware, and it is one of the best
games on the system. It has never been re-released.

**Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets** on Game Boy Color is a turn-based RPG. The Game Boy
Advance game of the same name, released the same year, is an isometric action game. They share a
licence and a title and nothing else.

## The pair that has to be played as a pair

**Oracle of Ages** and **Oracle of Seasons** were built together and released together. Finish
either and you get a password; enter it in the other and that game becomes a *linked game* —
your rings carry over, and the ending runs through to Ganon instead of stopping at the local
villain. **Played separately, both games end early.** Seasons is the easier one and the better
starting point.

A third game was planned. Linking three ways proved impossible with passwords and it was cut.

## The one that arrived too late

**Shantae** shipped in June 2002, months after the Game Boy Advance had taken the market. Capcom
printed very few. Original cartridges now sell for hundreds of dollars, and the game itself is
a technical showpiece — large jointed sprites and parallax scrolling on hardware that was
finished. It started a series that is still going.

## Still no complete Pokédex

Both **Gold** and **Crystal** gate eight Pokémon behind trading — Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler,
Haunter, Onix, Scyther, Poliwhirl, Seadra. Without link emulation, the Pokédex cannot be
finished on a single copy. **Dragon Warrior Monsters** had trading too, but locks nothing behind
it: every monster there is obtainable by breeding.
