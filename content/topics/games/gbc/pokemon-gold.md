# Pokémon Gold (Game Boy Color)

**Developer:** Game Freak | **Publisher:** Nintendo | **Year:** 2000 (US)

---

## Introduction

The original second-generation release, and the one that had to follow the biggest phenomenon in handheld gaming. It did so by being roughly twice the size of what came before.

Gold and Silver are the same game with different version-exclusive Pokémon; **Gold's mascot legendary is Ho-Oh**, Silver's is Lugia. [Crystal](topics/games/gbc/pokemon-crystal) came a year later and is the enhanced edition.

**If you are playing one of these for the first time, play Crystal.** Gold is here for completeness and for its own version exclusives.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move. **A:** Confirm. **B:** Cancel / run (hold).
- **Start:** Menu. **Select:** Register an item.

---

## What Generation 2 added

- **Two new types**, Dark and Steel, both introduced to break Psychic's Generation 1 dominance.
- **A real-time clock**: morning, day and night change which Pokémon appear.
- **Held items** in battle.
- **Breeding** at the Day Care.
- **Shiny Pokémon**, at roughly 1 in 8,192.
- **Two regions.** Johto, then the whole of Kanto after the credits, sixteen badges, ending against **Red** at level 81 on Mt. Silver.

---

## Gold's version exclusives

Gold gets Growlithe, Mankey, Spinarak, Gligar, Teddiursa and Mantine among others; Silver gets Vulpix, Meowth, Ledyba, Delibird, Phanpy and Skarmory. Neither is stronger. **Ho-Oh** is catchable in Gold at the Tin Tower; Lugia requires Silver.

---

## Route

Falkner, Bugsy, Whitney, Morty, Chuck, Jasmine, Pryce, Clair, then the Elite Four and Lance. Then Kanto.

**Whitney's Miltank is the wall.** Rollout gains power each consecutive hit and Milk Drink undoes your progress. A Fighting type, or anything that survives long enough to break the chain, solves it.

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Game Boy Color core with **rewind enabled**.

The clock works off host time, and unlike the original cartridges there is no battery to die.

**Trade evolutions are unreachable on a single copy.** Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, Haunter, Onix, Scyther, Poliwhirl and Seadra all need a trade, so a complete Pokédex is not achievable without link emulation.
