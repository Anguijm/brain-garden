# Contra — NES Walkthrough

Contra (1988) is one of the best run-and-gun games ever made and one of the hardest games on the NES. You are either Bill Rizer or Lance Bean, commandos sent to neutralize a terrorist organization called Red Falcon on Galuga Island. What begins as a jungle assault ends inside an alien structure. The game is a single hit from death at all times, and there are no continues by default — which is why the Konami Code is the most famous cheat in gaming history.

This guide covers every stage, every weapon, every boss, and every trick you need to clear the game. Co-op notes are included throughout.

---

## The Konami Code — Start Here

Before you do anything else: on the title screen, enter this sequence:

**Up, Up, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right, B, A, Start**

For two players: **Up, Up, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right, B, A, Select, Start**

This gives you 30 lives instead of 3. The code is not a crutch — it is how the game was designed to be played. The original arcade version had unlimited continues. The NES port cut that but gave you the Code. You will die many times regardless. Use it.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| D-Pad | Move left/right, climb up/down (platforms), aim while standing |
| A | Jump |
| B | Fire |
| Up | Aim up (while standing); climb up ledge or ladder |
| Down | Prone / slide under bullets |
| D-Pad + B | Fire in that direction; diagonal shots by combining up/down with left/right |
| Down while jumping | Slide / drop through a platform |

**Firing directions:** Contra has one of the most generous aiming systems of its era. While standing you can aim in eight directions by holding a direction as you fire. While running you fire forward. While prone you fire horizontally. This flexibility is the key to managing fast-moving enemies and turret angles.

**Climbing:** When you jump to a platform edge and hold up, the character grabs and climbs over. This is automatic but worth knowing since mistiming it under fire is a common death.

## Weapons Guide

Weapon capsules appear in gun pods (Red Falcon containers) and in item boxes. Shoot the lid off a pod to reveal the capsule, then walk into it. You can only hold one weapon at a time. Dying resets you to the default rifle.

### Default Rifle
Your starting weapon. Single shot, unlimited ammo, fires at walking speed. Serviceable but outclassed by everything else. If you have it in the later stages something has gone wrong.

### M — Machine Gun
Auto-fire in the held direction. Hold B and it fires continuously at a rapid rate, tracking your aim. A large upgrade from the rifle, workable all the way through the game. Good first grab.

### S — Spread Gun
Five bullets fanning out simultaneously. The best weapon in the game. It fires forward and covers a cone wide enough to hit anything on screen within two to three tiles. In the Base stages (top-down view) Spread Gun clears rooms effortlessly. Against bosses it delivers five times the damage of a normal bullet per trigger pull. Protect this weapon at all costs. When you die and lose it, your next priority is reclaiming it.

### L — Laser
A slow-moving beam that passes through enemies. Extremely powerful per hit — one beam kills most normal enemies. The trap is that it is slow to traverse the screen, and firing a second shot before the first beam expires cancels it. Do not mash B. Fire once, wait for the beam to disappear or hit something, then fire again. Against moving enemies at close range the Laser underperforms. Against the boss structures it is highly effective because the beam lingers in the hitbox.

### F — Fireball
Bounces along the ground in a sinusoidal arc. Hits below platform level, which sounds useful but the awkward arc makes it genuinely hard to aim, and it cannot hit airborne targets. The weakest powered weapon. Grab it if nothing better is available, replace it at the first opportunity.

### R — Rapid Fire (Rotary Gun)
Despite the name, this is a rate-of-fire booster rather than a separate gun. It increases your bullet speed and fire rate. Combine it with the Machine Gun and the effect is dramatic. On its own, it can feel underwhelming because the bullet is still the default rifle shot. Best used when you cannot find Spread or Laser and need to clear enemies faster.

### B — Barrier
Not a weapon — a one-hit shield. The Barrier absorbs one bullet or one contact hit that would otherwise kill you, then disappears. In a single-hit game this is your extra life. Grab it whenever it appears, especially before a boss.

**Weapon priority:** Spread > Laser > Machine Gun with Rapid Fire > Rapid Fire > Machine Gun > Fireball > Rifle.

## Stage-by-Stage Walkthrough

Contra has eight stages. The first four alternate between side-scrolling and top-down "Base" segments. Stages five through eight are all side-scrolling.

---

### Stage 1 — Jungle

The tutorial stage. Enemies pour in from left and right. The jungle's ground level, cliff edges, and bridges establish the basic rhythm: shoot incoming soldiers, watch for grenade throws, jump gaps.

**Key threats:**
- Basic soldiers — approach from both directions, sometimes diving for cover. Stay mobile.
- Grenade throwers — their arcing throw can catch you if you stand still. Move constantly.
- Turrets embedded in walls — shoot them out from a safe angle before walking past.

**Turret pods:** Orange gun pods hang from the top of the screen at regular intervals. Shoot the center spinning light to destroy the pod. Hits release a weapon capsule. First M capsule appears early. An S capsule appears in the second half — take it.

**Waterfall shortcut:** Near the end, platforms lead up to an elevated path above the main river. Take the high road when possible to avoid the ground-level enemy density.

**Boss: Gunner Base**
The boss is a wall of four gun turrets flanking a central eye. Each turret fires in a fixed angle. The eye opens periodically and is the only vulnerable point.

- Fire a full burst the instant the eye opens.
- If you have Spread Gun, position yourself at the horizontal center and fire upward into the open eye — all five shots hit simultaneously.
- If you have the Laser, time one beam into the open eye each cycle.
- Cycle through the turrets as you wait for openings — the turrets can be destroyed to reduce incoming fire.

---

### Stage 2 — Base 1 (Top-Down)

The perspective shifts to top-down. You move through a rectangular compound and must reach and destroy four core generators before the exit opens. The structure of every Base stage is the same: navigate corridors, eliminate guards, find the cores, destroy them all, exit.

**Movement:** In top-down view you walk in any of eight directions. Bullets also travel in eight directions. You cannot jump.

**Enemy behavior:** Guards patrol corridors. They flinch when shot and resume after a moment. The real threats are the Grebas (gray guards) and the Beemedals (spherical robots with spinning shields).

**Beemedals (spinning shield enemies):** Wait for the gap in the rotation and fire through it. Do not chase them around corners.

**Core generators:** Each is a small orange cluster embedded in the wall at the end of a side corridor. Shoot the blinking center repeatedly until it explodes. Destroying all four opens the final door to the reactor.

**Reactor boss:** Fire into the reactor core, dodging the spiral-pattern sparks. Spread Gun makes short work of it by covering multiple core segments simultaneously.

---

### Stage 3 — Waterfall

A vertical scrolling stage that climbs a waterfall face. You start at the bottom and ascend on ledges and ropes while the screen scrolls upward. If you fall off the bottom edge you die.

**Key threats:**
- Boulder soldiers — enemies that appear from behind rocks and toss grenades down at you.
- Flying drones — appear in the upper sections, move in a figure-eight pattern.
- Snipers on ledge edges — shoot them before you jump up to their level.

**Ropes:** Grab ropes to swing and jump between platforms. You can fire in any direction while hanging. This is the stage where diagonal firing earns its keep.

**Forced scroll trap:** The screen always scrolls upward at a fixed rate. You cannot pause or wait — keep climbing or the bottom edge kills you. In two-player, if one player falls behind they die and rejoin at the top where the other player is.

**Boss: Waterfall Core**
A large mechanical eye on the cliff face, flanked by four cannons. The cannons arc heavy artillery shots that bounce along the platform you stand on.

- Destroy the four cannons first to reduce fire density.
- The central eye opens in a rhythm: study two cycles before committing.
- Spread Gun positioned below and centered clears all four cannons quickly and hits the eye on the same bursts.
- With Laser: aim for the eye, not the cannons — the beam's penetration makes the cannons secondary.

---

### Stage 4 — Base 2 (Top-Down)

Identical structure to Base 1 but larger, with more corridors and more Beemedals. The cores are guarded more heavily. Take your time clearing corridors before advancing to each core.

**Flame thrower guards:** New enemy type. They carry a close-range flame weapon. Shoot them at maximum distance — close contact is a reliable death.

**Core pattern:** The four cores are arranged in the outer wings of the compound. Clear a path to the first two, then backtrack through the central corridor to reach the second pair.

---

### Stage 5 — Snow Field

Back to side-scrolling. A longer stage than the first three side-scrollers. The ground is snow-covered and enemies spawn from distant points, giving you a moment to read the spawns and aim ahead.

**Snowfield hazards:**
- Soldiers skiing from the background — they start small (harmless) and grow to full size as they approach. Shoot them before they reach you.
- Bushwacker enemies that burst from snowbanks when you pass — be suspicious of any snow pile.

**Mid-stage checkpoint:** There is a small fortified structure mid-stage with a heavy gun emplacement on top. Clear the gun before advancing past it.

**Pill boxes:** Small circular gun emplacements embedded in the ground. Shoot the top lid, then shoot inside. Do not stand in the arc of fire while attacking.

**Boss: Stone Soldier (Guruguru)**
A large head rolling at you with a spinning body. The stone face is invulnerable. The generator core inside the open mouth is the target.

- Position yourself at the left edge of the screen.
- The Guruguru rolls right to left repeatedly.
- When the mouth is facing toward you, fire straight ahead.
- Spread Gun hits the mouth from a distance without requiring precise timing.
- If the head rolls off screen, anticipate it coming back from the left edge.

---

### Stage 6 — Energy Zone

A brief but demanding stage. Floating platforms over an energy field that kills you if you fall in. Platforms appear and disappear on a timer.

**Platform timing:** Count the platforms. Each has a visible blink-before-disappear animation that gives you about half a second of warning. Do not stand on any platform longer than necessary.

**Enemy spawns:** Soldiers spawn from the sides and from above. In this stage they are secondary — the platforms kill more players than the enemies.

**Boss: Wall Carrier**
A moving wall of guns that sweeps the screen. Gun nozzles extend from the wall and fire in sequence.

- Jump over the ground-level guns.
- Duck under the elevated guns.
- Fire continuously into each gun extension to destroy it.
- The wall is eliminated segment by segment.

---

### Stage 7 — Hangar

A long industrial side-scroller with a vertical component at the end. Heavy armored enemies in this stage absorb more hits than any normal soldier. Save your Spread Gun if you have it.

**Key threats:**
- Heavy armor soldiers — take three hits from the rifle. Spread Gun drops them in one burst.
- Ceiling turrets — hang from above and fire downward. Shoot at the ceiling-mounted base to destroy the whole unit.
- Moving cranes — wide horizontal platforms on overhead tracks. Use them for high-ground angles but be ready to jump off before they run into the wall.

**Vertical section:** Near the end, the stage shifts to a vertical shaft. Climb platforms upward while enemies rain down from above.

**Boss: Hangar Controller**
A machine boss with four gun pods surrounding a central core. The core blinks to signal when it is vulnerable.

- Destroy the four outer pods to clear the field.
- Lay Spread Gun shots into the core when it blinks.
- The pods fire in a rotating sequence — after each fires, it has a brief cooldown where you can advance.

---

### Stage 8 — Alien's Lair

The final stage. You are inside the alien structure. The background is organic — flesh and bone instead of steel. The enemies are alien: Gronthis (crawlers), small alien heads, and the stage obstacles are living tissue.

**Structure:** The stage consists of two chambers before the final boss. Both chambers scroll as you advance.

**Alien crawlers:** Move in a fast, unpredictable pattern. They advance and retreat quickly. Spread Gun handles them cleanly. With any other weapon, aim ahead of their trajectory.

**Cocoons on the walls:** Do not let them hatch. Each contains an alien that fires energy blasts when emerged. Shoot the cocoon before it opens. If it opens, kill the alien before advancing.

**Pre-boss chambers:** Two sets of fleshy walls with organic gun nodes. Destroy every node on the wall. The nodes regenerate if you take too long — clear them methodically left to right.

### Final Boss: The Alien Heart

The last enemy is a giant organic heart surrounded by two Giger-style alien faces flanking it, mounted on a fleshy wall. This is the only encounter in the game that genuinely requires Spread Gun to complete efficiently.

**Layout:**
- Two guardian mouths (Giger heads) on the left and right.
- The heart is the large central pulsing red mass.
- Two additional smaller alien eyes flank the heart and fire energy bolts.

**Phase 1 — Guardians:**
Destroy both flanking mouths first. Each mouth opens and closes rhythmically. Fire into the mouth when it is open. Spread Gun hits the interior reliably from mid-screen. Laser requires more precise timing but works. The mouths fire horizontal projectiles and occasionally lunge forward.

**Phase 2 — Heart:**
Once both mouths are down, fire into the central heart directly. The heart is always exposed. The alien eyes continue firing — dodge the energy bolts by reading their angle just before they launch.

With Spread Gun: stand at the left edge, aim right, fire. The five-way spread covers the heart and both eyes simultaneously. The boss dies in seconds.

With Laser: stand center-screen, aim right at the heart. Fire once per beam cycle. Dodge the eye bolts while waiting for beam cooldown.

Without powered weapons: possible, but the pattern requires significant patience and the rifle's fire rate makes it a war of attrition. Use the Barrier before this fight if you have one.

**When the heart is destroyed:** The entire structure collapses. A countdown timer starts. The ending fires automatically.

## Two-Player Co-op Notes

Contra co-op is the best feature of the game, and also the source of many arguments. Bill and Lance are identical in all stats and controls.

**The revival mechanic:** There is none. If a player loses all their lives, the remaining player continues alone. Player 2 can continue from the stock when Player 2 gets a game over if Player 1 is still alive, provided Player 2 has more lives stored. In practice: if one player dies on a boss with no lives left, the other player has to finish the fight alone.

**Weapon drops:** Both players share the same weapon capsule. If Player 1 grabs an S capsule, Player 2 does not get one. Communicate. The ideal co-op setup is both players on Spread Gun, with each player clearing different sides of the screen.

**Stage 2 and 4 (Top-Down) co-op:** Both players are on screen simultaneously. Coordinate corridors — you can accidentally walk into each other's line of fire. Enemy count does not increase in co-op, but two players clearing corridors simultaneously makes the Base stages fast.

**Boss co-op:** Two Spread Guns on a boss ends boss fights extremely quickly. The Alien Heart with two Spread Guns is over in about four seconds.

**The friendly fire note:** There is no friendly fire. You cannot shoot your partner. You can, however, body-check each other into enemies or off platforms in the top-down stages. This is less of a gameplay concern and more of a co-op relationship stress test.

## Tips and Secrets

**The Konami Code (again):** 30 lives. Use it.

**Learn to go prone:** Holding Down makes your character drop flat. Many bullets pass completely over a prone character. This is especially valuable against turrets firing at chest height and against the Stage 5 skiing soldiers. In the top-down stages, going prone is not available.

**Fire while jumping:** You can fire in any direction during a jump. This includes firing backward while jumping forward, which is how you clear enemies behind you without slowing down.

**Do not stop moving:** Every moment you stand still is a moment a soldier or turret can target your fixed position. Move constantly, even small lateral shuffles. The AI aims at where you were, not where you are going.

**Weapon capsule resets on death:** When you die, your weapon resets to the default rifle. The weapon capsule you had does not respawn. Your first priority after a death is finding the next capsule. In well-known trouble spots (the Waterfall boss, the Stage 7 vertical section), knowing where the next capsule appears is critical.

**The Stage 8 Spread Gun guarantee:** Before the final boss, there is one final gun pod in the flesh corridor containing a Spread Gun capsule. Even if you have arrived at Stage 8 with nothing, this capsule makes the final boss survivable. Memorize its location and protect the capsule walk.

**Count your lives before the final boss:** If you are on your last life entering the Alien Heart fight and do not have Spread Gun, do not charge in. Wait, get hit, cycle to the next life (from continues), try again with better positioning.

**Barrier before bosses:** If a Barrier capsule appears anywhere near a boss transition, grab it. The extra hit point is worth more in boss encounters than against any stage enemy.

**Screen scrolling in Stage 3:** In the Waterfall stage, the screen scrolls whether you are ready or not. Practice the platform sequence in the lower third during early attempts. That section catches most first-time players because the scroll rate feels faster than it is.

**Boss patterns are fixed:** Every boss in Contra fires and moves in a completely predictable pattern. There is no randomization. Once you learn a pattern, it does not deviate. The game is hard because you learn by dying, not because the game cheats.

**Energy tank trick (two-player):** In two-player mode, if Player 1 is near death, Player 2 can be sacrificed to checkpoint-warp both players forward, since Player 2 rejoins at the other player's position. This is a deliberate risk tradeoff, not a glitch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I have to use the Konami Code?**
No, but without it you have 3 lives and no continues. The game is extremely difficult. With the Code you have 30 lives. Most players, even experienced ones, use the Code.

**Is there a continue screen?**
Yes. When you Game Over, the game gives you a short window to press Start and continue from the current stage with 3 lives. You can continue from any stage you reached. The Code does not need to be re-entered after a continue.

**Can I save progress?**
No. Contra has no save or password system. You play the full game in one session (about 20-30 minutes once you know the patterns). It is designed for arcade-style one-sitting play.

**What happens after you beat the game?**
The game loops at higher difficulty. Enemy bullets are faster, some enemy patterns change slightly. The structure of every stage and boss is the same, but the increased projectile speed makes even the early stages punishing. Most players consider the first clear a complete run.

**Why does the Laser keep canceling?**
Because you fired again before the previous beam cleared the screen. The Laser beam persists until it hits something or exits the screen. A second trigger pull during that window cancels the first beam. Slow down your trigger on the Laser.

**The boss won't die. Am I hitting it?**
Boss vulnerable points flash when hit. If you are not seeing flash, your shots are not connecting. Reposition. Every boss in the game has a specific target zone — it is always the eye, the core, the open mouth, or the blinking center. Hitting the surrounding structure deals no damage.

**What is the best weapon?**
Spread Gun, by a significant margin. It fires five shots simultaneously in a fan pattern, covers both horizontal and vertical threats, and deals full damage per shot — five times the rifle's output on a single trigger pull. Every experienced Contra player guards the S capsule aggressively.
