# Castlevania — NES Walkthrough

**Platform:** NES | **Year:** 1987 (US) | **Developer:** Konami | **Genre:** Action-Platformer

---

## Introduction

Castlevania puts you in the boots of Simon Belmont, a vampire hunter from a long line of monster killers, on a mission to ascend Dracula's castle and destroy the Count himself. The game runs across six blocks, each split into three stages, for eighteen stages in total. The castle is linear — you move right and up, fight what's in the way, and beat the boss at the end of each block.

What makes Castlevania hard is not complexity but precision. Simon's whip takes a full second to swing, his jumps are fixed arcs that can't be adjusted in the air, and one wrong step near stairs can launch him off a ledge and into a pit. The game punishes impatience and rewards reading every room before you walk into it.

This guide covers every block, every boss, every sub-weapon, and every piece of hidden meat in the walls. Save states are your friend here — use them, especially before boss rooms and the Medusa Head sections.

---

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| D-Pad Left/Right | Move |
| D-Pad Up | Climb stairs upward |
| D-Pad Down | Descend stairs / duck |
| A | Jump |
| B | Attack (Whip) |
| Up + B | Throw sub-weapon (costs hearts) |

**Critical things to know:**
- Once you jump, your horizontal arc is locked. You cannot steer. Plan where you will land before you leave the ground.
- Simon moves slowly up and down stairs. Enemies do not slow down. Avoid fighting on stairs when possible.
- Getting hit while on stairs knocks Simon sideways — usually off the screen, usually into a pit. This is not a glitch, it is the game's primary execution tax on carelessness.
- The whip's hitbox extends directly in front of Simon at mid-torso height. Against enemies that fly high (birds, bats) or stay low (slimes), you need to be level with them to connect.

## Whip Upgrades

Simon starts with the **Leather Whip**, which is short and weak. Two upgrades exist and both should be found as early as possible.

**Leather Whip → Chain Whip**
The chain whip has longer reach and deals more damage. You'll find the upgrade in a whip icon hidden in candles — usually in the second or third stage of Block 1.

**Chain Whip → Vampire Killer (Morning Star)**
The Vampire Killer is the fully upgraded whip: maximum reach, maximum damage, and it damages most enemies in one or two hits. Another whip icon in candles upgrades from Chain to Vampire Killer. Find it in Block 2. Keep it for the rest of the game.

**Damage note:** Getting hit does not downgrade your whip. Only dying resets your whip to Leather. This means whip maintenance is a per-life concern, not a per-hit one.

## Sub-Weapons

Sub-weapons are found in candles scattered through each stage. You can only hold one at a time — picking up a new one replaces whatever you had. They cost hearts to use (Up + B), and hearts refill from candles and killed enemies.

### The Dagger
Thrown straight forward at high speed, travels the full width of the screen. Cheap to use (1 heart), good for enemies at a distance, nearly useless against anything that isn't on the same horizontal plane. Solid as a default but not the best option for bosses.

### The Axe
Thrown in a high arc, hitting targets above Simon's head. Costs 2 hearts. The arc makes it excellent against the Giant Bat (Block 1 boss) and the Mummies (Block 3 boss). Useless against anything at floor level — the arc means ground enemies are rarely hit before the axe climbs past them.

### Holy Water
Throws a flask that shatters on impact and leaves a patch of holy flame on the ground. That flame lingers and damages anything that walks through it. Costs 1 heart per throw. Strong against ground-bound enemies and effective on Death's scythes (Block 5). The flame patch makes it one of the best weapons for area denial.

### The Cross (Boomerang)
The most important sub-weapon in the game. The cross travels in a straight horizontal line, reaches the edge of the screen, then arcs and returns to Simon, hitting everything in its path twice — once going out and once coming back. Costs 5 hearts per throw. It is expensive but devastates every boss from Block 3 onward. If you find the cross, take it and protect your heart supply.

### The Stopwatch
Freezes all enemies on screen for approximately 3 seconds. Costs 5 hearts. It sounds more powerful than it is, because enemies revive after the freeze and Simon still has to kill them. Most useful in Medusa Head corridors, where the freeze gives you a few seconds to advance without being knocked off a ledge.

### The Rosary
Looks like a cross necklace. Instantly destroys every enemy on screen. Extremely rare — only appears in specific candles. Use it without guilt.

### Double and Triple Shot
These are not separate weapons but powerups that multiply your current sub-weapon. A **Double Fire** icon (II in a block) lets you throw two sub-weapons simultaneously. **Triple Fire** (III) gives you three. These dramatically increase sub-weapon damage. The cross with Triple Fire can kill most bosses in seconds.

**Priority order:** Cross > Holy Water > Axe > Dagger > Stopwatch

## Stage-by-Stage Walkthrough

### Block 1 — The Castle Entrance
**Stages 1-1, 1-2, 1-3 | Boss: Giant Bat**

**1-1:** You start outside the castle. Walk right. Break every torch and candle you see — they contain hearts, sub-weapons, and occasionally whip upgrades. The first enemies are zombies: slow, walk toward you, easy to whip. Bats drop from the sky in clusters; jump over them or wait for them to swoop low and whip. There is a hidden piece of meat in a wall near the middle of the stage — whip suspicious-looking walls if your health is low. Grab the Dagger from a candle; it will be your sub-weapon until you find the Axe.

**1-2:** The interior of the castle wall. Stairs begin here. Go slowly on the staircases — if you see an enemy near a staircase, kill it before mounting the stairs. Medusa Heads appear for the first time: they fly in a sinusoidal (wavy) arc left to right. Learn the pattern now — the amplitude and frequency are consistent. Whip icons appear here; look for them in candles on the second floor section. The Chain Whip upgrade should appear by the end of this stage.

**1-3:** A longer run with mixed enemies: zombies, bats, and the beginning of the castle's regular rotation. Find the Axe sub-weapon in a candle near the midpoint — it matters for the boss. Before the boss door, break every candle to fill your hearts.

**Boss: Giant Bat**
The Giant Bat hangs from the ceiling and swoops at Simon. The simplest approach: stand at the far left of the room and throw the Axe in an arc when the bat descends. The arc naturally tracks upward, which is where the bat spends most of its time. Three to four axe hits should end it. Alternatively, whip it when it swoops — but the timing is tight and you'll take hits learning it.

---

### Block 2 — The Castle Interior
**Stages 2-1, 2-2, 2-3 | Boss: Medusa**

**2-1:** Two enemy types make their introduction: Fishmen (jump from the water floor, arcing toward you — wait for them to crest and whip mid-arc) and the first Axe Knights (they walk, stop, throw a vertical axe that bounces, then repeat). Axe Knights need two or three whip hits. Wait for the axe to travel up and away before moving in to attack. Hidden meat is in the wall of the lowest section.

**2-2:** The whip upgrade to Vampire Killer is here — it's in a candle in the upper section. Do not miss it; it changes the rest of the game. Find the Cross in another candle before this stage ends. The Cross replaces the Axe going forward. Enemies are denser here; slow down, kill everything methodically.

**2-3:** Medusa Heads return in larger numbers. The key corridor has them flying at mid-height in waves. The trick is to stand at the far right of each safe platform and whip toward them as they arrive at your level. Never jump unless you've confirmed your landing spot is clear. A single Medusa Head impact sends Simon flying — off platforms and into pits if you're unlucky.

**Boss: Medusa**
The giant Medusa head floats and fires two Medusa Head projectiles at Simon simultaneously. The arena has floating platforms at different heights. Three things matter: the Cross kills her fast, the Stopwatch makes the fight trivial by freezing her, and if you have neither, whip her when she's at your height and jump the projectiles. She has moderate health. With a Triple-Fire Cross, she's gone in under five throws. Without the Cross, patience wins over aggression.

---

### Block 3 — The Clock Tower Approach
**Stages 3-1, 3-2, 3-3 | Boss: The Mummies**

**3-1:** Spike traps appear for the first time — vertical spikes that extend and retract from the floor. Time your walks past them; the pattern is consistent. Knights with shields appear: they block frontal attacks and must be whipped from the front at the right moment (when they raise the shield for a different animation). A safe approach is to get close, wait for a gap in the shield cycle, and whip immediately.

**3-2:** The Medusa Head section that players remember. A long horizontal corridor leads to the next staircase, and the entire corridor is continuously populated with flying Medusa Heads. The Stopwatch becomes important here — throw it at the beginning of the corridor, advance as far as possible during the freeze, then resume dodging. Two to three Stopwatch throws and you're through. Without the Stopwatch: keep Simon at the far right edge of each platform, whip incoming heads rather than jumping over them, and move only when the path ahead is clear.

**3-3:** Relatively straightforward compared to 3-2. Bone Pillars (towers of bones that shoot fire arcs upward) appear. The Cross handles them from a distance — throw it horizontally and it will travel through the pillar for a hit on the way and a hit on the way back.

**Boss: The Mummies**
Two mummies, side by side, in a mid-sized room. They throw arcing bandage projectiles and shuffle toward Simon. The strategy: get to the right side of the room as fast as possible, then whip and jump in place while the mummies march toward you. When they cluster on your left, the Cross handles both simultaneously. If you have the Axe instead, aim the arc at the closer mummy and let the second one walk into your whip. Do not stand between them.

---

### Block 4 — The Underground
**Stages 4-1, 4-2, 4-3 | Boss: Frankenstein's Monster and Igor**

**4-1:** Moving platforms over pits. The platforms move in one direction at a fixed speed. Stand in the center and resist the urge to correct — over-correcting sends Simon off the edge. Red Skeletons appear for the first time: they re-assemble after being knocked down. Do not whip them on the ground unless you have a sub-weapon to hit them mid-reassembly — focus fire until they stay down (three to four hits with Vampire Killer).

**4-2:** Bat columns appear — vertical columns of bats that fly toward Simon in a fixed path. The Cross is effective; otherwise, whip them as they approach. This stage also has the first Fleamen (little hopping creatures that spawn rapidly from certain wall tiles). Kill the spawn point first if you can identify it.

**4-3:** More Red Skeletons and Axe Knights combined. Methodical clearing is the only approach. Before the boss door, there is a wall to the left of the stairs with hidden meat — whip it if you need the health.

**Boss: Frankenstein's Monster and Igor**
Two enemies in one room. Frankenstein is the large one — he walks slowly left and right and punches. Igor is the small hunchback who hops erratically across the room. The rule: kill Igor first. He is unpredictable, he bounces over your whip at unexpected moments, and having him alive while you manage Frankenstein causes most deaths in this fight.

Position: stand as far left as possible against the wall. Igor will hop toward you and pause briefly when he lands — that is your window. Whip him. When he gets too close, jump over him and reposition. Once Igor is down, Frankenstein is just a patience exercise: walk away from him, turn, whip when he's two steps away. He doesn't alter his walk based on your position much. The Cross handles both at range.

---

### Block 5 — The Clock Tower
**Stages 5-1, 5-2, 5-3 | Boss: Death**

**5-1:** The Clock Tower itself — enormous gears in the background, narrow platforms, constant Fleamen. The gears are not obstacles, but the Fleamen spawn from walls at irregular intervals. Clear the spawn point whenever you can see one. Moving platforms here have a slight delay before they start moving after Simon lands.

**5-2:** The most demanding platforming section in the game. Narrow ledges, no room for landing errors, and Medusa Heads fill every corridor. The Cross is essential here — throw it forward before advancing so it clears the heads in its path. Move in short segments: clear, advance to the next safe point, clear again.

**5-3:** Short section leading to the boss door. Stock your hearts here — Death is the most expensive boss fight sub-weapon-wise. Fill to maximum before the door.

**Boss: Death (Grim Reaper)**
Death is the hardest boss before Dracula. He floats at varying heights in the upper half of the room while seven scythes orbit him in a circle, then break off and fly toward Simon. The scythes are the main threat — each one is individually dodgeable but managing all seven simultaneously is chaos.

The Cross changes the fight: throw the Cross at Death's body. On the way out, it hits a scythe or two. On the way back, it hits more and often strikes Death a second time. Throw, dodge, throw again. With Triple-Fire Cross, Death's health falls fast.

Without the Cross, use Holy Water under Death's flight path — the lingering flame patches deal damage when he descends. The scythes cannot be destroyed with Holy Water but the flame area pressures Death to move more than your whip alone would.

If you somehow have only the whip: stand under Death, jump to whip his body when he descends, and dodge every scythe by timing small lateral movements. This is genuinely hard and will take multiple attempts without practice.

---

### Block 6 — Dracula's Chamber
**Stages 6-1, 6-2, 6-3 | Boss: Dracula**

**6-1:** A gauntlet of every enemy type combined. Red Skeletons, Axe Knights, Medusa Heads, and Fleamen appear in the same rooms. The Cross is essential for dealing with multiple enemies at once. Preserve your heart supply — you need it for Dracula.

**6-2:** A brief but spike-filled section. Most of the difficulty here is aesthetic deception — the spikes look lethal but the actual hitboxes are narrower than they appear on screen. You have more room to walk past them than the graphics suggest.

**6-3:** The final ascent. Short. The candle to the left of Dracula's door respawns if you go back down and return — use this to fill your hearts to maximum before the fight.

---

## Boss: Count Dracula

Dracula is a two-phase fight. The room has a central stone floor with pits on each side. Do not fall into the pits.

**Phase 1 — Dracula in his cape:**
Dracula stands at the far right of the room and teleports repeatedly. Each time he appears, he is invincible for roughly one second, then his cape opens and he fires three fireballs in a spread pattern (one high, one mid, one low). Only his head and upper body take damage — the cape is invincible.

Track his position. When he appears and the cape starts to open, throw the Cross immediately. It will hit him on the way out and on the way back for two hits per throw. If you don't have the Cross, wait for the fireball burst to end (jump over the mid and low shots, let the high one pass over), then step in and whip his head.

Never whip while he's cloaked and invincible — you waste animation frames you need for dodging. Wait, dodge, hit.

**Phase 2 — Demon Dracula:**
Dracula transforms into a floating demon head with a different attack: clusters of small fireballs that erupt in multiple directions. The clusters are slow enough to walk between. His hitbox is now his entire visible form.

Stand under where he floats and jump to whip his face. The Cross, if you have hearts left, still works — throw it at him and it handles the back-and-forth damage automatically. Dracula's Phase 2 health is substantial but his attacks are more predictable than Phase 1. Don't rush. Take the hits only when necessary, keep whipping, and he'll fall.

## Hidden Meat (Health Restoration)

Meat is hidden in breakable wall sections throughout the game. Whip suspicious-looking wall tiles — anything that appears slightly different in shading or seems out of place. Major locations:

- Block 1, Stage 1-1: Upper wall section past the first set of stairs.
- Block 2, Stage 2-1: Lowest water-level section, left wall.
- Block 3, Stage 3-3: Several hidden pieces before the boss staircase.
- Block 4, Stage 4-3: Left wall adjacent to the final staircase.
- Block 6, Stage 6-3: Left wall before Dracula's door — check both sides of the door corridor.

If you're low on health before a boss, walk back through the previous rooms until you find a wall piece you missed. The game doesn't bar you from backtracking within a stage.

## Codes and Passwords

The NES Castlevania has a password system. Codes are long alphanumeric strings. Notable shortcuts:

**Start at Block 4:** `BGTB4E AAAAAA`
**Start at Block 5:** `AGTQDE TTATAX`
**Start at Block 6:** `AGTQ5E TTATAX`

These are useful when you can consistently clear the early blocks and want to practice the later ones. Under emulation, save states make passwords largely unnecessary — but for any kind of "no save state" run, knowing the Block 5 and 6 passwords saves you considerable time.

## Frequently Killed By / FAQ

**Q: I keep dying on the stairs. What am I doing wrong?**
Stop using stairs as a combat surface. Kill every enemy near a staircase before you start climbing. If you're mid-stair and an enemy appears, try to descend to a flat surface before engaging. The hit-on-stairs knockback is effectively instant death in most staircase situations.

**Q: The Medusa Head section in Block 3 is impossible.**
It isn't, but it requires the Stopwatch. Throw it at the start of the corridor, sprint forward, throw again when enemies unfreeze. Two uses of the Stopwatch covers most of the corridor. Without the Stopwatch, move in short hops between the waves of heads, and only jump when you've confirmed the landing zone is clear. Never jump over a Medusa Head — let it pass by walking under it if possible.

**Q: I run out of hearts constantly.**
Two causes. First, you may be throwing sub-weapons too liberally between bosses — conserve hearts until a boss room or a dangerous corridor. Second, you may not be breaking all the candles and killing all the enemies along the route. Every enemy drops hearts with some frequency. Kill everything.

**Q: Death killed me repeatedly. Any shortcut?**
The only shortcut is the Cross. If you lost it somewhere in Block 5, that's fixable — there are candle setups where the Cross reappears in Blocks 5-2 and 5-3. If your run has no Cross at all, Holy Water under Death's position and careful scythe dodging is the method. It's slower but reliable once you know the scythe orbit pattern.

**Q: Does Dracula have a fixed teleport pattern?**
Not exactly fixed, but his landing spots are limited. He will appear either at the left side, the center, or the right side of the room, always at roughly standing height. He will not appear directly on top of you. Watch for the flash that signals the teleport and position yourself to be at a safe distance from any of his possible landing zones.

## Overall Tips

- **Attack early, not late.** Simon's whip hits a full second after you press B. Attack when enemies are slightly farther away than feels natural.
- **The Cross is the whole game.** Everything from Block 3 onward is significantly harder without it. If you pick up any other sub-weapon by accident in the late game, immediately find a candle that gives the Cross back.
- **Triple Fire makes bosses trivial.** The III upgrade is uncommon but it appears in specific candles in Blocks 3–5. If you find it with the Cross equipped, the next boss will barely be a fight.
- **Don't waste healing.** Meat restores a large portion of health. Save it for dangerous sections, not for damage you've already taken from weak enemies.
- **Conserve hearts before bosses.** Walk the last stage of each block slowly, break every candle, kill every enemy. Enter each boss with a full heart count.
