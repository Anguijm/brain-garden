# Mega Man 2 — NES Walkthrough

Mega Man 2 is the benchmark action-platformer on the NES. Eight robot masters, a Wily fortress, a weapon system that rewards learning, and a difficulty curve that is demanding without being unfair. This guide covers the full game: stage order, weapon strategies, Energy Tank locations, Item pickups, boss strategies, and the Wily Castle. The guide assumes you are playing on Normal difficulty; Difficult mode costs Mega Man more damage per hit.

---

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| D-Pad Left/Right | Move |
| D-Pad Up | Climb ladder (up), aim some weapons upward |
| D-Pad Down | Descend ladder, duck (no ducking in MM2 — Mega Man can't crouch) |
| A | Jump. Hold longer for higher jump. |
| B | Fire arm cannon or selected weapon |
| Start | Pause, open weapon/item menu |
| Select | Cycle through weapons (hold Start to open menu) |

One thing that trips players up: you select weapons from the pause menu, not by cycling with a button. Open it with Start, use the D-pad to select a weapon, then close with Start.

## Weapons Guide

You earn one weapon from each robot master. Here is every weapon, what it does, and how useful it actually is.

**Metal Blade** (from Metal Man)
Mega Man fires a large saw blade. It travels in one of eight directions — hold a direction on the D-pad when firing to change the angle. Costs only 1 energy unit per shot, which is almost nothing. This is the single most powerful and versatile weapon in the game. You will use it constantly. Metal Man is the correct first boss for exactly this reason.

**Air Shooter** (from Air Man)
Fires a spread of small tornadoes that arc upward. Effective against flying enemies and the Crash Man boss. Less useful on the ground since the spread makes hitting low targets awkward. Moderate energy cost.

**Bubble Lead** (from Bubble Man)
A bubble that rolls along the floor. Deals damage to enemies it touches and can hit floor-level targets arm cannon cannot. Most importantly: it is the only weapon that damages the final Wily boss. Keep at least a sliver of energy on this weapon through the entire Wily Castle.

**Quick Boomerang** (from Quick Man)
Rapid-fire boomerangs at low energy cost. Fast and useful for general combat. Metal Blade is better, but Quick Boomerang still earns use against Metal Man during the Wily boss rush.

**Crash Bomber** (from Crash Man)
Fires a sticky bomb that attaches to a surface and detonates after a short delay. Damage output is high. Required for four of the Wily Castle bosses. Budget your Crash Bomber energy carefully — running dry before the Boobeam Trap means an E-Tank rescue or a restart.

**Time Stopper** (from Flash Man)
Freezes all enemies and projectiles on screen for the duration of its energy bar. You cannot fire while it is active. The entire weapon bar drains as one use — you hold it until it runs out. Deals fixed damage to Quick Man (exactly half his health). Its main use is getting through Quick Man's laser rooms safely.

**Leaf Shield** (from Wood Man)
Wood Man surrounds himself with orbiting leaves; you do the same. While the shield orbits, press B to throw it forward as a projectile. The shield blocks some enemy shots while active. Air Man's weakness. Situationally useful.

**Atomic Fire** (from Heat Man)
A charged fire shot. Tap B for a weak shot; hold B to charge for a medium or full-power blast. Full charge is extremely powerful — one-shots most enemies and deals massive boss damage — but drains energy fast. Wood Man's primary weakness. Use charged shots sparingly.

## Recommended Boss Order

This order minimizes difficulty by chaining weapon advantages as early as possible:

**1. Metal Man** — Arm cannon only. Metal Blade is the payoff.  
**2. Air Man** — Metal Blade helps but is not his weakness. Arm cannon if you prefer. Gets you Item 2 and Air Shooter.  
**3. Crash Man** — Air Shooter wrecks him. Gets you Crash Bomber.  
**4. Flash Man** — Metal Blade. Gets you Time Stopper and Item 3.  
**5. Quick Man** — Time Stopper for his laser rooms and to deal half his boss health. Gets you Quick Boomerang.  
**6. Bubble Man** — Metal Blade. Gets you Bubble Lead.  
**7. Wood Man** — Atomic Fire (charged shots), or Metal Blade, or Crash Bomber. Gets you Leaf Shield.  
**8. Heat Man** — Bubble Lead. Gets you Atomic Fire and Item 1.

## Stage Walkthroughs

### Metal Man

**Theme:** Industrial factory with conveyor belts that push Mega Man left or right.  
**Key enemies:** Met (hard hats that only reveal themselves to shoot), Picket Man (throws picks), Sniper Joe (shields himself between shots), Moles (pop from the ground).  

The opening section has conveyors moving left and right. Walk against the current to maintain position; when you need to cross gaps, time your movement with the belt direction. Met enemies are harmless while crouched — only shoot when they pop up to fire.

About midway through, you reach a vertical ladder section. Climb past the Sniper Joes and into a room with a series of conveyor platforms over a pit. An **Energy Tank** sits in this area, reachable by taking the upper path before descending to the boss door.

Metal Man jumps back and forth across the room and slides across the floor, throwing Metal Blades in two directions. His pattern is: jump, land, throw blade left, throw blade right, repeat. Stay toward the center of the room, jump his slides, and fire arm cannon shots when he lands. Three to four hits finish him. He is easy — the game's warm-up boss.

**Reward:** Metal Blade, Item 2 (see Items section)... wait, Air Man gives Item 2. Metal Man gives no Item. His reward is the Metal Blade.

---

### Air Man

**Theme:** Sky platform stage with cloud backgrounds.  
**Key enemies:** Pea (floats toward you), Batton (bats that hang then swoop), Telly (rotating enemy).  

This stage is mostly linear platform hopping with flying enemies. Metal Blade dispatches most of them efficiently. The main hazard is the Battons that swoop from off-screen — fire a preemptive Metal Blade in their direction.

There are sections where you must hop across small cloud platforms over bottomless pits. Take your time. There is no penalty for pausing on a platform to wait for enemies to clear.

Air Man himself hovers in place and alternates between two attacks: a spread of three large tornadoes that arc toward you, and a single tornado burst. The tornadoes fill the room and are hard to avoid with the arm cannon since you have to keep moving. With Leaf Shield, you can walk into the tornado stream and the shield blocks shots — then throw it at Air Man for damage. Without Leaf Shield, stay at the far left, jump over the low tornadoes, and fire Metal Blade at him during the gaps. He takes four to five Metal Blade hits.

**Reward:** Air Shooter, Item 2 (the hover platform).

---

### Crash Man

**Theme:** Construction site with ladders, springs, and scaffold platforms.  
**Key enemies:** Springer (springs that bounce), Spring Head, Skeleton Joe (throws bones in an arc).  

The stage is heavily vertical. You climb many ladders and deal with Skeleton Joes whose bone throws arc unpredictably — wait for them to throw, then move into the opening. Springers bounce vertically and are best dispatched with Metal Blade before they can collide.

An **Energy Tank** is hidden in a side room partway through the stage. After a ladder climb past Springers, check left before continuing right — a concealed passage leads to a large item pickup room.

The **Yashichi** (a full health and weapon energy refill) is in the room with the narrow platform section. Look for a room where enemies zip across on a track — the Yashichi is on a platform at the far end of the room. Grab it before the boss fight.

Crash Man jumps in large arcs and drops Crash Bombers on the ground below him. The bombs land and explode after a short delay. Stay on the move and jump when he jumps so you end up on the opposite side of the room from his landing spot. Fire Air Shooter during the windows when he is in the air. Air Shooter hits him multiple times as the tornadoes arc upward. Five to six hits.

**Reward:** Crash Bomber.

---

### Flash Man

**Theme:** Ice stage with mirror-like floors and a cold aesthetic.  
**Key enemies:** Changkey Maker (spawns small fire sprites), Shotman (fires upward arcs).  

The floors are slippery — you have some momentum when walking and cannot stop instantly. Adjust your movement expectations. Changkey Maker enemies release small fire sprites that chase you; kill the maker first with Metal Blade before dealing with the sprites.

Shotman enemies fire bullets that arc upward in a spread pattern. They stand still and fire. Kill them from a distance with Metal Blade before closing.

Flash Man himself stands in the center of the room, jumps, and periodically activates his Time Stopper to freeze Mega Man — you take reduced damage while frozen but cannot move. His pattern: jump, land, fire three shots horizontally, jump, land, Time Stop. Fire Metal Blade as fast as you can during the windows between his freeze. He dies in three to four Metal Blade hits.

**Reward:** Time Stopper, Item 3 (wall-climbing platform).

---

### Quick Man

**Theme:** Futuristic facility with the famous laser beam gauntlet.  
**Key enemies:** Quick Met (fast spinning met), Springer.  

The stage opens normally, but soon you enter **three rooms with horizontal laser beams** that sweep the screen from left to right (or right to left). Each beam instantly kills Mega Man on contact. The rooms require either memorizing the pattern and running through precisely, or using Time Stopper.

**Time Stopper strategy (recommended):** Activate Time Stopper the moment you enter the first beam room. The beams freeze in place. Walk through all three rooms. Time Stopper's energy will run out somewhere in the second or third room — if it runs out while you are between beams, you may still get through on momentum. Carry an E-Tank to refill if needed (Time Stopper has its own energy bar, not shared with Mega Man's health).

**Pattern memorization (optional):** The beams move in predictable cycles. Watch two full cycles before moving. The gaps are large enough to walk through with practice, but one mistake kills instantly.

After the laser rooms, the stage is straightforward. Quick Man's boss room: he dashes back and forth across the screen at high speed and throws Quick Boomerangs that return to him. He moves faster than almost any enemy in the game. 

Activate Time Stopper immediately at the start of the fight — it deals exactly half Quick Man's health as fixed damage when activated. Then finish the other half with Crash Bomber or arm cannon. If your Time Stopper is depleted, use Crash Bomber (several hits) or arm cannon (many hits while dodging his dashes).

**Reward:** Quick Boomerang.

---

### Bubble Man

**Theme:** Underwater stage with reduced gravity and aquatic enemies.  
**Key enemies:** Octobulb (fires bubbles), Swimming enemy types, Bitt (bouncing fish-like enemies).  

The underwater physics make Mega Man float when jumping — longer, higher arcs that can be hard to control. Enemy movement is also slowed. Take your time and learn the floaty jump physics before attempting precision platforms.

Watch for spike-covered ceilings in some sections. The camera angle keeps the spikes off-screen until you are almost in them — hug the floor in rooms where the ceiling is hidden.

An **Energy Tank** is in a room partway through, reachable by taking the lower path at a branching section.

Bubble Man himself moves by jumping in large arcs — the underwater physics make his jumps absurdly high. He fires bubbles diagonally forward and downward, and occasionally fires spike balls. Stand in the far corner and fire Metal Blade when he lands near you. He dies quickly to Metal Blade — three to four hits.

**Reward:** Bubble Lead.

---

### Wood Man

**Theme:** Forest with large trees and platforms among branches.  
**Key enemies:** Mono Roader (rolling wheel enemy), Fly Boy (dives from above), Big Fish (giant fish that leaps from bottom pits).  

Big Fish enemies jump from pits at the base of the screen. They are large and hard to dodge — learn which pits they come from and be positioned away from them. Metal Blade kills them in one hit.

Fly Boy enemies come from off-screen and dive at angles. Pre-aim Metal Blade upward when walking under trees where they can appear.

Wood Man has the most complex boss pattern of the eight masters. He stands in the center of the room and deploys a Leaf Shield — four orbiting leaves — then throws the entire shield at you as a projectile. While the shield is orbiting him, you cannot damage him with most weapons. After throwing the shield, he jumps, and the cycle repeats.

The opening windows for attack: the moment he appears (before the shield deploys), and immediately after he throws the shield (before it returns). Fire charged Atomic Fire during these windows for massive damage. Metal Blade also works — fire as fast as you can during the throw window. Crash Bomber sticks to him and deals damage even through the shield cycle. Three to four charged Atomic Fire shots finish him.

**Reward:** Leaf Shield.

---

### Heat Man

**Theme:** Fire and lava stage. The most visually intense stage.  
**Key enemies:** Friender (robotic dog that fires flames), Atomic Fire enemy sprites.  

The opening section has standard enemies. Then you reach the notorious **disappearing block** section: a series of blocks over lava that appear and disappear in sequence. The blocks cycle in a set pattern. Watch the pattern for one full cycle before moving, then follow the safe blocks. The timing is not fast — you can move at a measured pace.

**Item 1** is obtained from Heat Man after clearing the stage (it is a weapon/item reward, not found in the stage). It creates a moving platform that jets forward — this item is what makes the disappearing block section much easier if you have it from a previous run. On a first playthrough, you do not have Item 1 yet; navigate the blocks manually.

Friender enemies breathe a narrow stream of fire. They appear in groups on the floor. Fire Metal Blade before they can position — one hit each.

Heat Man stands in the center of the room and attacks in two ways: he curls into a fireball and rolls across the floor (unavoidable if you are standing in his path — jump over him), or he fires Atomic Fire shots. The fight is moderately difficult. Stay toward the edges, jump the rolling fireball, and fire Bubble Lead when he stands upright. Bubble Lead deals heavy damage to him — five to six hits. Without Bubble Lead, Crash Bomber or arm cannon works but takes much longer.

**Reward:** Atomic Fire, Item 1 (the jet sled platform).

## Item Locations and Uses

**Item 1** — Reward from defeating Heat Man.  
Creates a small jet sled that appears under Mega Man and flies forward at medium speed until it hits a wall. Hold it to ride, or use it as a platform. Required in some Wily stages to cross wide horizontal gaps. High weapon energy cost — do not use it casually.

**Item 2** — Reward from defeating Air Man.  
Creates a hovering platform that floats in the air wherever you deploy it. You can repeatedly deploy it mid-jump to effectively fly across gaps. Low energy cost per use. This is the most versatile movement tool in the game — use it freely in any stage you find difficult to navigate.

**Item 3** — Reward from defeating Flash Man.  
Creates a platform that climbs vertically along a wall. Deploy against a wall surface and stand on the platform to ride it upward. Required in specific Wily stage sections. Moderate energy cost.

## Energy Tank Locations

Energy Tanks (E-Tanks) restore Mega Man's entire health bar when used from the pause menu.

- **Metal Man's stage:** Upper path in the conveyor belt room before the descent to the boss.
- **Crash Man's stage:** Side room off the main vertical section, left of the main ladder path.
- **Wily Stage 1:** In the main traversal section before the boss.
- **Wily Stage 4:** Near the Boobeam Trap — critical to find before the fight.

Carry all E-Tanks into the Wily stages. The boss rush in Wily 5 and the Wily fights themselves will exhaust your health.

## Wily Castle Walkthrough

### Wily Stage 1 — Mecha Dragon

Standard traversal opening with platform enemies. At the end of the stage, a giant mechanical dragon (the Mecha Dragon) chases Mega Man up a vertical shaft. You must climb and leap across platforms while the dragon pursues from below. Keep moving upward — falling behind means it catches you. If you have Item 2, use it to cover gaps fast.

At the top, the Mecha Dragon is the boss. It flies back and forth and breathes fireballs. Stand on the high ledge on the right side of the room and fire Crash Bomber at its face when it passes. Several hits end it.

### Wily Stage 2 — Picopico-kun

Platform traversal with a boss at the end: **Picopico-kun**, a large wall structure made of blocks. It absorbs little Met enemies and incorporates them into its body, expanding. It also fires projectiles from its body. Use Crash Bomber on it — stick bombs to the central sections and let them detonate. The absorbed Mets can be shot out of it. Target the core blocks. Several Crash Bombs finish it.

### Wily Stage 3 — Guts-Dozer

A short stage leading to the **Guts-Dozer**, a massive robotic bulldozer that charges across the room. It fires bullets from its eye. Jump over it when it charges, fire Crash Bomber at the eye, and land behind it. Repeat. It turns around and charges again. Several Crash Bomb hits on the eye finish it.

### Wily Stage 4 — Boobeam Trap

**The most punishing single encounter in the game.** 

This stage has few enemies but a maze-like layout. The boss at the end is the **Boobeam Trap**: five Crash Bomber-resistant cannons embedded in walls. The only weapon that damages them is the Crash Bomber — and each cannon requires one Crash Bomb to destroy. You have five cannons and a limited Crash Bomb supply.

**Do not waste a single Crash Bomb before this fight.** If you used Crash Bombers carelessly in earlier stages, you may not have enough to clear all five cannons. Use an E-Tank to refill if you find one nearby — check the area before the boss door.

The cannons fire at fixed intervals. Learn which cannon fires next, dodge, and place a Crash Bomb on the nearest cannon while the others are dormant. Work systematically from one side to the other. After all five are destroyed, the fight ends.

### Wily Stage 5 — Boss Rush

Eight rooms, each containing one of the robot masters you fought earlier. They use the same patterns. You have all their weapon weaknesses now — hit each one with its weakness for a fast kill:

1. Flash Man — Metal Blade
2. Air Man — Leaf Shield  
3. Bubble Man — Metal Blade
4. Quick Man — Crash Bomber (Time Stopper is depleted most likely)
5. Heat Man — Bubble Lead
6. Wood Man — Metal Blade or Crash Bomber
7. Crash Man — Air Shooter
8. Metal Man — Metal Blade (his own weapon destroys him in one or two hits)

Use E-Tanks between rooms where needed. This gauntlet is the game testing whether you learned it.

### Wily Stage 6 — Wily Machine 2 and Final Form

The final stage. Short traversal, then the endgame boss sequence.

**Wily Machine 2 (Phase 1):** The machine has a large central body with a cockpit. It fires projectiles from the cockpit. Fire arm cannon or Metal Blade at the cockpit window — it is the only vulnerable part. After enough hits, the cockpit explodes.

**Wily Machine 2 (Phase 2):** The body continues fighting with a different attack pattern. Keep firing Metal Blade or arm cannon at the exposed sections. Several more hits finish it.

**Alien (Wily's true form):** Dr. Wily appears as a hovering alien form. This is the final boss.

**Critical:** The alien is immune to all weapons except Bubble Lead. Fire Bubble Lead at it until it dies. It moves slowly and fires a spread of energy projectiles — dodge by moving to the opposite side of the room and fire Bubble Lead continuously. Eight to ten hits finish it.

After the Alien falls, watch the ending sequence. Wily admits defeat.

## Secrets and Codes

**Metal Blade costs almost nothing.** Each shot costs 1 energy unit. You can fire hundreds of shots before running out. Use it as your primary weapon for almost every enemy in the game.

**Metal Blade fires in eight directions.** Hold up-right and press B to fire diagonally upward, for example. This lets you hit enemies you would otherwise have to jump to reach.

**Metal Man is destroyed by his own weapon.** During the boss rush in Wily Stage 5, equip Metal Blade and fire it at Metal Man. He dies in one or two hits. His own creation defeats him.

**Time Stopper in Quick Man's stage.** You do not need perfect timing to survive the laser rooms. Activate Time Stopper at the entrance to the first beam room and walk through all three rooms while everything is frozen. The weapon will run out by the end — if you have energy left when it expires, pause immediately and refill with an E-Tank if you have one.

**Boobeam Trap Crash Bomb count.** You need exactly five Crash Bombs available for the fight. If you arrive with fewer, you cannot complete the fight without cheating or restarting. Save Crash Bombers throughout the Wily stages.

**Bubble Lead in the final fight.** It is easy to forget Bubble Lead exists since its in-game utility is modest. Remember it the moment you reach the Alien. It is the only thing that works.

**Password system.** Mega Man 2 uses a 5x5 dot grid password. After completing certain combinations of stages and collecting E-Tanks, your password encodes that state. Write it down before turning off the console. The title screen has a Password option. Common passwords to know:
- Completing all 8 robot masters with 4 E-Tanks: A5-B2-B4-C1-C3-C5-D4-D5-E2
- Wily Stage 1 start (all bosses cleared, 4 E-Tanks): A5-B2-B4-C1-C3-C5-D4-D5-E2

**Difficulty affects damage received.** Normal mode takes less damage per hit. Difficult mode doubles or triples incoming damage — not recommended for a first playthrough.

## General Tips

**Learn Metal Blade's range immediately.** The diagonal shots reach enemies at angles the arm cannon cannot, and the low energy cost means you can fire freely.

**Use the pause menu deliberately.** The weapon menu is your inventory screen. Taking five seconds to swap to the right weapon before a boss fight is worth it. There is no penalty for pausing.

**E-Tanks are the safety net.** Carry them into the Wily stages. You will need them for the boss rush and the final fight.

**The boss rush is not optional.** Wily Stage 5 forces you to fight all eight robot masters again in sequence. This is the game confirming you understood what you learned.

**Height matters in most boss fights.** Many bosses fire at floor level. If you can get to a higher platform and fire downward, you avoid the majority of their projectiles.

**Rush does not exist in this game.** Rush the robotic dog is a Mega Man 3 addition. You navigate on your own in MM2, aside from the Items.
