# Mega Man 3 — NES Walkthrough

## Introduction

Mega Man 3 arrived in North America in 1992, and it brought the series' biggest expansion yet: a slide move, a loyal robot dog named Rush with multiple support modes, a rival in Break Man who harasses you between stages, and eight all-new Robot Masters followed by a second act of four revisited stages packed with Doc Robots running the AI of Mega Man 2's bosses. The result is a long game — longer than anything in the series to that point — and one of the richest action-platformers on the NES.

The basic loop is the same as Mega Man 2: pick a stage from the menu, defeat the Robot Master, absorb their weapon, use that weapon as the weakness of the next boss on your list. What changes here is the slide, which transforms how you handle enemies and tight passages, and Rush, who opens entire shortcuts you would not otherwise find. If you rushed through Mega Man 2 and wondered why people loved the series, Mega Man 3 is why.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| D-Pad | Move, aim charged buster (no charge in MM3), duck |
| A | Jump |
| B | Fire / use weapon |
| Down + A | **Slide** — Mega Man ducks and slides forward |
| Select | Pause / open weapon menu |
| Start | Pause |

**The slide** is the most important new addition. It lets you pass under enemy fire, dodge boss attacks that would otherwise hit you, reach low passages, and cancel momentum mid-air if you land and slide immediately. In tight boss rooms it often means the difference between taking a hit and not. Get comfortable with it before any stage becomes difficult.

---

## Rush Support Items

Rush is acquired in stages as you progress. All three modes share a single ammo bar displayed on the weapon screen.

**Rush Coil** — Available from the start. Summon Rush and jump on his back for a boosted spring jump, roughly twice your normal height. Reaches platforms and items that are otherwise inaccessible. Low ammo cost; use it freely.

**Rush Marine** — Obtained after defeating Shadow Man. A submarine form for navigating water sections. Fires homing torpedoes. Used primarily in Shadow Man's revisited stage and a few Wily Castle sections. Steers sluggishly on land.

**Rush Jet** — Obtained after defeating Needle Man. A flying platform that moves horizontally at a fixed speed. The most useful Rush form for exploration. Press Up or Down to adjust altitude slightly. Carries you across bottomless pits, over obstacles, and — critically — through walls when aimed at the left edge of a room (see the Secrets section).

## Weapons

You collect a weapon from each Robot Master. All weapons consume ammo from their individual bar and refill from the yellow weapon energy capsules dropped by enemies.

| Weapon | From | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Magnet Missile** | Magnet Man | Homes toward the nearest enemy. Fires horizontally then curves up or down. Excellent against fast-moving targets. |
| **Hard Knuckle** | Hard Man | Slow-moving fist that travels straight forward. Very high damage. Can be aimed up and has a slight arc. Essential for several boss kills. |
| **Top Spin** | Top Man | Activates a spinning attack on contact. Devastatingly powerful but drains ammo instantly on impact — one boss kill can cost nearly the entire bar. Aim carefully. |
| **Shadow Blade** | Shadow Man | A shuriken that can be fired in eight directions by holding a diagonal on the d-pad before pressing B. Pierces certain objects. Efficient ammo use and excellent range. |
| **Spark Shock** | Spark Man | Fires an electric bolt that freezes enemies in place. Frozen enemies can be walked through. Does not actually deal damage to most enemies — its primary use is immobilization and Boss weaknesses. |
| **Search Snake** | Snake Man | Fires a snake that travels along floors and climbs walls. Good for reaching enemies on lower terrain. Three snakes fire simultaneously at full ammo. |
| **Gemini Laser** | Gemini Man | Fires a laser that bounces off flat surfaces. Excellent against Needle Man since the room's back wall reflects it back into him. Moderate ammo cost. |
| **Needle Cannon** | Needle Man | Rapid-fire needle shots. Holds button to fire continuously. Good all-purpose weapon for enemies Shadow Blade does not cover. |

## Recommended Boss Order

The weakness chain in Mega Man 3 is circular — every boss has a specific weapon weakness, and each weapon comes from the previous boss on the chain. The order below is practical for a first playthrough: the stages escalate in difficulty as you go, and you never reach a boss without their weakness in hand.

### 1. Magnet Man
**Weakness:** Mega Buster (arm cannon) or Shadow Blade (acquired later)
**No weakness weapon needed** — start here. Magnet Man is one of the easier Robot Masters and his stage is forgiving. He fires homing magnet missiles and occasionally activates a magnetism field that pulls Mega Man toward him — when you see this, jump and stay airborne until it passes. Keep mid-screen distance and shoot consistently. The arm cannon kills him in about twelve shots.

His stage introduces the magnet-platform sections where you walk on ceilings — gravity reverses when you step on the ceiling tiles. The camera does not flip, so ceiling navigation is disorienting at first. Take it slowly and shoot anything that moves above you (which is now below you).

### 2. Hard Man
**Weakness:** Magnet Missile
Switch to Magnet Missile and the fight becomes trivial — the homing trajectory tracks him automatically regardless of where he jumps. He telegraphs his dive bomb (rears up, leaps across the room) with a brief pause. Hard Knuckle bounces when it hits the floor; jump over the bouncing fist. Seven Magnet Missiles end it.

Hard Man's stage has a long outdoor section with pipes and hard-hat Mets. Slide under the hard-hat enemies when their shields are up rather than waiting for them to lower. The stage is straightforward once you clear the first outdoor stretch.

### 3. Top Man
**Weakness:** Hard Knuckle
Top Man spins across the room throwing Top Spin projectiles and can briefly become invulnerable while spinning. Fire Hard Knuckle when he pauses between spin bursts. Four to six hits. Watch the ceiling — Top Man bounces if he hits it and the trajectory can be unpredictable. Stay left or right and fire across the room rather than fighting at close range.

Top Man's stage has moving platforms over spikes and a section with blocks that assemble into a larger barrier. Slide through low gaps and don't rush the platform sections.

### 4. Shadow Man
**Weakness:** Top Spin
Top Spin is powerful enough to kill Shadow Man in four direct hits, but it drains fast — don't fire it into walls or miss. Shadow Man throws shadow blades in a wide arc and can vanish and reappear across the room. He moves quickly. Wait for him to land and fire Top Spin immediately on contact. The fight is over in seconds if your aim is good.

**Shadow Man's stage** contains this game's most famous secret: a hidden room behind a wall accessible only via Rush Jet (see Secrets). The stage itself has rooftop sections, falling spike logs, and a disappearing-block corridor. Rush Coil reaches a 1-Up before the mid-stage checkpoint.

### 5. Spark Man
**Weakness:** Shadow Blade
Shadow Blade fires in eight directions, which matters here because Spark Man bounces around the room unpredictably. He charges up a large spark shot that must be slid under — watch for the crouching animation before it fires. Hold Up-Right or Up-Left and shoot Shadow Blades diagonally to hit him while he charges. Seven hits.

Spark Man's stage has the game's electric spark platforms: touch them and you take damage, but they move in predictable patterns. Study each section for a second before crossing rather than rushing through.

### 6. Snake Man
**Weakness:** Shadow Blade (also Needle Cannon)
Snake Man hops across the room and fires Search Snakes along the floor that track Mega Man. Jump over the incoming snakes and fire Shadow Blades during his descent. He can also jump very high — expect him to reach the top of the screen. Stay central and keep firing. Seven Shadow Blade hits.

Snake Man's stage has a long outdoor section with the Telly robots (miniature UFOs) and a large vertical room descending with moving platforms. The Search Snakes fired by enemies here are fast — keep moving and use the slide.

### 7. Gemini Man
**Weakness:** Search Snake
Gemini Man splits into a real version and a holographic clone. The clone copies his movements but cannot be damaged. The real Gemini Man fires the Gemini Laser; the clone fires a wider-spread burst. The real one is whichever takes damage from Search Snake — fire snakes and watch which version flinches. Three Search Snakes per cluster, aim at the floor so they climb up. Once you've identified the real one, concentrate fire.

Gemini Man's stage takes place in an icy cave with low-gravity water sections. The ice floors cause sliding — treat them as momentum extensions and plan jumps accordingly.

### 8. Needle Man
**Weakness:** Gemini Laser
Needle Man fires his Needle Cannon in rapid bursts and jumps aggressively. The Gemini Laser bounces off the back wall of his arena, which means firing it straight forward hits him on both the outward and return trip — each firing can land two hits. Four Gemini Laser shots (eight impacts) end the fight. Jump his shots, fire Gemini Laser, repeat.

Needle Man's stage is the hardest of the eight. Large spike needles drop from the ceiling in predictable patterns — wait for each set to extend and retract before moving through the gap. Slide where possible; standing-height gaps require it. Rush Coil reaches energy tanks above the main path.

## Break Man (Proto Man) Encounters

Between stages, Break Man — Mega Man's brother, revealed in the ending — intercepts you three times. He appears without warning before specific stages. He slides, fires the arm cannon, and jumps. He is not killable in the traditional sense — the fight ends when you reduce him to low health or when he decides to leave.

His pattern is straightforward: he slides toward you, jumps, and fires mid-jump. Jump when he slides; duck or slide when he fires on the ground. The arm cannon handles him well enough. Taking damage does not matter much since no reward is locked behind a clean win, but learning his patterns pays off for the endgame. He appears before Hard Man's stage, Snake Man's stage, and one Wily Castle approach.

## Doc Robot Stages

After all eight Robot Masters are defeated, four stages reopen with the Doc Robot — a machine running the AI of Mega Man 2's Robot Masters. Each revisited stage contains two Doc Robot fights.

### Shadow Man Stage 2: Wood Man + Heat Man
- **Wood Man (Doc):** His Leaf Shield is the cue — wait for him to drop it, slide toward him, and fire. The arm cannon or Shadow Blade works. Jump the falling leaves.
- **Heat Man (Doc):** Uses Atomic Fire. When he charges the large shot, slide to the opposite end of the room. He blinks before firing. Hit him between charges with Hard Knuckle or arm cannon.

### Gemini Man Stage 2: Flash Man + Bubble Man
- **Flash Man (Doc):** He freezes time — when unfrozen, he fires and moves. The freeze is unavoidable; just make sure you are not standing in his fire path when you unfreeze. Shoot during his brief vulnerability window. Top Spin ends it fast.
- **Bubble Man (Doc):** Mostly immobile; fires Bubble Lead. Stay on the high platforms. Rapid arm cannon.

### Spark Man Stage 2: Metal Man + Quick Man
- **Metal Man (Doc):** Throws metal blades in multiple directions. Stay mid-screen and fire Search Snake along the floor. He takes the snake hits and staggers.
- **Quick Man (Doc):** The hardest Doc Robot. He dashes across the room constantly. Shadow Blade fired diagonally can intercept his dash path. Spark Shock freezes him briefly — use it to set up arm cannon shots. This fight is the Doc Robot section's real difficulty spike.

### Needle Man Stage 2: Air Man + Crash Man
- **Air Man (Doc):** Fires tornadoes in an arc. Stay near the center of the room, jump the low tornadoes, and fire Hard Knuckle through the gaps in the spread.
- **Crash Man (Doc):** Jumps and fires Crash Bombs. The bombs have a short delay before exploding. Position yourself to avoid the blast and fire back during his landing.

## Wily's Fortress

Six stages. Energy tanks are strongly recommended — bring four or five minimum.

### Wily Stage 1: Kamegoro Maker
The boss fires large turtles that split into smaller ones on impact with a wall. Weakness: **Shadow Blade**. Fire at the turtles before they split and target the Kamegoro Maker between volleys. The room floods progressively; use Rush Marine or jump to stay above the water line.

### Wily Stage 2: Yellow Devil Mk-II
Identical mechanic to the original Yellow Devil — the body disassembles and reassembles across the room, and the eye is the only vulnerable point. Weakness: **Hard Knuckle**. Hit the eye as it passes. Memorize the sequence of segments; the path is consistent. The arm cannon also works if ammo is low.

### Wily Stage 3: Hologram Mega Man (Copy Robot)
A copy of Mega Man that mirrors your weapon and movement. Weakness: **Top Spin**. Equip Top Spin, walk into him to make contact, and the fight ends immediately. Do not waste time; this is a one-hit kill if you use the right tool.

### Wily Stage 4: Robot Master Rematch
Eight teleporter doors, each containing a Robot Master rematch. The order is random-appearing but fixed. Carry full energy tanks. Weakness weapons still apply — the order above still works. Spark Shock (Spark Man's weapon) notably **freezes** rather than damages several of the rematch bosses, which is a bug you can exploit to tank your way through with arm cannon while they are frozen.

### Wily Stage 5: Wily Machine No. 3
Two phases.
- **Phase 1:** A large skull mobile. Weakness: **Spark Shock**. Hit the cockpit repeatedly.
- **Phase 2:** The machine rises, Wily exposed in the glass pod. Weakness: **Hard Knuckle** or **Search Snake**. Fire directly at the pod. He fires scatter bombs — slide under them.

### Wily Stage 6: Gamma
The final boss is a massive robot. Two phases.

- **Phase 1:** Gamma attacks with two fists alternately. He swings one, you dodge, and you fire at whichever weapon pod is active. Weakness: **Hard Knuckle** into the head. His fists each have a gap in their swing — slide through the gap if you are cornered.
- **Phase 2:** Gamma's head is destroyed; Dr. Wily sits exposed at the top. He dips down periodically to fire. Weakness: **Hard Knuckle** or **Top Spin** to the cockpit pod when he descends. Top Spin ends this phase very quickly — equip it, make contact on his descent, and it deals massive damage. His fire spread is wide; stay to one side and slide under it.

## Secrets and Tricks

### Rush Jet Wall Glitch
In any stage where Rush Jet is available, summon Rush Jet, ride to the far left edge of the screen, and let Mega Man pass through the left wall. Many stages contain hidden rooms on the other side of the left border holding extra lives, weapon energy, and energy tanks. This works most consistently in Top Man's and Shadow Man's stages but functions in several others. The game does not flag these rooms as reachable, and they are not necessary for completion — they exist as surplus rewards for exploration.

### Energy Tank Farming
Energy tanks (E-Tanks) are the most valuable item in the game and can be farmed. Find an E-Tank in a stage, pick it up, then intentionally die. When you continue from the last stage you selected, the E-Tank you collected has been saved in your inventory — and the E-Tank in the stage has respawned. Repeat to build a stockpile. Four to five E-Tanks entering Wily's Fortress makes the harder fights manageable.

### Shadow Man Stage Hidden Room
Shadow Man's stage contains a wall section partway through with no visible entrance. Use Rush Jet at the left edge to clip through and reach a room with a 1-Up and extra weapon energy. This is the most well-known application of the Rush Jet wall glitch and is worth doing on any first run.

### Doc Robot Energy Tank
In Needle Man's revisited stage (Doc Robot: Air Man + Crash Man), there is an E-Tank above the main path reachable with Rush Coil. Do not miss it before the second Doc Robot fight — Air Man's tornadoes can drain health quickly and you want the cushion.

### Slide Under Hard Knuckle Bounce
When Hard Knuckle bounces off the floor, you can slide underneath it. This applies in Hard Man's stage against the Hard Knuckle-throwing enemies and against Hard Man himself. The bounce has a fixed arc height; slide when it starts to descend.

## Tips

**Manage ammo across the Doc Robot stages.** The Doc Robot fights require specific weapons and they cannot be changed mid-fight. Stage revisits are long — keep one eye on your weapon ammo bars as you traverse them so you do not arrive at the Doc Robot fight empty.

**The arm cannon is always an option.** The damage is lower, but it never runs out. In Robot Master fights where your weakness weapon is running low, switching to the arm cannon and grinding the remaining health is always viable. Most Robot Masters take twelve to fourteen arm cannon hits.

**Proto Man leaves if you wait.** The Break Man fights have no timer, but standing still for an extended period eventually makes him leave. This is not a tactic for a normal playthrough, but if you are trying to conserve health for a subsequent stage, being extremely passive works.

**Slide constantly.** Many standard enemies — including the hard-hat Mets, which only expose themselves briefly — are best handled with a slide through them rather than waiting to shoot. The slide passes through their attacks during the early animation frames. Use it offensively as well as defensively.

**Shadow Blade covers five fights.** Its ammo efficiency and eight-direction firing make it the most versatile secondary weapon in the game. Shadow Man through Hard Man's weakness chain means it is available by stage four of eight — stock up on Shadow Blade energy whenever you can.

**Gemini Laser double-hit is the fastest Needle Man kill.** A single Gemini Laser shot fired straight at Needle Man bounces off the far wall and returns, hitting him twice. With full ammo you can end the fight before he lands his second jump.

**Wily Stage 4 Spark Shock exploit.** Several Robot Masters are bugged against Spark Shock — it freezes them instead of dealing damage, but while frozen they take full arm cannon damage with no knockback. If ammo is low on weakness weapons, freeze with Spark Shock and unload the arm cannon.
