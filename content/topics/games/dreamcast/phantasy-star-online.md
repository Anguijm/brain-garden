# Phantasy Star Online Ver. 2 (Dreamcast)

**Developer:** Sonic Team | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 2001

---

## Introduction

**The first console online role-playing game**, and one of the genuinely important games in this library historically. In 2000, consoles did not go online. The Dreamcast shipped with a modem as standard, and Sonic Team built a game around it where four players anywhere in the world explored dungeons together.

It is the direct ancestor of every console online RPG that followed, and it did something no game had needed to before: it worked across language barriers using a **symbol chat** system, so a Japanese and an American player could coordinate without sharing a word.

**Version 2** is the expanded release, adding Ultimate difficulty, higher level caps and new areas.

---

## The honest caveat

**The official servers are long dead.** Sega shut them down years ago.

What you have here is the **single-player game**, which is real and substantial — a full offline campaign across four areas with a story, loot and levelling — but it is not the thing that made this game famous. Fan-run private servers exist and the Dreamcast version can reach some of them, but that requires network configuration well beyond a normal emulator setup and is not set up on this box.

**Play it for the offline campaign and for the historical artefact.** Do not expect the 2001 experience.

---

## Controls

- **Stick:** Move. **A:** Attack / confirm. **B:** Cancel.
- **X / Y:** Action palette slots — weapons, techniques, items.
- **L / R:** Cycle the action palette.

---

## Combat

Real-time, but with a **rhythm**. Attacks come in a three-hit chain and each hit must be timed — pressing attack immediately spams and misses; pressing on the beat lands the full combo with a damage bonus.

**Most people who bounce off this game are mashing.** The timing window is generous once you know it exists.

---

## Classes

Three families, each in three variants:

- **Hunter** — melee, high health, the straightforward choice
- **Ranger** — guns, ranged, safe but slower to kill
- **Force** — techniques (magic), fragile, the strongest late

Each has human, newman and android variants with different stat curves and technique access.

---

## Loot

The core loop is the one every game since has copied: kill things, get randomised weapon and armour drops, get slightly stronger, kill harder things. **Rare drops are genuinely rare** and were the currency of the game's community.

---

## Strategy

**Time your attack chains.** Do not mash.

**Use the action palette.** Swapping weapons mid-fight is how you handle mixed enemy types.

**Photon arts and techniques** are on the same palette as weapons; slot healing where you can reach it.

**Hunter for a first run.** It is the most forgiving.
