# Crazy Taxi (Dreamcast)

**Developer:** Hitmaker (Sega AM3) | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 2000

---

## Introduction

An arcade game about driving a taxi very badly and being rewarded for it. You pick up passengers, drive them where they want to go as fast as physically possible, and earn more for arriving quickly and for driving recklessly on the way.

It is a perfect arcade design: one rule, immediately understood, with a skill ceiling far above where most people stop.

**The Dreamcast version's licensed soundtrack — The Offspring and Bad Religion — is missing from most later re-releases**, which is why the original is still the one people seek out.

---

## Controls

- **Stick:** Steer. **A / R:** Accelerate. **B / L:** Brake and reverse.
- **X:** Change gear (drive/reverse) — needed for the special techniques.

---

## The three techniques that matter

The game is playable without these and unrecognisable with them.

**Crazy Dash.** Shift into drive and hit accelerate at the same instant from a standstill, and you launch forward. Repeat it while moving to keep boosting.

**Crazy Drift.** At speed, tap into reverse and back to drive while turning, and the taxi slides sideways at full speed. This is how you take corners without losing time.

**Crazy Stop.** The reverse-into-drive trick used to stop instantly at a destination rather than overshooting.

**Learning the Crazy Dash roughly doubles your score.** It is not explained clearly in the game, and most people who played it casually never knew.

---

## Scoring

You earn the fare, plus bonuses for speed of delivery. **Passengers are colour-coded by distance** — the arrow above them shows how far they want to go, and long fares pay more but eat clock.

The timer extends on each successful delivery, so a run continues as long as you keep succeeding. **Failing to deliver in time ends the run.**

"Crazy Through" — passing close to traffic at speed — and "Crazy Jump" add tip money.

---

## Modes

**Arcade** is the original three-minute-plus structure. **Original** mode is a larger Dreamcast-exclusive map. **Crazy Box** is a set of skill challenges that teach the advanced techniques and is where you should go to learn them.

---

## Strategy

**Learn the Crazy Dash in Crazy Box first.**

**Take the green-arrow short fares early** to build the clock, then take long fares once you have time banked.

**Drive through parks, over kerbs, down stairs.** The map is fully traversable and the direct line is rarely the road.

**The arrow points at the destination, not the route.** Follow it and improvise.
