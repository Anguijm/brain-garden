# Skies of Arcadia (Dreamcast)

**Developer:** Overworks | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 2000

---

## Introduction

An unusually **optimistic** role-playing game, which is why it stands out. Sky pirates, floating continents, airships, and a protagonist who is straightforwardly brave rather than tormented. The genre in 2000 was deep in its brooding phase; Skies of Arcadia went the other way and is remembered fondly for it.

Vyse, Aika and Fina fly a ship around a world of floating islands, discovering things, and the **discovery mechanic is literal** — you find and register landmarks for money and reputation.

Made by Overworks, the studio formed from Sega's *Sakura Wars* team, and it is the best original RPG on the console.

---

## Controls

- **Stick / D-Pad:** Move. **A:** Confirm. **B:** Cancel.
- **X / Y:** Menus and ship functions.
- **In ship battles:** Grid-based turn selection.

---

## Two combat systems

**On foot** it is turn-based with a twist: **Spirit Points are a shared party pool**, not per-character magic. Everything strong — spells, special moves, defending well — draws from the same pool, which refills a little each turn.

This means a party cannot all use their best abilities at once. **Deciding who spends the shared spirit each turn is the actual tactical layer**, and it is more interesting than the usual per-character MP.

**In ship battles** you fight airship to airship on a grid of turns, seeing several turns ahead, choosing when to fire cannons, when to brace for an incoming attack, and when to hold for a special. It is closer to a puzzle than a fight and it is genuinely distinct.

---

## Moonstones and magic

Magic is tied to six coloured moons, and **your equipped weapon colour determines which spells you can cast**. Changing a character's colour changes their entire magical role mid-battle, and levelling each colour is separate.

**Green heals, purple debuffs, red damages, blue is speed, yellow is lightning, silver is the rare late one.** Rotating colours to level them all is worth doing early.

---

## Discoveries

**Sixty-odd discoveries** are hidden across the world map — landmarks you fly over and register. They pay well, they are the main optional content, and several are easy to miss permanently as the world changes.

There is also **Vyse's crew**: recruitable characters who improve your ship and unlock a base. Most are missable.

---

## Strategy

**Manage the shared Spirit pool.** Do not let one character drain it on a normal fight.

**Level all six magic colours** by rotating weapon colours during easy battles.

**Explore the map in your ship** whenever it expands. Discoveries are how you afford good equipment.

**Recruit crew as you pass them.** They are easy to miss and they matter.

## Emulation Notes

Runs well on Batocera's Flycast core. **This is a two-disc game**, grouped into a single entry by an `.m3u` playlist on this box, with disc changes made from the emulator's disc-control menu.

A GameCube version, *Skies of Arcadia Legends*, added content and rebalanced the notoriously high random-encounter rate. **This Dreamcast original has a heavier encounter rate** — worth knowing, since it is the most common complaint.

Higher internal resolution and widescreen both work well.
