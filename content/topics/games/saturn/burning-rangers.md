# Burning Rangers (Sega Saturn)

**Developer:** Sonic Team | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1998

---

## Introduction

**The last game Sonic Team made for the Saturn**, released in 1998 when the console was already finished, and one of the most ambitious things on the hardware.

You are a futuristic firefighter. The levels are buildings on fire, and the fire is real-time — it spreads, it blocks routes, and it changes the level while you are in it.

Technically it is the Saturn at full stretch: transparency effects the hardware was bad at, particle fire, and voice acting throughout. It also drops frames and the collision is imperfect, which is the honest trade.

---

## Controls

- **Analogue stick:** Move. **A:** Jump / jetpack boost.
- **B:** Fire extinguisher shot — **hold to charge**.
- **C:** Camera. **L / R:** Strafe / camera.

---

## The job

Each mission is a burning structure. You have three tasks, in this order of importance:

1. **Rescue survivors.** Find them, then teleport them out. This is the score and the point.
2. **Extinguish fires** to open routes.
3. **Reach and neutralise the source** — usually a boss or a reactor.

**Fire spreads while you work.** Take too long and routes close behind you. It creates real pressure that a static level never would.

---

## Crystals

Extinguishing fire drops **crystals**, which are simultaneously your ammunition, your health and the currency for rescues. Everything runs on the same resource, so putting out fires you did not need to is not free — but neither is ignoring them.

---

## Navigation

A voice on the radio gives directions, and the levels are genuinely maze-like under smoke. **The navigation voice is the map** and there is not much else.

---

## The randomised layouts

Levels have **variable arrangements** between playthroughs, and survivor placement changes. Combined with the letter-grade scoring, it is designed for repetition rather than a single run.

Rescued survivors send you **email between missions**, which is a small thing that people remember thirty years later.

---

## Strategy

**Rescue first, extinguish second.** Score comes from people.

**Do not burn crystals on fires that block nothing.**

**Follow the voice.** Trying to navigate by sight through smoke wastes the clock.

**Charge the extinguisher** for large fires; tapping it wastes crystals.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs on Batocera's Saturn setup using the **beetle-saturn** core. This is one of the more demanding Saturn games to emulate accurately because of the transparency effects, and it is a fair test of whether the Saturn setup is working properly.

**Uses the analogue stick**, like Sonic Team's [NiGHTS](topics/games/saturn/nights-into-dreams). Never re-released on anything.
