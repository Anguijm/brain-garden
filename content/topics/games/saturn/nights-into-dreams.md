# NiGHTS into Dreams (Sega Saturn)

**Developer:** Sonic Team | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1996

---

## Introduction

Sonic Team's follow-up to Sonic, and nothing like it. Two children enter a dream world and fly through it as NiGHTS, a purple jester, on rails through loops and spirals in a scored time attack.

It shipped alongside the **3D Control Pad**, an analogue controller Sega built for this game — before the Nintendo 64's analogue stick was on shelves in Japan. The game is designed around analogue input and feels wrong without it.

---

## Controls

- **Analogue stick:** Fly. NiGHTS moves on a 2D plane through a 3D world.
- **A / B / C:** Boost / dash.
- **Loop around objects:** Draw a complete circle with your flight path to collect everything inside it.

**Looping is the core mechanic.** Flying a closed loop around a group of items collects all of them at once and creates a link in a combo chain. Chaining loops without breaking is where the score is.

---

## How the scoring works

Each level is four "Mares" — segments — and in each you must collect **20 blue chips** and deliver them to the Ideya Palace to break it open. Do all four, then fight the boss.

**You are on a timer that only runs while you are NiGHTS.** Fall, get hit, or run out of flight time and you drop to the ground as the child, walking slowly, with the clock still running.

**The score is the point of the game.** It is a time attack with a grading system — C through A, then higher — and the game is short precisely because it expects replay.

---

## The link system

Collecting items in an unbroken chain multiplies the score. Breaking the chain resets the multiplier. **A perfect run through a Mare is one continuous flight**, which is what separates a C grade from an A.

---

## Structure

Two children, Claris and Elliot, each with three dream levels plus a shared final one. Short — under two hours to see everything once — and built entirely around returning for better scores.

**A Christmas version**, *Christmas NiGHTS*, was given away with magazines and is a separate, seasonal reworking that is well worth knowing about.

---

## Strategy

**Loop everything.** Never fly in a straight line past a group of items.

**Learn the level layout before chasing score.** The first few runs are reconnaissance.

**Do not touch the ground.** Walking as the child is slow and it is where runs die.

**Watch the flight timer**, not the level clock. Running out of NiGHTS time is the real failure.

## Emulation Notes

Runs on Batocera's Saturn setup using the **beetle-saturn** core.

**This game needs an analogue stick**, which your controller has. It was designed for the 3D Control Pad and the digital D-pad version is a substantially worse experience — if the flight feels stiff, the analogue stick is not being read and the controller mapping is the thing to check.
