# Metal Gear Solid (PlayStation)

**Developer:** Konami | **Publisher:** Konami | **Year:** 1998

---

## Introduction

The game that made stealth a mainstream genre and cinematic presentation a standard expectation. Hideo Kojima had made two *Metal Gear* games on MSX; this one moved them to 3D, added full voice acting, and told a story about nuclear deterrence and genetic determinism that took itself entirely seriously.

Solid Snake infiltrates a nuclear facility in Alaska to stop a terrorist group with a walking battle tank.

Note that the [Game Boy Color game on this box](topics/games/gbc/metal-gear-solid) shares the name and is a completely different, original game.

---

## Controls

- **Left stick / D-pad:** Move — **press lightly to walk quietly**.
- **Circle:** Punch / attack. **X:** Action. **Square:** Fire weapon.
- **Triangle:** First-person view. **Select:** Codec. **R1:** Aim mode.

---

## Stealth

**The radar is the game.** It shows guard positions and their vision cones. Learning to read it, rather than the third-person view, is the whole skill.

Three alert states cascade:

1. **Alert** — guards converge, reinforcements keep arriving
2. **Evasion** — they have lost sight and are searching
3. **Caution** — they have given up but stay watchful

**Fighting through an Alert is almost never right.** Break line of sight, hide, wait.

**Footprints in snow, footsteps on metal, and your own heartbeat on the radar** all give you away.

## The moments people remember

**Psycho Mantis** reads your memory card and comments on your other Sony games, then "moves" your controller. He dodges everything you do — until you **unplug the controller and put it in port 2**, which he cannot read. Nothing in the game tells you this; a codec call hints at it.

**Sniper Wolf** requires the sniper rifle and Diazepam to steady your hands.

**The torture sequence** demands rapid button mashing, and how you handle it changes the ending.

**The Meryl codec frequency is printed on the back of the game's case**, not in the game. Emulated, that means looking it up — it is 140.15.

---

## Items and weapons

Cardboard box, thermal goggles, chaff grenades, nikita missiles, C4, socom pistol. **Chaff grenades disable cameras and electronic detection** and are the most under-used item in the game.

---

## Strategy

**Watch the radar, not the screen.**

**Press the stick gently to walk** rather than run — running is loud.

**Knock on walls** to draw a guard away from a route.

**Use first-person view** to spot things the fixed camera hides.

**Save often.** Ration is not infinite and boss fights are long.

## Emulation Notes

Runs well on Batocera's PlayStation core. **Two discs, grouped as one entry** here by an `.m3u` playlist.

**The Psycho Mantis controller trick needs the emulator's port setting**, not a physical replug: switch the pad to port 2 in the input menu, beat him, then switch back. It works, but it is not the elegant moment it was on hardware.

*The Twin Snakes* on GameCube is a remake with different voice direction and cutscenes; opinion on it is genuinely split. This is the original.
