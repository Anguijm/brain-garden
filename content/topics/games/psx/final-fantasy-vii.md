# Final Fantasy VII (PlayStation)

**Developer:** Square | **Publisher:** Sony | **Year:** 1997

---

## Introduction

The game that made Japanese role-playing games mainstream in the West, and the one that decided the PlayStation would beat the Nintendo 64. Square had made every previous Final Fantasy for Nintendo hardware; cartridges could not hold pre-rendered video, so they left, and took the genre's audience with them.

Cloud Strife, a mercenary, joins an eco-terrorist cell attacking a corporation draining the planet's life for power. It gets stranger from there.

**Three discs**, around forty hours, and a mid-game event that people still avoid spoiling thirty years later.

---

## Controls

- **X:** Confirm. **Circle:** Cancel. **Triangle:** Menu. **Square:** Map.
- **D-pad / stick:** Move. **L1+R1:** Run from battle.

---

## Materia

The whole character system, and it is elegant: **materia are orbs slotted into weapons and armour**, and they grant everything — magic, summons, stat changes, command abilities.

Because materia are equipment rather than class, **any character can do anything**. Move your healing materia to someone else and they become the healer.

**Linked slots are where the depth is.** Two connected slots let a Support materia modify the one beside it:

| Combination | Effect |
|---|---|
| **All + any magic** | Hits every target |
| **Quadra Magic + summon** | Casts it four times |
| **HP Absorb + attack** | Drains health as you hit |
| **Final Attack + Phoenix** | Auto-revives the party when you die |
| **Added Effect + Hades** | Inflicts every status on hit |

**Materia levels up and splits.** A mastered materia produces a fresh copy, so the strong combinations are farmable.

**One catch:** equipping materia **lowers physical stats and raises magical ones**, so loading a physical attacker with magic makes them worse at their job.

---

## Limit Breaks

Filled by taking damage. Each character has several tiers, unlocked by using the previous ones enough and finding manuals. **Limit Breaks are frequently stronger than anything else available**, and the final tier for each character is a substantial side quest.

---

## Missables

The game will let you lose things permanently:

- **Yuffie** and **Vincent** are entirely optional characters and easy to never meet.
- The **Great Gospel** (Aeris's final Limit) is missable.
- Several key items become unobtainable after certain plot points.
- The **Wutai** side quest closes late.

---

## Strategy

**Link All with your attack magic** early. It is the single best combination in the game.

**Do not over-equip materia on physical characters.**

**Recruit Yuffie and Vincent** — both are optional and both are strong.

**Save in multiple slots**, especially before disc changes.

**Farm materia on the Gelnika or in the Northern Crater** for the endgame.
