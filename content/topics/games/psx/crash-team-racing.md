# Crash Team Racing (PlayStation)

**Developer:** Naughty Dog | **Publisher:** Sony | **Year:** 1999

---

## Introduction

The kart racer that people who dislike kart racers respect. *Mario Kart 64* was the target, and Naughty Dog's answer was to build a game with a **genuine skill ceiling** — a boost system deep enough that expert play looks nothing like beginner play.

It was Naughty Dog's last Crash game before they moved to *Jak and Daxter*, and it is still argued to be the best kart racer ever made.

---

## Controls

- **X:** Accelerate. **Square:** Brake. **Circle:** Fire item.
- **L1 / R1:** **Hop, and hold to slide.**
- **D-pad / stick:** Steer.

---

## The boost system, which is the entire game

This is what separates CTR from every other kart racer, and it is not optional at high level.

**While sliding, a meter at the bottom fills.** Press the *other* shoulder button when it enters the orange-to-red zone and you get a **boost**. Do it three times in a single slide for the maximum.

Then — and this is the crucial part — **land from that slide and immediately start another.** A skilled player chains slides continuously, holding boost for an entire lap.

**Someone who does not learn this will lose to someone who has, by a full lap.** The tracks are designed with slide opportunities on nearly every corner and several straights.

**Sacred fire / U-turn boosts**, reserve boost carried through jumps, and boost-preserving landings are further layers on top.

---

## Items

Wumpa fruit is the currency: **collect ten and your items become supercharged**, and your top speed rises. Most players ignore fruit and should not.

Items include bowling bombs, TNT crates (which stick to your kart and you shake off by hopping), Nitro crates, missiles, invisibility and the Warp Orb — the blue-shell equivalent that hunts the leader.

---

## Adventure mode

A genuine single-player campaign with a hub world, boss races, relic time trials, CTR token challenges and gem cups. **It is far more substantial than a kart game needs to be** and it is the reason the game has such affection attached.

**N. Tropy and Oxide time trials** are the endgame, and they demand near-perfect boost chaining.

---

## Strategy

**Learn to chain slides before anything else.** Nothing else matters as much.

**Collect wumpa fruit constantly.**

**Hop to shake off a TNT crate**, do not brake.

**Take the inside line only when you can still slide it.** A tight line without boost is slower than a wide line with it.

**Practise on Crash Cove** until three-boost slides are automatic.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs well on Batocera's PlayStation core at raised internal resolution. The **analogue stick works**, but the game was built around the D-pad and most competitive players still use it — steering precision matters less than boost timing here.

A remake, *Nitro-Fueled*, released in 2019 and is faithful. This is the original.
