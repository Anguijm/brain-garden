# Castlevania: Symphony of the Night (PlayStation)

**Developer:** Konami | **Publisher:** Konami | **Year:** 1997

---

## Introduction

The other half of the word "metroidvania". [Super Metroid](topics/games/snes/super-metroid) supplied the first half; this supplied the second, and every game in the genre since owes both.

Konami took a linear, stage-based action series and rebuilt it as a single explorable castle with role-playing statistics, equipment, levelling and hundreds of items. Alucard, Dracula's son, wakes to find the castle returned.

It sold modestly, was rediscovered, and is now regarded as one of the best games ever made.

---

## Controls

- **D-pad / stick:** Move; down to crouch; **down + jump** to drop through platforms.
- **X:** Jump. **Square:** Attack. **Triangle:** Use item.
- **Circle:** Transform (once unlocked). **L1 / R1:** Shield / cycle.
- **Back-forward + attack** and other fighting-game motions: special weapon techniques.

---

## The castle, and the twist

The map fills in as you explore. Abilities open new routes: double jump, mist form, bat form, wolf form, gravity boots.

**Reaching what appears to be the final boss and winning gives you a short, unsatisfying ending.** The game is roughly half over at that point.

**To get the real game, you must equip three specific items before that fight** — the Holy Glasses, and then in the boss room, remove your equipment and wear the **Ring of Vlad** and **Heart of Vlad**. Doing so reveals that the boss is possessed, and the castle's **entire inverted second half** opens up.

**Nothing tells you this.** It is the most famous hidden requirement in the genre, and a first-time player will almost certainly finish with 20% map completion and think they are done.

---

## Weapons and the shield rod

Dozens of weapons with distinct move sets, plus familiars, spells entered as fighting-game motions, and armour.

**The Shield Rod plus Alucard Shield** combination is famously broken — equip both and press attack, and it becomes an invincible damage aura that trivialises the game. Worth knowing whether you want to avoid it or not.

---

## Strategy

**Explore everything before the throne room.** Map percentage is the game's own progress bar.

**Do not skip the library.** The Librarian sells key items, including the Holy Glasses.

**Save the castle's second half for when you have flight.**

**Try weapon motions.** Most weapons have special techniques nothing announces.

**Aim for 200.6% map completion** if you want everything.

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's PlayStation core — it is a 2D game and undemanding.

**Internal resolution helps very little**, since the art is sprite-based; a CRT-style shader is arguably the better presentation here. The prologue and cutscenes are the only 3D-ish elements.

Michiru Yamane's score is among the most celebrated in games.
