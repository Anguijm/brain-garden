# Spyro the Dragon (PlayStation)

**Developer:** Insomniac Games | **Publisher:** Sony | **Year:** 1998

---

## Introduction

Insomniac's first platformer, and the game that proved the PlayStation could do open, colourful 3D worlds when the received wisdom was that it could not. The technical trick was a custom level-of-detail system that let huge draw distances render without popping — the reason Spyro's worlds feel airy where contemporaries feel like corridors.

A purple dragon frees his elders from crystal after a wizard turns them to stone.

---

## Controls

- **Left stick / D-pad:** Move. **X:** Jump; **X again** to glide.
- **Square:** Charge — a horn-first sprint that kills armoured enemies.
- **Circle:** Flame breath — kills soft enemies.
- **R1 / L1:** Camera. **Triangle:** Look around.

---

## Charge or flame

The one rule that governs all combat: **some enemies can only be charged, others only flamed.**

Armoured, metal or spiky enemies resist fire and must be rammed. Large or fireproof-looking enemies must be flamed. Getting it wrong does nothing at all, which confuses new players who assume they are missing.

---

## Gliding

Jump, then press jump again at the apex to glide. **Gliding is the traversal system**, and most of the game's secrets are behind a glide from a high point to somewhere that looks unreachable.

**There is no double jump.** If a gap seems impossible, you are gliding from the wrong place, not missing a move.

---

## What you collect

- **Gems** — currency for the balloonist, and the completion metric
- **Dragons** — trapped elders, the primary objective
- **Dragon eggs** — held by thieves who must be chased down

**120% completion** requires every gem, dragon and egg across all worlds.

**Sparx**, the dragonfly following you, is your health: he changes colour as you take hits and vanishes before you die. **He also points toward nearby gems**, which most players never notice.

---

## Strategy

**Watch Sparx's colour.** Gold, blue, green, gone — four hits.

**Feed Sparx butterflies** from small critters to heal.

**Glide from the highest point available**, not the nearest.

**Charge armoured, flame soft.** Learn the visual tells.

**Come back for gems later**; some need abilities from other worlds' balloonists.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs very well on Batocera's PlayStation core, and it is one of the best cases for **raised internal resolution** — fully 3D with clean, flat-shaded art that sharpens beautifully.

The 2018 *Reignited Trilogy* remakes this and its two sequels. This is the original, and its draw distance still reads as impressive.
