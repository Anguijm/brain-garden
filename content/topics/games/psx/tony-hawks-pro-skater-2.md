# Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 2 (PlayStation)

**Developer:** Neversoft | **Publisher:** Activision | **Year:** 2000

---

## Introduction

One of the highest-rated games ever released, and the rare sequel that is simply the first game with the missing piece added. That piece is the **manual**, and it changed the entire structure of play.

It is also a cultural artefact: the soundtrack — Rage Against the Machine, Papa Roach, Bad Religion, Naughty By Nature — introduced a generation to punk and hip-hop, and is inseparable from the game's memory.

---

## Controls

- **X:** Ollie / jump. **Circle:** Grab trick. **Square:** Flip trick. **Triangle:** Grind.
- **D-pad + trick button:** Different tricks per direction.
- **Up-down or down-up on the D-pad after landing:** **Manual**.
- **L1 / R1:** Rotate / spin.

---

## The manual, and why it matters

A manual is a wheelie — balancing on two wheels, steered with the D-pad, with a balance meter you must correct.

**It keeps your combo alive between tricks.** Before manuals, a combo ended when you touched the ground. With them, you can land a grind, manual across the flat, ollie into another grind, manual again, and chain the entire level into one score.

**This transformed the series from a trick game into a line game.** Scores went from thousands to millions.

---

## Structure

**Career mode**: each level has objectives — score targets, collect S-K-A-T-E, find the secret tape, a level-specific task — completed across multiple two-minute runs. Completing them earns cash to buy stats and tricks.

**Stat points are spent, not earned passively.** Air, hangtime, ollie, speed, spin, landing, switch. **Spend early points on Air and Ollie**; everything else follows from getting higher.

**Create-a-skater** and a **park editor** are both here and both substantial.

---

## Special tricks

Building your special meter by landing tricks unlocks your skater's signature moves, worth far more points. **The meter drains and resets when you bail**, so a long combo is a compounding risk.

---

## Strategy

**Learn to manual immediately.** It is most of the scoring.

**Buy Air and Ollie first.**

**Do not chase the biggest trick.** A long chain of small tricks beats one huge one, because the multiplier is the number of tricks.

**Revert does not exist here** — that arrives in THPS3. Ramp-to-ramp combos have to route through manuals on flat ground.

**Bail early rather than late.** Losing a combo is better than losing the run.
