# R-Type (Master System)

**Developer:** Compile / Irem | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1988

---

## Introduction

R-Type is the horizontal shooter that made the genre serious. It is slow, deliberate, and built entirely around one idea — the **Force** — that no other shooter had tried.

The Master System conversion is remarkable. The arcade original ran on hardware far beyond an 8-bit console, and this port keeps the level layouts, the bosses, the Force mechanic and most of the feel. It is widely considered one of the best arcade conversions on the system.

It is also very hard, in the memorisation-heavy way arcade games of 1987 were hard.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move.
- **Button 1:** Fire. **Hold to charge**, release for the Wave Cannon.
- **Button 2:** Detach or recall the Force.

---

## The Force, which is the whole game

The Force is an indestructible orb that attaches to the front or back of your ship.

- **Attached**, it blocks all enemy fire from that side and adds firepower.
- **Detached**, it flies forward independently, still indestructible, clearing space ahead.

Because it cannot be destroyed, correct Force placement makes otherwise lethal sections survivable. Most of R-Type's difficulty is really a positioning puzzle: *where does the Force need to be for this passage.*

**Attach it to the rear** when enemies come from behind. **Detach it into a corridor** you cannot safely enter. **Attach it to the front** as a battering ram.

---

## Weapons

Collected as coloured pods, each changing the Force's fire pattern:

- **Red** — a straight, powerful beam.
- **Blue** — reflecting shots that bounce off terrain. Best in enclosed sections.
- **Yellow** — shots that travel along floors and ceilings.

Plus **Bits**, small satellites that orbit above and below and block fire, and **Missiles** that add ground-tracking damage.

---

## Strategy

**Memorise, then execute.** R-Type is not a reaction game. Each stage has fixed enemy placements and the intended solution is usually a specific route and Force position. Dying while learning is normal.

**Never lose your loadout.** Death strips you back to a bare ship and respawns you mid-stage, which is frequently unrecoverable. Caution at high power beats aggression.

**Charge the Wave Cannon constantly.** Hold fire when nothing needs shooting — a charged shot is available the moment something does.

**Stage 3's boss** is the famous wall-of-a-ship. The Force detached into its underside is the intended answer.

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Master System core, and **rewind is enabled** — which matters more here than almost anywhere else in the library. Death strips your Force and weapons, and a late-stage death without them usually ends the run. Unwinding a few seconds preserves a loadout that took twenty minutes to build.

The ROM is `R-Type (World)` — one image for all regions, so it is the correct North American file.
