# Psycho Fox (Master System)

**Developer:** Vic Tokai | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1989

---

## Introduction

Psycho Fox is a bright, strange platformer built on the same engine as Vic Tokai's *Kid Kool*, and it has one mechanic that makes it worth playing: **you can change species, and each animal handles differently.**

You start as the fox and can become a hippo, a monkey or a tiger. These are not cosmetic — they change jump height, weight, and speed, and several routes only work as a specific animal.

It is also unusually open for a 1989 platformer. Levels have multiple paths, hidden areas above and below the obvious route, and a fair amount rewarded for exploring rather than running right.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move, duck.
- **Button 1:** Jump.
- **Button 2:** Punch. Also throws the Birdfly if you have one.

---

## The four animals

| Animal | Handling |
|---|---|
| **Fox** | Balanced. The default and a good all-rounder. |
| **Hippo** | Heavy. Poor jump, but **smashes through blocks** the others cannot. |
| **Monkey** | **Highest jump**, light. Reaches places nothing else can. |
| **Tiger** | **Fastest**, twitchy to control. |

You swap at totem poles found through the levels. **Weight matters**: the hippo falls through weak floors that hold the monkey, and the monkey clears gaps the hippo cannot.

---

## The Birdfly

A small bird companion you carry and **throw as a projectile**. It flies forward, damages what it hits, and returns.

It is also your **extra life buffer** — taking a hit while carrying the Birdfly loses the bird instead of killing you. Losing the bird then leaves you one hit from death.

**Keep the Birdfly.** Recovering it is possible but awkward, and playing without it is a different, harsher game.

---

## Structure

Seven areas of several stages each, with a boss at the end of every area. The visual variety is high — grasslands, ice, caves, industrial — and each area introduces a hazard type.

**Hidden bonus stages** are reached by finding concealed doors, and they hold extra lives and Birdflys. Exploring upward is usually rewarded.

---

## Strategy

**Switch animals deliberately.** A section that feels impossible is usually asking for a different body — the monkey for height, the hippo for breaking through.

**Guard the Birdfly.** It is a free hit and a ranged attack in one.

**Explore up.** The main route is the lowest and least rewarding path through most stages.

**Learn the weight differences.** Weak floors, breakable blocks and long gaps all respond differently per animal, and the game rarely signposts which one it wants.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Master System core with **rewind enabled**, useful for the pit deaths that come with a game this vertical.

The ROM is `Psycho Fox (USA, Europe, Brazil)`. A spiritual successor, *Magical Hat no Buttobi Turbo! Daibouken*, exists on Japanese hardware and is essentially the same engine with different art.
