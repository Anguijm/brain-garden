# Shinobi (Master System)

**Developer:** Sega | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1988

---

## Introduction

This is a conversion of the 1987 arcade Shinobi — and unlike *The GG Shinobi* on Game Gear, which was an original design, this one keeps the arcade structure: rescue the hostages, reach the boss, repeat.

Joe Musashi throws shuriken at range and strikes with a sword up close, and the game is built around a two-plane layout: enemies occupy a foreground and a background row, and you switch between them.

It is a competent port of a genuinely good arcade game, and considerably more forgiving than the coin-op original.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move, duck. **Up** to jump to the upper plane, **Down** to drop to the lower.
- **Button 1:** Jump.
- **Button 2:** Attack — shuriken at range, sword in close.
- **Both buttons:** Ninja magic (once per stage).

---

## The two-plane layout

Most stages have a **foreground and background walkway**. Enemies and hostages occupy both, and your attacks only reach the plane you are standing on.

Switching planes is how you reach hostages and how you avoid fire — an enemy shooting along the lower plane cannot hit you on the upper. New players fight everything on one level and find the game crowded; the design expects constant switching.

---

## Hostages

**Every hostage in a stage must be freed** before the exit opens. They are small figures chained in place, freed by touching them, and some are deliberately tucked on the plane you are not using.

Freeing all of them also grants a weapon upgrade for the rest of the stage — from single shuriken to a spread, and eventually to a gun.

**A missed hostage means a wasted stage**, because you cannot progress. If the exit will not open, you walked past someone.

---

## Ninja magic

Once per stage, both buttons together triggers a screen-clearing technique. It is a panic button and it does not carry over — using it is always better than dying with it unused.

---

## Bosses and bonus stages

Each mission ends with a boss with a readable pattern. Between missions there are first-person **bonus stages** where you throw shuriken at ninja leaping toward the screen; clearing them grants an extra life.

---

## Strategy

**Free every hostage before hunting the exit.** Sweep both planes.

**Use the plane switch defensively.** Dropping a level to dodge a volley is faster and safer than jumping.

**Spend the magic.** It resets each stage.

**Range beats melee.** The sword only appears at close quarters, and close quarters is where you take damage. Keep shuriken distance.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Master System core with **rewind enabled**, useful for the one-hit hazards.

Worth not confusing with the Game Gear *Shinobi* also in this library — that one is *The GG Shinobi*, an original five-ninja design, and a different game entirely despite the shared name.

The ROM is `Shinobi (USA, Europe, Brazil) (Rev 1)`; Rev 1 is the later revision and the one to have.
