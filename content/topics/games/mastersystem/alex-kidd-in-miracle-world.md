# Alex Kidd in Miracle World (Master System)

**Developer:** Sega | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1986

---

## Introduction

Alex Kidd was Sega's mascot before Sonic, and in Europe and Brazil this game was built into the console — millions of people's first Master System experience started here whether they owned a cartridge or not.

It is a platformer with two unusual ideas. Alex attacks with his **fists** rather than by jumping on enemies, which means combat is a punch with reach rather than a stomp. And boss fights are **janken** — rock, paper, scissors — against opponents who telegraph their choice if you know where to look.

It is short, colourful, and considerably harder than it appears.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move, duck.
- **Button 1:** Jump.
- **Button 2:** Punch. Alex's fist has real reach and destroys most blocks.
- **Down + Button 1:** Enter doors and some passages.

---

## Punching blocks

Almost every block in the game is destructible, and many contain money, items or 1-ups. Punching your way through scenery is normal play, not a secret — clearing a path upward through a wall of blocks is often the intended route.

**Money buys vehicles and items** from shops: the Motorcycle (fast, fragile), the Pedicopter (flight, invaluable), and power-ups.

---

## The janken bosses

Each boss is decided by rock-paper-scissors, best of three. Lose and you die instantly, regardless of health.

**They are not random.** Each opponent has a tell, and several repeat a fixed sequence. Losing a boss costs a life, so the practical approach is to learn the pattern across attempts — which is exactly the sort of trial-and-error that rewind removes.

The finger signs: **rock** is a closed fist, **paper** an open hand, **scissors** two fingers. Alex's opponents show theirs a fraction before the resolve.

---

## Level notes

Seventeen stages across Radaxian, ranging from open landscapes to underwater sections and vehicle stages.

**The underwater levels** change your movement entirely — Alex swims rather than jumps and cannot punch as effectively. They are the most disliked part of the game.

**Vehicle stages** use the Motorcycle or Pedicopter, and the Pedicopter sections are among the more forgiving parts of the game if you bought one.

**Instant death is common.** Spikes, falls and lost janken all kill outright rather than damaging you. Alex has no health bar — one hit is one life.

---

## Strategy

**One hit kills you.** That is the single most important thing to internalise. Alex has no health, so caution beats speed everywhere.

**Punch everything.** Money and 1-ups are hidden in ordinary-looking blocks, and you will need both.

**Buy the Pedicopter when you can.** It trivialises several stages.

**Learn the janken tells.** Losing a boss on a coin flip is the game's most frustrating failure, and it is avoidable.

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Master System core. **Rewind is enabled**, and this is one of the games it helps most: one-hit deaths plus rock-paper-scissors bosses make for a lot of lost progress that isn't teaching you anything.

The ROM is `Alex Kidd in Miracle World (World) (Sega Ages)` — one image for all regions, so it is the correct North American file. Note the built-in version that shipped in later European consoles differs slightly from the cartridge.
