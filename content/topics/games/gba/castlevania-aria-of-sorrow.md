# Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow (Game Boy Advance)

**Developer:** Konami | **Publisher:** Konami | **Year:** 2003

---

## Introduction

The best of the three Game Boy Advance Castlevanias, and the one most often named in the same breath as *Symphony of the Night*.

Set in **2035**, which no other Castlevania is. Soma Cruz is pulled into Dracula's castle during a solar eclipse and discovers he can absorb the powers of the things he kills.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move; down to crouch, up to interact.
- **A:** Jump. **B:** Attack. **R:** Backdash. **L:** Guard (with certain souls).
- **Start:** Menu. **Select:** Map.

---

## The Tactical Soul system

Kill an enemy and you may absorb its **soul**. There are around 110, and they are the whole progression system.

Souls come in three types you equip simultaneously:

| Type | Slot | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Bullet (red)** | Attack | A ranged or special attack, costs MP |
| **Guardian (blue)** | Held | A familiar, aura, or transformation held down |
| **Enchant (yellow)** | Passive | Permanent stat or ability change |

**Some souls are required to progress.** Traversal abilities — double jump, sliding, flight, water walking — are souls dropped by specific enemies, so the metroidvania keys are drop-based rather than chest-based.

**Drop rates are low for the interesting ones**, and this is the game's one design flaw. Grinding a specific enemy for a specific soul is a real part of playing it.

---

## The true ending

Beating the obvious final boss gives you a **bad ending**. The real one requires equipping a specific combination of three souls — Flame Demon, Giant Bat and Succubus — in the correct room near the end, which unlocks the Chaotic Realm and the actual conclusion.

**Nothing in the game tells you this.** It is worth knowing in advance rather than finishing and wondering why the story stopped mid-sentence.

---

## Weapons

Unusually for Castlevania, Soma uses swords, spears, axes, knuckles and katanas rather than a whip, and each class has its own attack arc and speed. **Weapon choice matters more than in any other game in the series** — a spear's straight thrust and a katana's wide arc suit completely different rooms.

---

## Strategy

**Absorb everything.** Even dull souls fill the collection, and the collection itself has rewards.

**Check the soul list against enemies you have already passed.** If a route is blocked, you probably need a soul from an enemy two areas back.

**Backdash constantly.** It is faster than walking and it is the main defensive tool.

**Look up the three-soul true ending requirement before the final area.** It is unguessable.

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Game Boy Advance core with **rewind enabled**, which takes some of the pain out of soul grinding — though rewind cannot re-roll a drop that has already been decided.

**Use the GBA colour-correction shader.** Castlevania's art is dark by design and looks flat and grey without it.
