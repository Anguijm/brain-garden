# Chrono Trigger (SNES)

**Developer:** Square | **Publisher:** Square | **Year:** 1995

---

## Introduction

Square assembled what the press called a "Dream Team" — Hironobu Sakaguchi of Final Fantasy, Yuji Horii of Dragon Quest, and Akira Toriyama drawing it — and the result is regularly voted the best role-playing game ever made.

It earns it mostly by respecting your time. There are **no random encounters**, enemies are visible on the field, and the whole game can be finished in around twenty hours.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move. **A:** Confirm/talk. **B:** Cancel/run.
- **X:** Menu. **Y:** Map/secondary.
- **L / R:** Cycle in menus.

---

## Combat

Turn order runs on the **Active Time Battle** system: each character has a gauge that fills, and when it is full they act. It does not pause while you decide, so hesitation costs you.

Enemies fight **where they stand on the field**, which means positioning matters — an area-effect attack hits whoever is clustered.

**Techs** are special abilities. The system's signature idea is that **two or three characters can combine techs**: Crono's lightning plus Lucca's fire makes something neither has alone. There are dozens of these combinations, and the game does not list them until you have the components.

---

## Time travel

You move between roughly 65,000,000 BC, 12,000 BC, 600 AD, 1000 AD, 2300 AD and the End of Time.

**Actions in one era change later ones.** A forest planted in the past exists in the present. A character helped in 600 AD leaves a descendant in 1000 AD. Much of the optional content is a chain across eras.

---

## New Game Plus, which this game invented

Finish it once and you can start again **keeping your levels, equipment and techs**, facing a fresh world at full strength.

This is not a bonus mode. **Chrono Trigger has thirteen endings**, and almost all of them require fighting the final boss at a point in the story where you would normally be far too weak. New Game Plus is how you do that — and the concept, which this game introduced, is now standard across the industry.

---

## Strategy

**Fight the final boss early on a New Game Plus run.** That is where the alternate endings live.

**Do the sidequests before the endgame.** Each character has a personal quest that grants their best weapon and matters to the ending.

**Experiment with tech combinations** whenever the party changes. New pairings unlock silently.

**Do not grind.** The game is tuned for a player who fights what is in the way and nothing more.
