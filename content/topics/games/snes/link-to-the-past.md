# The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past (SNES)

**Developer:** Nintendo EAD | **Publisher:** Nintendo | **Year:** 1992 (US)

---

## Introduction

The game that fixed the template. The original *Zelda* invented the formula and *Zelda II* went sideways; this is where the series settled into the shape every 2D Zelda since has used, and most 3D ones too.

Ganon is sealed in the Dark World. Agahnim is undoing the seal. Link has a uncle, a sword, and eventually two entire overlapping maps.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move.
- **A:** Context action — lift, read, talk, dash with boots.
- **B:** Sword.
- **X:** Map. **Y:** Selected item.
- **L / R:** Shield-relevant, and used by some items.

---

## Two worlds

Halfway in, the **Dark World** opens: a corrupted mirror of Hyrule occupying the same coordinates. A hill in one is a pit in the other; a lake in one is dry ground in the other.

The **Magic Mirror** returns you to the Light World from anywhere, and specific **warp tiles** send you the other way. The intended loop is: walk somewhere impossible in the Light World, cross to the Dark World where the terrain differs, walk past the obstacle, and mirror back.

**Most of the game's puzzles are geographic, not mechanical.** When something is unreachable, the answer is usually in the other world at the same spot.

---

## Items that are keys

- **Pegasus Boots** — dash, break cracked walls, cross gaps
- **Hookshot** — pull yourself across gaps and to enemies
- **Hammer** — flatten stakes, break certain rocks
- **Flippers** — swim
- **Titan's Mitt / Power Glove** — lift heavier rocks
- **Magic Mirror** — the world-switching tool the whole second half depends on
- **Cane of Somaria / Byrna** — create blocks, become invulnerable

---

## Structure

Three Light World dungeons, then seven Dark World dungeons, then Ganon's Tower. The Dark World dungeons can largely be done **in any order**, which is unusual for the series and worth using.

---

## Strategy

**Bomb and dash every suspicious wall.** Cracked walls look barely different from solid ones.

**Talk to everyone before Agahnim.** The Light World has a lot of content that becomes awkward to reach later.

**Collect heart pieces as you pass them.** Backtracking across two worlds late is tedious.

**Do the bottle side quests.** Four bottles exist and a fairy in a bottle is a free resurrection.

**Use the Magic Mirror constantly.** It is the fast-travel system as well as a puzzle tool.
