# EarthBound (SNES)

**Developer:** Ape / HAL Laboratory | **Publisher:** Nintendo | **Year:** 1995

---

## Introduction

A role-playing game set in a parody of suburban America, where you fight hippies, taxi cabs and piles of vomit with a baseball bat, and which turns without warning into one of the strangest and most affecting things Nintendo has published.

It sold badly. Nintendo's marketing campaign was built around scratch-and-sniff cards and the slogan "this game stinks", the box was oversized and expensive, and Americans in 1995 did not want a cartoon RPG about children. It became a cult object afterwards and the price of an original cartridge reflects that.

Known in Japan as *Mother 2*.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move. **A:** Confirm/talk/interact. **B:** Cancel/dash.
- **X:** Menu. **Y:** Quick status. **L / R:** Cycle characters.

---

## The rolling HP meter

Damage does not apply instantly. Your health **rolls down like an odometer**, and you keep acting while it falls.

This means a fatal blow is survivable if you heal, or win the fight, before the counter reaches zero. It is a genuinely brilliant mechanic that turns near-death into a race, and no other game has meaningfully copied it.

---

## Systems worth knowing

**You cannot see enemy health.** There is no HP bar for the opposition. You judge fights by feel.

**Weak enemies die instantly.** Walk into an enemy far below your level and the battle resolves automatically with no fight — an elegant anti-grinding measure.

**Money arrives by ATM.** Your father deposits your earnings and you withdraw from cash machines. He also saves your game, by telephone.

**Homesickness.** Ness can become homesick and start missing turns until you call your mother.

**Inventory is small and items are bulky.** Managing it is a real constraint, and Escargo Express storage exists for a reason.

---

## Structure

Eight "Your Sanctuary" locations to visit, gathering melodies, across a road trip through Onett, Twoson, Threed, Fourside and beyond. The tone drifts steadily from suburban comedy toward something much darker, and the final area is genuinely unsettling.

---

## Strategy

**Buy and carry healing items generously.** The rolling HP meter only saves you if you have something to drink.

**Use the phone.** Saving costs nothing and the game will let you walk a long way without one.

**Talk to everyone.** Directions are conversational and the writing is the reason to play.

**Do not sell equipment you might re-equip.** Inventory pressure tempts you into it.

**Keep the Escargo Express number handy** for storing quest items.

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's SNES core with **rewind enabled**.

The original cartridge included an anti-piracy system that, if triggered, increased enemy encounters to unplayable levels and then **deleted your save at the final boss**. Properly dumped ROMs like the one on this box do not trigger it.

A sequel, *Mother 3*, released only in Japan on Game Boy Advance and is playable in English through a well-regarded fan translation.
