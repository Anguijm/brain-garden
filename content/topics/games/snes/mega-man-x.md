# Mega Man X (SNES)

**Developer:** Capcom | **Publisher:** Capcom | **Year:** 1994

---

## Introduction

Capcom rebuilt Mega Man for the Super Nintendo and, in doing so, made the best game in the whole franchise. X can **dash**, **cling to and climb walls**, and take upgrades that permanently change what he is capable of.

It is also the game people point to when explaining wordless tutorial design: the opening stage teaches every mechanic, punishes you for not learning them, and ends with a boss that beats you no matter what — an intentional loss that sets up the plot.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move; down to look down.
- **B:** Jump. **Y:** Fire — **hold to charge**.
- **A / R:** Dash. **L / R:** Cycle weapons.
- **Against a wall in mid-air:** X slides down; jump off it to climb.

**Wall jumping is not optional.** Several upgrades and one whole stage require chaining wall jumps upward, and it is the fastest way to move anywhere.

---

## The upgrades, and why order matters

Four **armour parts** are hidden in stages, each in a capsule:

| Part | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Legs (Chill Penguin)** | The **dash** — get this first, always |
| **Helmet (Sting Chameleon)** | Headbutt through certain ceiling blocks |
| **Body (Sting Chameleon)** | Halves damage taken |
| **Arm (Flame Mammoth)** | Stronger charged shot |

Plus **heart tanks** (permanent health) and **sub-tanks** (stored refills).

**The Legs upgrade in Chill Penguin's stage should be the first thing you get.** It is reachable immediately, and everything in the game is easier with a dash.

---

## The boss weakness chain

Each of the eight Mavericks is weak to another's weapon, and beating them in the right order makes the game dramatically easier:

**Chill Penguin → Spark Mandrill → Armored Armadillo → Launch Octopus → Boomer Kuwanger → Sting Chameleon → Storm Eagle → Flame Mammoth**

There is a second effect people miss: **beating certain bosses changes other stages.** Kill Chill Penguin and Flame Mammoth's lava freezes. Kill Storm Eagle and Spark Mandrill's stage loses power. The order rewrites the levels, not just the difficulty.

---

## The hidden Hadouken

There is a **Street Fighter fireball** in this game. It requires all heart tanks, all sub-tanks and all armour, then a specific repeated-death ritual in Armored Armadillo's stage. It one-shots almost everything, including the final boss.

---

## Strategy

**Legs first, from Chill Penguin.**

**Follow the weakness chain** unless you want the harder game deliberately.

**Revisit stages after each boss.** Some upgrades only become reachable once a stage has changed.

**Charge the special weapons.** Charged Maverick weapons behave completely differently from their uncharged form, and most players never try it.
