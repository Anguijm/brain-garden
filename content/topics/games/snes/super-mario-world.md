# Super Mario World (SNES)

**Developer:** Nintendo EAD | **Publisher:** Nintendo | **Year:** 1991

---

## Introduction

The Super Nintendo's pack-in game, and the one that had to prove the new hardware was worth buying after [Super Mario Bros. 3](topics/games/nes/super-mario-bros-3) had apparently perfected the formula on the old one.

It introduced **Yoshi**, the spin jump, the cape, and a world map with branching paths and secret exits that is still the best in the series.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move; down to duck and to enter pipes.
- **B:** Jump. **A:** **Spin jump** — bounces off enemies that would otherwise hurt you, and breaks blocks below.
- **Y / X:** Run, and pick up / throw.
- **L / R:** Scroll the screen sideways to look ahead.

**The spin jump is the most under-used move.** It kills spiked enemies safely, breaks turn blocks from above, and is the answer to several stages people find unfair.

---

## The cape

The cape feather is the best power-up Mario has ever had. Run to full speed, jump, and you glide; time the D-pad correctly and you climb, indefinitely.

**A skilled cape flight breaks most levels open.** It is also the tool behind many of the secret exits.

---

## Yoshi

Yoshi eats enemies and gains abilities from shell colour:

| Shell | Yoshi gains |
|---|---|
| **Red** | Spits three fireballs |
| **Yellow** | Ground-pound dust clouds that stun |
| **Blue** | **Wings — flight** |

Holding a blue shell in Yoshi's mouth grants flight for as long as you hold it, which trivialises several stages.

---

## The map, and secret exits

96 exits exist across 72 levels. Many stages have **two exits**: the normal one, and a hidden keyhole reached with a key. Secret exits open red paths on the map to whole zones.

**The Star Road** is a network of warp points reached through secret exits, and it leads to the **Special World** — eight brutal levels which, once cleared, permanently change the game's palette and turn Koopas into Mario masks.

---

## Strategy

**Look for keys.** A key and keyhole in a level means a branch on the map you have not seen.

**Bank power-ups in the reserve box.** The item at the top of the screen drops when you take damage; keeping a cape or mushroom there is a free extra life.

**Learn the cape climb.** Rising indefinitely turns hard levels into short ones.

**Do not skip Star Road.** It is the fast-travel network and the gate to the Special World.

**The spin jump handles Muncher plants and spinies.** Most "impossible" moments are a normal jump used where a spin jump was intended.
