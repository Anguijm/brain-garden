# Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories (PSP)

**Developer:** Rockstar Leeds / Rockstar North | **Publisher:** Rockstar | **Year:** 2006

---

## Introduction

A full open-world Grand Theft Auto on a handheld, in 2006, running the whole of Vice City with traffic, radio stations and a physics-driven city. That it works at all is the achievement.

Set in **1984**, two years before *Vice City*, following Victor Vance — the brother of that game's Lance Vance. It is the last Grand Theft Auto built on the PlayStation 2 engine.

---

## Controls

- **Stick:** Move. **D-Pad:** Camera and weapon cycling.
- **X:** Accelerate / sprint. **Square:** Brake / jump. **O:** Fire. **Triangle:** Enter/exit vehicle.
- **L / R:** Lock-on target, look behind in vehicles.

**One stick again**, so the camera lives on the D-pad or auto-follows. **Auto-aim carries the combat** and is on by default for good reason.

---

## What is new here

**Empire building.** You take over businesses from rival gangs, choose what each one does — protection, drugs, loan sharking, smuggling — and defend them from attacks. Each generates income and each has its own missions.

**This is the closest a Grand Theft Auto had come to a management layer** at that point, and it is the main reason to play this over the other portable entries.

**Swimming**, which the original *Vice City* did not have. Falling in water no longer kills you.

**Helicopters and better flight**, with the whole city usable vertically.

---

## Structure

Standard Grand Theft Auto: story missions from characters around the map, plus side activities — vigilante, paramedic, taxi, races, and unique jumps.

**The empire missions are effectively an optional second campaign** and are where the money comes from.

---

## Strategy

**Take empire sites early.** Income compounds, and the story missions get much easier when you can afford good weapons and body armour.

**Defend your businesses.** Losing one to a rival means retaking it.

**Use auto-aim.** Manual aiming on one stick is a disadvantage, not a skill.

**Save often.** Mission failures send you back a long way.

**Buy armour before every story mission.** It is cheap and it is the difference in most firefights.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs well on Batocera's PPSSPP core — this is one of the more demanding PSP games, and the UM790Pro handles it comfortably.

**Raising internal resolution helps a great deal**, though the draw distance and texture work are what they are. 2x or 3x is the sweet spot; beyond that you are sharpening low-resolution textures.

Both PSP Grand Theft Auto games were later ported to PS2; the PSP versions are what run here.
