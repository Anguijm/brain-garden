# Valkyria Chronicles II (PSP)

**Developer:** Sega WOW | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 2010

---

## Introduction

The sequel to the PlayStation 3 *Valkyria Chronicles*, moved to a handheld — a decision that disappointed everyone who loved the first game and produced something genuinely good anyway.

Its combat system, **BLiTZ**, is unlike anything else in the genre: you select a unit from a top-down tactical map, then **control it directly in third person in real time** while enemies shoot at you, spending a shared pool of command points.

Set two years after the first game, at a military academy, which shifts the tone from war drama toward school story. That shift is the main complaint.

---

## Controls

- **Tactical map:** D-pad to select units, X to deploy into them.
- **In unit:** Stick to move (drains an action gauge), **Square to aim**, X to fire.
- **O:** End that unit's action. **Triangle:** Special orders.

---

## How a turn works

Each turn you have **Command Points**. Spending one lets you take control of a unit and act with it. You can spend several CP on the same unit, acting repeatedly — which is the core tactical decision.

While you move, **the enemy shoots at you in real time**. Movement is not free and standing in the open is fatal.

Aiming pauses the action, but firing gives the enemy a chance to react.

**Cover, flanking and suppression all matter mechanically**, which is unusual for a turn-based game.

---

## Classes

Scout (fast, weak), Shocktrooper (durable, close-range), Lancer (anti-tank), Engineer (repairs and ammunition), Sniper, Armored Tech, Gunner.

**Scouts are the strongest class in the first game and remain very strong here**, because movement range is the most valuable resource. A scout can cross most of a map on one CP.

**Classes branch** into advanced specialisations as they level, and the branching is permanent per unit.

---

## What the PSP version changed

**Maps are smaller and split into sections** connected by gates, because the handheld could not render the first game's large continuous battlefields. This is the biggest structural loss.

**Far more missions**, and a class-and-recruitment system with much more depth than the original.

---

## Strategy

**Move in cover, not in the open.** Crouching in grass or behind sandbags changes hit rates dramatically.

**Use scouts to capture bases.** Capturing a base gives a new spawn point and often ends a mission.

**Do not spend all CP on one unit** unless you are finishing a mission this turn.

**Shoot from behind.** Rear shots do far more damage, as in the tactics games.

**Level classes evenly early**, then specialise.

## Emulation Notes

Runs well on Batocera's PPSSPP core. The cel-shaded "CANVAS" art style scales very cleanly, so **a higher internal resolution is particularly worthwhile here** — it looks close to its console sibling at 3x.

*Valkyria Chronicles III* was never released in English officially and remains PSP-only in Japan.
