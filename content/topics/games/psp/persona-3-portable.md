# Shin Megami Tensei: Persona 3 Portable (PSP)

**Developer:** Atlus | **Publisher:** Atlus | **Year:** 2010 (US)

---

## Introduction

The handheld version of *Persona 3*, and for many people the definitive one. It is the game that established the modern Persona formula: a school-life simulation by day, a dungeon crawl by night, and a calendar that runs whether you use your time well or not.

**The major addition is a female protagonist route**, which is not a reskin — it has its own social links, its own music, and a substantially different tone. It was exclusive to this version for over a decade.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad / stick:** Move. **X:** Confirm. **O:** Cancel.
- **L / R:** Cycle in menus and shift targets.
- **Triangle:** Persona menu.

---

## What the PSP version traded away

**The overworld is gone.** Instead of walking around the city in 3D, you move a cursor over a static map and select destinations. This is faster and less atmospheric, and it is the main argument for playing the PS2 version instead.

**Anime cutscenes are replaced** by static images with text.

**What it gained** is direct control of your party members in battle, which the original PS2 release did not allow — there, allies acted on AI "tactics" you could only steer loosely, and it is the most complained-about aspect of the original.

---

## The calendar

You have one year. Each day gives you limited time, and everything competes:

- **Social Links** — relationships that strengthen your Personas
- **Stats** — Academics, Charm, Courage, raised by activities
- **Tartarus** — the dungeon, where levelling happens
- **Rest** — because exploring makes you tired and tired characters fight badly

**You cannot do everything in one run.** The game is designed so a first playthrough leaves things unfinished, and New Game Plus is the intended answer.

---

## Combat and weaknesses

Turn-based, and built on one rule: **hit an enemy's elemental weakness and you get another turn**. Knock down all enemies and you get an All-Out Attack.

The same applies against you. **A single unblocked weakness can cascade into a party wipe** in two turns.

**Analyse everything.** Knowing an enemy's weakness is the difference between a trivial fight and a fatal one.

---

## Persona fusion

You collect Personas and **fuse them** into stronger ones, inheriting skills from the parents. Social Links grant experience bonuses to fusions of the matching arcana, which is how the school simulation and the dungeon connect mechanically.

---

## Tartarus

A single procedurally generated tower, hundreds of floors, opened one block at a time by story progress. It is repetitive by design and it is the most criticised part of the game.

**Do not leave Tartarus until the last week before a full moon.** Falling behind means facing a boss underlevelled with no time to fix it.

---

## Strategy

**Climb Tartarus steadily**, a bit each week, rather than in panicked bursts.

**Prioritise Social Links that match your strongest Personas.**

**Raise Courage early.** It gates several other links.

**Always know the weakness before committing.**

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs well on Batocera's PPSSPP core. Largely 2D presentation, so **modest internal resolution increases** are sufficient; the sprite work does not benefit from 4x the way a 3D game does.

*Persona 3 Reload* (2024) remakes the PS2 version with modern systems but, at release, did not include the female protagonist route this version introduced.
