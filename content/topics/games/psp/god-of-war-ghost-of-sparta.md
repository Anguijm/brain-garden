# God of War: Ghost of Sparta (PSP)

**Developer:** Ready at Dawn | **Publisher:** Sony | **Year:** 2010

---

## Introduction

The second God of War on PSP and the better of the two. Ready at Dawn had already proved with *Chains of Olympus* that the series could work on a handheld; here they pushed the hardware considerably further, and the result is frequently named among the best-looking PSP games ever made.

Set between *God of War* and *God of War II*, following Kratos's search for his brother Deimos.

---

## Controls

- **Stick:** Move. **Square / Triangle:** Fast and heavy attack with the Blades.
- **X:** Jump. **O:** Grab / context kill.
- **L:** Block. **R:** Item / secondary.
- **L + face buttons:** Magic.

**The PSP has one analogue stick and no right stick**, so the camera is fixed and the dodge is mapped to a shoulder-plus-stick combination rather than a second stick. It works, but it is the one place the handheld conversion shows.

---

## Combat

Combo-based. Light attacks build chains, heavy attacks break guards, and the grab is both a finisher and a health source.

**Two additions over the earlier games:**

**The Thera's Bane fire mechanic.** Charging the Blades with fire increases damage and changes some combos. It is the main new toy and it is worth using constantly rather than saving.

**The Arms of Sparta** — a spear and shield, giving a ranged throw and a genuine block that the Blades do not have.

---

## Quick-time events

Large enemies and bosses end with a button-prompt sequence. **Failing one usually does not kill you** but restarts the sequence, and on bosses it can cost significant health.

---

## Upgrades

Red orbs from kills and chests upgrade weapons and magic. **Upgrade the Blades first** — they are what you use for ninety percent of the game, and spreading orbs across everything leaves you weak everywhere.

Health and magic are raised by finding **Gorgon Eyes** and **Phoenix Feathers**, three of each per upgrade, hidden in optional chests.

---

## Strategy

**Grab whenever the prompt appears.** Context kills restore health and are faster than combos.

**Block, then counter.** Blind aggression against the later enemies loses.

**Explore for chests.** Health and magic upgrades are hidden and the game gets much harder without them.

**Use the fire charge liberally.** It refills.

## Emulation Notes

Runs very well on Batocera's PPSSPP core, and this is one of the strongest arguments for PSP emulation: **at 3x or 4x internal resolution it looks like a PlayStation 2 game**, which is roughly what it was aiming at.

Both PSP God of War games were later remastered for PS3; the PSP originals are what run here.
