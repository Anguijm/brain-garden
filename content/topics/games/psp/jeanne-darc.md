# Jeanne d'Arc (PSP)

**Developer:** Level-5 | **Publisher:** Sony | **Year:** 2007

---

## Introduction

Level-5's strategy RPG retelling the Hundred Years' War with demons in it. It is a fantasy version of Joan of Arc's story, and it is far better than that description suggests — it is generally considered the most **approachable** strategy RPG on the PSP, and a good entry point for anyone who found [Tactics Ogre](topics/games/psp/tactics-ogre-let-us-cling-together) or [Final Fantasy Tactics](topics/games/psp/final-fantasy-tactics-war-of-the-lions) impenetrable.

It sold poorly in the West and is the least-known of the three.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad / stick:** Move cursor. **X:** Select. **O:** Cancel.
- **L / R:** Rotate the battlefield.
- **Triangle:** Unit information.

---

## Burning Aura, which is the mechanic

Kill an enemy and the tile you are standing on lights up with a **Burning Aura** for one turn.

**Any ally who moves onto that tile gets an immediate extra turn.**

This chains. Kill with unit A, move unit B onto the aura, B acts again, kills something, creates another aura, move unit C onto it — and a well-planned turn can see your whole army act twice.

**This is what makes the game feel different from every other tactics game.** It rewards positioning and kill order rather than raw stats, and a clean chain is genuinely satisfying to construct.

---

## Armlets and transformation

Certain characters can **transform** using armlets, becoming much stronger for a limited number of turns. Transformed units also inflict a state that stuns nearby demons.

**Save transformations for the turn you plan to break the enemy line**, not for when you are in trouble.

---

## Skill stones

Abilities are **stones slotted into a grid** on each character. Stones can be moved between characters, and adjacent stones of matching types combine into stronger effects.

It is a flexible system that rewards planning but does not punish experimentation, because nothing is permanent.

---

## Difficulty

**Notably gentler than its peers.** No permadeath, generous checkpointing, and a difficulty curve that teaches rather than tests. This is a criticism from veterans and a virtue for everyone else.

---

## Strategy

**Plan the whole turn before moving anyone.** Burning Aura chains have to be sequenced.

**Kill with your weakest unit where possible**, so the strong ones can use the aura.

**Keep units adjacent.** Aura chains need someone nearby to step in.

**Slot skill stones for adjacency bonuses**, not just for the individual effects.

Runs well on Batocera's PPSSPP core. Cel-shaded 3D that scales cleanly, so a higher internal resolution is worth setting.

Never re-released on any platform, and physical copies are increasingly expensive.
