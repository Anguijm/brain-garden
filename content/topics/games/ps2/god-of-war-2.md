# God of War II (PlayStation 2)

**Developer:** SCE Santa Monica | **Publisher:** Sony | **Year:** 2007

---

## Introduction

Released in March 2007, five months **after** the PlayStation 3 had launched, and it is routinely called the best-looking game on the PlayStation 2. Santa Monica had spent years learning the hardware and spent all of that knowledge at once.

Kratos, now the God of War, is betrayed by Zeus and goes back in time to change his fate.

---

## Controls

- **Left stick:** Move. **Right stick:** **Evade roll**.
- **Square / Triangle:** Fast and heavy attack with the Blades of Athena.
- **X:** Jump. **Circle:** Grab / context kill.
- **L1:** Block / parry. **R1:** Interact. **L1 + face:** Magic.

---

## Combat

Combo-driven, with three things that separate competent play from button mashing:

**Parry.** Blocking at the moment of impact deflects and opens the attacker. It is not taught clearly and it trivialises several fights.

**Evade with the right stick.** Rolling is faster than blocking and cannot be broken by heavy attacks.

**Air juggling.** Launching an enemy and continuing the combo airborne keeps you safe from everything on the ground.

---

## Weapons and magic

The Blades are the mainstay. Secondary weapons — the Barbarian Hammer, the Spear of Destiny — have entirely different movesets and specific uses. Magic includes Typhon's Bane (ranged), Cronos' Rage (area) and Euryale's Head (petrification).

**Petrifying an enemy and then shattering them** is both efficient and the answer to several of the harder crowds.

---

## Upgrades

Red orbs from combat and chests. **Upgrade the Blades first and fully** — every other weapon is situational, and spreading orbs leaves you weak in the fights that matter.

Health and magic grow via **Gorgon Eyes** and **Phoenix Feathers**, hidden in optional chests, three per upgrade.

---

## Quick-time events

Large enemies and bosses end in button-prompt sequences. Failing costs health and restarts the sequence rather than killing you outright, in most cases.

---

## Strategy

**Learn to parry.** It is the largest single improvement available.

**Roll, do not block, against heavy enemies.**

**Grab whenever prompted.** Context kills restore health.

**Explore for chests.** The difficulty assumes you found most of the upgrades.

**Use the secondary weapons on crowds** — several have far better area coverage than the Blades.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs well on Batocera's PS2 core, and **at high internal resolution it is genuinely striking** — the art was built at the limit of the console and it scales beautifully.

[Ghost of Sparta](topics/games/psp/god-of-war-ghost-of-sparta) on PSP, also on this box, is the handheld entry from the same era and holds up next to it better than it has any right to.
