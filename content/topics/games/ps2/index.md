## The one that changed everything after it

**Resident Evil 4** invented the over-the-shoulder third-person shooter. Gears of War, Dead Space,
The Last of Us and most of what followed descend from it directly. It went through four discarded
versions first, one of which became *Devil May Cry*.

Note that the **PS2 version is the weakest of the original three ports** — converted down from the
GameCube original with reduced lighting and textures, because the PS2 had less graphics memory. It
gained the *Separate Ways* campaign in exchange.

## Two by the same designer

**Ico** and **Shadow of the Colossus** are both Fumito Ueda's, built on "subtractive design" —
removing everything that broke the atmosphere until almost nothing conventional was left. Ico has
no health bar and no music for most of its length; Shadow of the Colossus has sixteen enemies and
no others at all.

Shadow of the Colossus ran at around 20 frames per second on real hardware in its worst moments.
**Emulation holds a steadier frame rate than the console ever managed**, which is the clearest case
on this box for playing a game here rather than on the original machine.

## The most argued-about Final Fantasy

**Final Fantasy XII** lets you program your party's behaviour with if-then rules called Gambits, to
the point where well-written rules clear ordinary fights unattended. Whether that is the game
playing itself or the game's actual depth is a twenty-year argument. Know which side you are likely
to land on before starting it.

## Multi-disc

**Metal Gear Solid 3: Subsistence** is two discs, grouped into a single entry here by an `.m3u`
playlist with the individual discs hidden.

## One control nuance emulation cannot fully reproduce

The DualShock 2 had **pressure-sensitive face buttons**, and *Snake Eater* used them — releasing the
fire button gently lowers a gun without firing, and pushing the stick slightly crawls instead of
walking. Modern controllers have digital face buttons, so that nuance is approximated. It affects a
handful of moments and does not compromise the game.
