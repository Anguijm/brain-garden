# Kingdom Hearts II (PlayStation 2)

**Developer:** Square Enix | **Publisher:** Square Enix | **Year:** 2006

---

## Introduction

Disney characters and Final Fantasy characters in an action role-playing game, which sounds like a marketing exercise and is instead one of the best-feeling combat systems on the console.

*Kingdom Hearts II* fixed nearly everything people disliked about the first game — the camera, the platforming, the Gummi Ship sections — and produced combat fluid enough that people still play it for the mechanics alone.

---

## Controls

- **Left stick:** Move. **Right stick:** Camera (fixed from the first game, where it was on the shoulders).
- **X:** Attack / jump in menus. **Triangle:** **Reaction Command** — context-sensitive special.
- **Square:** Guard. **Circle:** Jump.
- **L1 + face:** Shortcut magic. **L2 / R2:** Cycle targets.

---

## The Triangle button

**Reaction Commands** are the signature. A prompt appears above the action and pressing Triangle does something spectacular and specific to the situation — riding a chandelier, reversing an enemy's attack, launching off a rival's shield.

They make combat cinematic without taking control away, and they are why the game feels the way it does.

---

## Drive Forms

Sora fuses with a companion to transform, each form changing his moveset entirely:

| Form | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Valor** | Two Keyblades, pure melee, no magic |
| **Wisdom** | Ranged magic, glide-shooting |
| **Master** | Balanced, best mobility |
| **Final** | Free-floating flight, extremely strong |
| **Anti-Form** | Uncontrolled, no magic, cannot be chosen |

**Forms level up separately**, and levelling them grants Sora permanent abilities — High Jump, Quick Run, Aerial Dodge, Glide — that carry over to normal play.

**Anti-Form triggers randomly** when you use forms too often. It cannot be levelled and it cannot be cancelled.

---

## Structure

Disney worlds visited in sequence, each with its own story, plus original locations. The **opening is famously long** — several hours as a different character, Roxas, before you play as Sora at all. It is the most common point at which people stop.

---

## Strategy

**Level Drive Forms deliberately.** The permanent movement abilities they grant are the real reward.

**Use Reaction Commands.** Ignoring Triangle makes fights far longer and harder.

**Set magic shortcuts** on L1. The menu is too slow mid-fight.

**Equip abilities, do not hoard AP.** Ability points do nothing unequipped.

**Push through the Roxas prologue.** It is worth it and it is the game's biggest structural flaw.

## Emulation Notes

Runs well on Batocera's PS2 core at higher internal resolution.

**This is the original North American release**, not *Final Mix*. Final Mix was Japan-only on PS2 and added bosses, abilities and difficulty options; it reached the West only in the HD collections years later. Everything here is the standard version.
