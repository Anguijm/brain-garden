# Metal Gear Solid 3: Subsistence (PlayStation 2)

**Developer:** Konami | **Publisher:** Konami | **Year:** 2006

---

## Introduction

*Snake Eater* set in 1964, in a Soviet jungle, and it is where the whole Metal Gear timeline actually begins — Naked Snake's mission here creates everything that follows.

**Subsistence is the expanded re-release**, and it is the version to have. The original *Snake Eater* used the series' traditional **fixed overhead camera**, which was workable in corridors and a serious handicap in open jungle. Subsistence added a **fully controllable third-person camera**, which fixed the game's one real design problem.

It also included the original *Metal Gear* and *Metal Gear 2* MSX games, and online multiplayer, now defunct.

---

## Controls

- **Left stick:** Move — **pressure-sensitive**, so how far you push determines crawl, walk or run.
- **Right stick:** Camera (the Subsistence addition).
- **Triangle:** Action / CQC. **Square:** Fire — **pressure-sensitive**, releasing gently lowers the gun without firing.
- **Circle:** Aim. **X:** Crouch / prone.

---

## Survival systems

Three meters, and they interact:

**Stamina** drains constantly and is refilled by **eating** — you hunt, trap and forage. Snakes, frogs, mushrooms, fish. Low stamina means shaking hands, worse aim, and audible stomach growling that alerts guards.

**Food rots.** Carried food spoils over time and eating spoiled food makes Snake vomit.

**Injuries** are treated individually in a **cure menu**: a bullet must be removed with a knife, then disinfected, stitched, bandaged. A broken bone needs a splint. Burns need ointment. **Untreated wounds do not heal.**

**Camouflage** is chosen manually — uniform and face paint — and a percentage indicator shows how well you match your surroundings. Changing camo as terrain changes is constant.

---

## CQC

Close-quarters combat: grab an enemy, then **interrogate, use as a human shield, slit their throat, or knock them out**. Interrogating yields information about the area.

---

## The End

There is a boss — an ancient sniper — fought across three jungle areas in a duel that can take an hour. **He can be killed before the fight by sniping him during a cutscene earlier.** He can also be defeated by **saving the game, quitting, waiting a week of real time, and returning to find he has died of old age.** Both are intentional.

---

## Strategy

**Change camouflage constantly.** The percentage is not decoration.

**Eat before you are starving**, and drop rotten food.

**Treat every injury immediately.**

**Use CQC rather than shooting.** Non-lethal play is rewarded and ammunition is scarce.

**Use the new camera.** If it feels like the old fixed view, the game is in the original *Snake Eater* mode.

## Emulation Notes

Runs on Batocera's PS2 core. **This is a two-disc release**, grouped into a single entry by an `.m3u` playlist here, with disc changes from the emulator's disc-control menu.

**The pressure-sensitive face buttons of the DualShock 2 are a real design element** — releasing Square gently to lower a gun without firing, or pushing the stick slightly to crawl. Modern controllers have digital face buttons, so that nuance is approximated rather than reproduced. It affects a small number of moments and the game remains fully playable.
