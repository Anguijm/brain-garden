# Final Fantasy Adventure (Game Boy)

**Developer:** Square | **Publisher:** Square | **Year:** 1991

---

## Introduction

Final Fantasy Adventure is not a Final Fantasy game. It is the **first Mana game** — known in Japan as *Seiken Densetsu* — and the direct ancestor of *Secret of Mana* on SNES. Square attached the Final Fantasy name for the Western release because the series had brand recognition and Mana did not.

Structurally it is Zelda with experience points: real-time overhead action, a sword, a world map, dungeons — but with levels, stats, equipment and a party member who follows you. It is more melancholy than Zelda too, with a story that kills characters and means it.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move.
- **A:** Attack. **Hold to charge** the power meter, release for a stronger strike.
- **B:** Use the equipped item or magic.
- **Select/Start:** Menu — equipment, magic, items.

### The charge meter

Attacking empties a small power bar that refills over a second or two. Swinging wildly does minimal damage; waiting for the bar to fill does full damage.

That is the combat system, and it is why button-mashing fails here. **Wait, strike, back off, wait.**

---

## Progression

**Levels come from kills**, and on level-up you choose which stat to raise — Power, Stamina, Wisdom or Will. That choice is permanent.

- **Power** — physical damage.
- **Stamina** — HP and defence.
- **Wisdom** — magic power.
- **Will** — MP.

A balanced Power/Stamina build carries most players; magic is useful but the game is winnable as a fighter.

**Weapons have uses beyond combat.** The axe breaks rocks, the sickle cuts bushes, the chain flail crosses gaps by grappling. Several are required for progress, so keep the full set rather than only the strongest.

---

## Companions

An NPC accompanies you for most of the game, fighting alongside you and occasionally required for a puzzle. They change as the story moves, and the changes are not always happy.

---

## Strategy

**Wait for the charge bar.** The single biggest difference between struggling and not.

**Keep every weapon.** The axe, sickle and flail are keys as much as weapons.

**Raise Stamina early.** Survivability compounds; damage does not help if you are dead.

**Save often.** The game has battery saves and a difficulty curve that spikes.

**Talk to everyone after story events.** Directions come from NPCs.

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Game Boy core with **rewind enabled**.

The ROM is `Final Fantasy Adventure (USA)`. Worth knowing the naming history if you go looking for more: this is *Seiken Densetsu: Final Fantasy Gaiden* in Japan, was re-released in Europe as *Mystic Quest* (unrelated to *Final Fantasy Mystic Quest* on SNES), and was remade for Game Boy Advance as *Sword of Mana* and again in 2021 as *Adventures of Mana*. Four names, one game.
