# Sonic the Hedgehog 2 (Mega Drive / Genesis)

**Developer:** Sega Technical Institute / Sonic Team | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1992

---

## Introduction

Sega's answer to Mario, on its second and best attempt. *Sonic 2* released worldwide on a single coordinated date Sega called "Sonic 2sday" — 24 November 1992 — which was a genuinely novel marketing idea at the time and is now how every large game launches.

It introduced **Tails**, the **spin dash**, and the half-pipe special stages.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move; **down to roll** while moving.
- **A / B / C:** Jump (all three).
- **Down + jump, released:** **Spin dash** — the addition that fixed the first game.

### The spin dash

Hold down, tap jump repeatedly to rev, release. Sonic launches at full speed from standstill.

**The first Sonic game had no way to accelerate from a stop except walking.** Every steep slope you failed to climb meant backing up and running at it. The spin dash removes that entirely and it is why *Sonic 2* plays better than its predecessor by a wide margin.

---

## Momentum, which people fight

Sonic is not a precision platformer and playing him like one is why some people never get on with the series. Rolling down a slope builds speed; running uphill loses it; jumping preserves whatever you had.

**Holding right constantly is the beginner mistake.** The levels are built to be rolled through, with jumps used sparingly.

---

## Rings, and the only rule that matters

Rings are your health. **One hit with rings scatters them and you survive; one hit with zero rings kills you.** You can retrieve some of the scattered rings in the moment before they vanish.

A single ring is as good as fifty for survival purposes, which means grabbing one ring in a dangerous section is worth a detour.

---

## Chaos Emeralds and Super Sonic

Enter a **star post with 50 rings** and jump into the ring above it to reach a special stage — a half-pipe where you collect rings while dodging bombs.

Collect all **seven Chaos Emeralds**, then hold 50 rings and jump: Sonic turns gold, becomes invincible, and runs faster. **Super Sonic is the reward for the special stages** and it is also required for the good ending.

---

## Tails

Player two controls Tails, who is invincible and cannot lose the game — an unusually thoughtful design for a younger sibling. In single-player he follows the computer's own logic and is mostly harmless.

---

## Strategy

**Spin dash everywhere.** It is faster than running and it hurts enemies.

**Grab one ring before entering anything dangerous.**

**Learn the special stage rhythm early.** The Emeralds get harder to obtain later as the ring requirements climb.

**Roll down slopes; do not jump down them.** Momentum is preserved by rolling and lost by jumping.

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Mega Drive core with **rewind enabled**, which is genuinely useful in the special stages — a single bomb late in a run costs the whole attempt.

The famously cut **Hidden Palace Zone** exists in the ROM's data and is reachable with cheat devices; the 2013 mobile remaster restored it properly.
