# Road Rash II (Mega Drive / Genesis)

**Developer:** Electronic Arts | **Publisher:** Electronic Arts | **Year:** 1992

---

## Introduction

Illegal motorcycle racing where you punch the other riders. *Road Rash II* is the refinement of the first game: more tracks, better handling, a **chain weapon**, and split-screen two-player, which the original lacked.

The formula is simple and it works: finish in the top three to earn money, buy a faster bike, race a harder circuit.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Steer; up to accelerate, down to brake.
- **B:** Accelerate. **A:** Brake. **C:** **Attack** — punch, or swing whatever you are holding.
- **Direction + C:** Attack left or right.
- **Up on the D-pad while riding:** Wheelie (on some configurations).

---

## Combat

You throw punches at riders beside you. Land enough and they crash.

**The chain is the addition that defines this game.** Some racers carry one, and **you take it by beating them while they hold it** — you cannot buy it. A chain has longer reach and knocks riders down far faster than fists.

**Police carry clubs and will target you.** Getting caught by a cop ends the race, and they specifically chase whoever is riding most aggressively.

---

## The economy

Finishing well pays. Crashing costs — repairs come out of your winnings, and running out of money ends your career.

Bikes come in three classes:

| Class | Character |
|---|---|
| **Rat bikes** | Cheap, slow, forgiving |
| **Sport bikes** | Fast, twitchy, poor at low speed |
| **Super bikes** | Very fast, expensive, unforgiving |

**Buy the best bike you can afford as early as possible.** The difficulty scales with the circuit, not with your bike, so an underpowered machine gets steadily further behind.

---

## Crashing

Hit a cow, a tree, an oncoming car or the ground and you are thrown off. **You then have to run back to your bike**, on foot, while the race continues. This is the real cost of a crash and it is usually unrecoverable in a close race.

---

## Strategy

**Take a chain from someone in the first race you can.** It changes every subsequent race.

**Attack riders on the inside of corners.** They have nowhere to recover.

**Brake for traffic, do not swerve.** Swerving at speed into oncoming cars is the most common crash.

**Watch for the cop siren** and stop fighting until he passes.

**Do not upgrade to a super bike too early.** The handling will cost you more races than the speed wins.

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Mega Drive core with **rewind enabled**, which is useful given how much a single crash costs.

The split-screen two-player mode is the one thing this game has that the original did not, and it is worth a second controller.
