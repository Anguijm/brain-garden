# Comix Zone (Mega Drive / Genesis)

**Developer:** Sega Technical Institute | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1995

---

## Introduction

A brawler that takes place **inside a comic book**. Sketch Turner, a comic artist, is pulled into his own pages, and the game is drawn as panels on a page — you fight through one panel, then physically tear or climb through the gutter into the next.

The presentation is the reason it is remembered. It is also, unusually for a Mega Drive game, genuinely difficult in a way that has kept most people from finishing it.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move; down to crouch.
- **A:** Use item / switch item.
- **B:** Attack — a full combo string, plus directional variations.
- **C:** Jump.
- **Down + B:** Sweep. **Up + B:** Uppercut. **B in air:** Flying kick.
- **A + direction on scenery:** Tear the paper, break objects, find items.

---

## The health problem

**Sketch loses health when he breaks scenery.** Punching a wall, ripping a panel or smashing a barrel costs you, because you are tearing the paper he is standing on.

This is a deliberate and unusual design: **destruction is how you find items and open routes, and it is also how you die.** Every optional smash is a bet.

**There are no health pickups worth speaking of.** A whole playthrough runs on one health bar with tiny top-ups, which is why the game has a reputation for being brutal.

---

## Roadkill the rat

You carry a rat that can be sent into small gaps to fetch items and trip switches. He is also your only real source of healing, occasionally.

---

## Items

Knives, bombs, and energy drinks, held in a small inventory. **Save the energy drinks.** The temptation is to use one at half health; the game's difficulty spikes late and an unused drink at the final fight is worth more than a comfortable middle.

---

## Two paths

The game has **branching routes** and multiple endings, and it is short — around an hour if you know it. The branching is the intended reason to replay, and there is a "true" ending requiring specific actions.

---

## Strategy

**Do not smash scenery you do not need.** Every break costs health and health is the whole difficulty.

**Learn the combo string.** B repeated does a fixed sequence; interrupting it wastes damage.

**Use the rat for anything in a gap.**

**Block by ducking.** There is no block button; crouching avoids high attacks.

**Ration energy drinks to the final stage.**

---

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Mega Drive core with **rewind enabled** — which for this game is close to essential unless you specifically want the original difficulty. There is no save system, it is a single-sitting game, and the health economy is punishing.

The art was hand-drawn and digitised, and it is one of the best-looking games on the hardware.
