# Gunstar Heroes (Mega Drive / Genesis)

**Developer:** Treasure | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1993

---

## Introduction

Treasure's first game, made by ex-Konami staff who left because Konami would not let them make anything but sequels. What they made instead is the most technically extravagant thing on the Mega Drive and one of the best action games of the era.

The sprite count, the scaling, the multi-jointed bosses — the hardware is being pushed past what Sega's own studios were getting out of it.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move; down to slide.
- **B:** Shoot. **C:** Jump. **A:** Grab / throw.
- **Hold shoot + direction:** Fire while stationary, aiming freely.

**Two firing styles are chosen at the start:** *Fixed*, where you plant your feet and aim in any direction, or *Free*, where you shoot while moving but lock your aim to your facing.

**Fixed is more powerful; Free is more forgiving.** Most players should start on Free and switch once the bosses start demanding precision.

---

## The weapon combination system

Four weapon types drop through the game — **Force, Lightning, Chaser, Fire** — and you carry **two at once**. Carrying two combines them into one of ten distinct weapons:

| Combination | Result |
|---|---|
| Chaser + Chaser | Homing shots, weak but hands-free |
| Fire + Fire | Flamethrower, huge close damage |
| Lightning + Lightning | Piercing beam |
| Force + Lightning | The **Lightning Sword**, generally considered the best |
| Fire + Chaser | Homing flames that circle you |
| Force + Fire | Long-range flame lance |

**Picking up a weapon replaces one you are holding**, so combinations are lost by accident constantly. Knowing which pair you want before you grab something is most of the strategy.

---

## Melee

You can **grab enemies and throw them**, slide into them, and suplex them. Treasure built a full close-range moveset into a run-and-gun, and against certain enemies it is faster than shooting.

---

## Structure

Seven stages selected in any order at first, each with an entirely different gimmick — a mine cart, a board game, a vertical shooter section, a fight on top of a moving vehicle. The **board game stage** is a dice-rolling minigame that turns into fights and is unlike anything else on the system.

Bosses are the point. Every one is a set piece with several phases and visible mechanical joints.

---

## Strategy

**Choose your weapon pair deliberately.** Lightning Sword (Force + Lightning) handles most of the game.

**Do not grab a random weapon drop mid-fight.** It will break your combination.

**Use melee on the small enemies.** Throwing is faster than shooting and conserves nothing you need.

**Play co-op if you can.** It was built for two players and the second player changes the pacing.
