# Streets of Rage 2 (Mega Drive / Genesis)

**Developer:** Sega / Ancient | **Publisher:** Sega | **Year:** 1992

---

## Introduction

The best side-scrolling brawler on any 16-bit console, and one of the few games from the era where the soundtrack is as famous as the game. Yuzo Koshiro and Motohiro Kawashima built a Mega Drive sound driver that produced club music the hardware should not have been able to make.

Mr. X is back, Adam has been kidnapped, and four fighters walk from the beach to the syndicate's compound hitting everything in between.

---

## Controls

- **D-Pad:** Move (eight directions, with depth).
- **B:** Attack.
- **C:** Jump.
- **A:** **Special** — clears space around you, but costs health.
- **B + C together:** Back attack.
- **Forward, forward + B:** Running attack (Axel's is a charging punch, Skate's a rolling tackle).

---

## The four fighters

| Character | Speed | Power | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Axel** | Medium | High | The balanced pick; his Grand Upper is one of the best moves in the genre |
| **Blaze** | Fast | Medium | Best reach on her jump kick, good crowd control |
| **Max** | Slow | Very high | Wrestler, grapples do enormous damage, cannot escape a crowd |
| **Skate** | Very fast | Low | Rolling attacks, best for hit-and-run, weakest per hit |

**Axel is the recommended start.** Max is the most fun once you understand that a grab is safer than a punch.

---

## The special-move economy

The A button clears enemies around you but **takes your own health**. That trade is the game's core decision: it is almost always correct when you are surrounded, and almost always wrong when you are not.

**Health lost to a special is not lost if you kill everything before taking damage**, because clearing a screen fast preserves more health than a slow careful fight. New players hoard the special and get cornered; experienced players spend it constantly.

---

## Grabs and throws

Walk into an enemy and you grab them. From there:

- **Attack** repeatedly for a combo
- **Away + attack** to throw them behind you
- **Toward + attack** for a different throw
- **Jump while holding** for a piledriver

**Throwing an enemy into other enemies damages all of them.** This is the crowd-control tool that does not cost health.

---

## Weapons and food

Pipes, knives, katanas and grenades are picked up and have limited durability. Food is hidden in **breakable scenery** — smash every barrel, phone booth, oil drum and sign.

---

## Strategy

**Break everything.** Health hides in scenery and you will need it.

**Fight along the vertical axis.** Line yourself up with one enemy at a time rather than standing in the middle of a group.

**Throw enemies into each other** instead of punching through them.

**Use the special before you are cornered, not after.**

## Emulation Notes

Runs perfectly on Batocera's Mega Drive core with **rewind enabled**.

The soundtrack is the reason to have decent audio attached to this machine. The volume is set to 100% on this box, which is worth knowing if the sound bar is turned up from previous sessions.

Two-player co-op is the intended way to play it and works with a second controller paired.
