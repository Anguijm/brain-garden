# NewHome

A house remodel in Japan, tracked here from demo to move-in and beyond. This page is
the hub: where things stand right now, what's being worked, and what waits. The
detail lives in three companion notes:

- **[The plan](projects/newhome/plan)**: the whole house, area by area, with what's
  done, what's in progress, and what each area still needs.
- **[Decisions](projects/newhome/decisions)**: every call made, dated, with the
  reasoning, so nobody has to remember in six months why an option was rejected.
- **[Ideas](projects/newhome/ideas)**: the parking lot. Anything worth considering
  lands here with a date and a status before it earns a place in the plan.

Research on how to do a thing (flooring over tatami, shoji repair, service upgrades)
gets the vault's normal treatment: staged sources, labeled claims, one note per
question, filed under this project as it's produced.

All state below is as reported by the operator, 2026-09-19.

## Now (before move-in)

- Kitchen: paint and new floor, then install the ordered counters, stove, and IKEA pieces.
- Middle living area (the old tatami room, mats already out): new floor, paint, finish out.
- Small bedroom 1: finish the flooring edge, install trim, refinish the closet doors, clean the glass.
- Master bedroom (traditional Japanese, two connected spaces): finish painting, refresh the shoji, lay the already-ordered flooring over the tatami.
- Small bedroom 2: paint the ceiling; the wood paneling stays.
- TV living area: take down the small wall (confirm it is not load-bearing first); otherwise left as is.

## Safety flags

- The balcony over the yard is gone, so the doors out of the two small bedrooms open
  onto a drop. Until a deck or balcony is built, those doors need to be secured shut.
- The stairs are steep and dark. Better lighting is on the list; treat it as a safety
  item, not a nicety.

## Waiting

- Delivery of the kitchen counters, stove, and IKEA order.

## Later (after move-in)

- Redo both water closets, upstairs and downstairs.
- Build a wood deck or balcony where the old balcony was.
- Take down part of the front wall for a parking space at the front of the yard.
- Request a service upgrade from 30-amp to 50-amp.
- Replace or refinish the roof.
- Paint the whole house exterior.
- Stair lighting.
