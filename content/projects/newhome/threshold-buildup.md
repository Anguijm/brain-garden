# Building the floor up to the threshold

The question, from the operator on site 2026-09-19 with tape-measure photos: the
main floor sits about 2 cm below the threshold, and the new hardwood flooring (the
same product as upstairs) should finish flush with it. What goes underneath, and
how is it built? The operator's first idea was thin battens on roughly 30 cm
spacing, thin plywood over them, flooring on top.

## The answer in one line

Assessment: for a raise of about 2 cm over a sound existing floor, skip the
battens; glue and screw structural plywood of the right thickness straight down
and lay the flooring on it. Battens are the tool for raises of 4 cm and up, or
for leveling a sagging floor.

## The flooring, identified

The operator named the product on 2026-09-19: TOMOYASU 天然木フロアタイル KINO
(キーノ), the くり colorway, C-F-3, one case of 21 pieces. FACT, from
[the seller's KINO product page](https://www.a-yuka.com/floortile/kino.html)
(Style Dart / a-yuka.com, read 2026-09-19): the tile is **12 mm thick** (a 2 mm
oak wear surface on a plywood core), 125 mm wide in random lengths up to
1200 mm; one case holds 11 full-length pieces plus random lengths equal to 5
more and covers 2.4 square meters; it locks with the patented Valinge 5G/FD
click system plus the maker's own short-edge lock, lays floating with no
adhesive or nails, can be pulled up and reused, and is rated for floor heating.
The three colorways (くるみ, しらかば, くり) are all oak on the surface; くり
names the color, not the wood.

Two consequences for this job. The 12 mm assumption in the table below is
confirmed, so the table stands. And this is a floating click floor, so the
flooring layer gets no glue and no staples; the plywood below it is still glued
and screwed as written.

## Measure the gap with the flooring itself

Lay a KINO piece flat on the existing floor, pushed against the threshold. The
lip of threshold still standing above it is exactly the underlayment thickness
to buy. Check both ends of the doorway and several points along the wall; in an
older house the gap varies. As of this note the gap is estimated at roughly
2 cm from photos, not yet measured properly.

## Why not battens at 2 cm

Assessment, from standard Japanese construction practice: the 303 mm module the
operator remembered as "about 30 cm" is the standard batten and joist pitch, and
sheet goods are sized 910 x 1820 mm to land on it. But the thinnest batten stock
worth using is about 15 mm, and thin plywood spanning 303 mm between battens
flexes under point loads like chair legs; the standard sheet over that span is
12 mm structural plywood. The minimum honest batten stack is therefore about
15 + 12 + 12 = 39 mm, roughly double the gap available. Direct-glued plywood
underlayment is the standard method at this height.

## The stack, by measured gap

KINO is 12 mm; a thin underlay sheet under a floating floor adds 1 to 2 mm and
counts in the stack. Use the sheet as the fine-tuning layer:

| Gap at doorway | Under the KINO | Total build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| about 18 mm | 5.5 mm lauan plywood | 17.5 mm |
| about 21 mm | 9 mm structural plywood | 21 mm |
| about 22-23 mm | 9 mm ply + 1-2 mm underlay sheet | 22-23 mm |
| about 24 mm | 12 mm structural plywood | 24 mm |
| about 25-26 mm | 12 mm ply + 1-2 mm underlay sheet | 25-26 mm |
| about 27 mm | 15 mm structural plywood | 27 mm |

Home-center plywood comes in 2.5, 4, 5.5, 9, 12 and 15 mm (Assessment: standard
stocked sizes; confirm at the store). Aim flush to 1 mm below the threshold,
never above it: a floor proud of the threshold catches feet permanently, and
1 mm shy disappears.

## Shopping list

- Structural plywood (構造用合板, F☆☆☆☆ formaldehyde rating), 910 x 1820 mm,
  thickness from the table. Cases of KINO: room area in square meters divided by
  2.4, rounded up, plus a case for cuts and the no-pieces-under-20-cm rule.
- One sheet of thin lauan plywood (2.5 or 4 mm) if the gap needs it.
- A thin underlay sheet for click flooring (下地シート, 1 to 2 mm) if the gap
  math calls for it; the KINO seller stocks them.
- Urethane floor adhesive in cartridges for the plywood layer only; Konishi
  床職人 KU928C-X is the common one. The cartridge states its coverage.
- Coarse-thread screws 32 to 38 mm for the plywood.
- KINO's own transition strips for exposed edges if wanted. FACT from
  [the seller's strip page](https://www.a-yuka.com/floortile/kinom.html) (read
  2026-09-19): slope type about 980 x 50 x 14.6 mm and side type about
  980 x 30 x 14.6 mm, matched to the 12 mm tile, in the same three colors,
  installed with removable double-sided tape.
- A 1.8 m aluminum straightedge and a level. No staples, no tacker, no spacers:
  the flooring floats, and each KINO case includes L-angle spacers for the 5 mm
  wall gap (FACT, same product page).

## Method

1. Map the floor: the plank-offcut check plus the straightedge on a rough grid,
   numbers chalked on the floor. This shows whether the floor is flat-but-low
   (this method) or dipping away from the doorway (see the last section).
2. Screw the existing boards down into their joists wherever anything squeaks or
   moves. Only chance to kill squeaks forever.
3. Plywood: joints staggered, 2 to 3 mm gap between sheets, about 5 mm at walls,
   adhesive in a bead grid plus screws about every 15 cm on sheet edges and 20 cm
   in the field. Keep plywood joints off the existing board joints.
4. Let the flooring acclimatize in the room for a few days. Then lay it the
   KINO way, per the seller's instructions (same product page): start at the
   left of the room with the click receivers facing right and toward you, 5 mm
   gap at every wall held by the included L-angles, short edges pressed down to
   lock, each row started with the offcut from the previous row so joints
   stagger, no piece shorter than 20 cm (they curl), cut with a hand saw and
   measure the last piece in each row against the wall rather than with a tape.
   To take a piece back out, slide it apart; prying breaks the lock.
5. Door casings: rather than scribing tiles around them, cut the casing bottom
   with a hand saw laid flat on a flooring offcut and slide the tile under.
6. At the threshold the floating floor still needs its expansion gap. Cover it
   with KINO's side-type transition strip taped to the threshold face, or leave
   a neat 3 to 5 mm shadow gap if the strip's small flange standing proud at
   the doorway is worse than the gap (Assessment: judge it in place).

## Rubber or sound-dampening under the layers?

No, in either position (operator asked 2026-09-19). Assessment: acoustic
underlays and rubber mats exist for apartment floors, where the requirement is
footstep noise reaching the unit below; on the ground floor of a detached house
there is nobody below to protect, and the soft layer costs real things. A
compressible layer under nailed flooring lets the floor flex on every step, so
staples and glue joints work loose and the floor squeaks within a year. It makes
the floor feel spongy underfoot, the standard complaint even where the mats are
mandatory. And it eats 3 to 10 mm of a roughly 20 mm height budget.

What makes the floor quiet is the method above: old boards screwed into the
joists first, plywood glued and screwed, so the base is one stiff slab. The
flooring itself is now known to be a floating click floor (KINO, above), and a
floating floor is the one case that takes a thin sheet: 1 to 2 mm foam or felt
directly under the tiles as a slip and leveling layer. That thin sheet is the
whole allowance; a thick rubber acoustic mat is still wrong here for the same
reasons.

A moisture film between the old floor and the plywood is also wrong here: the
glue cannot bond through plastic, and over a normal dry crawlspace it is not
needed. A damp crawlspace is fixed from below, not by changing this stack.

## If the floor is not flat

If the mapping shows the floor dropping more than a few millimeters away from the
doorway, decide between following the floor (the plywood follows the dip; easy,
acceptable in a living area) and leveling it (battens with shims, which needs the
40 mm class of depth and belongs with the kitchen-side framing rebuild, not this
overlay). The kitchen subfloor is being rebuilt from the framing anyway; set its
finish height off this same threshold so the whole chocolate brown run comes out
flat.

## How much to trust this

The method and the sheet sizes are standard practice and standard stock, stated
here from general construction knowledge, not from staged sources: treat the
dimensions as Assessment and confirm thickness availability at the home center.
The one number that decides everything, the real gap, has not been measured yet;
nothing should be bought until the plank-offcut check is done.
