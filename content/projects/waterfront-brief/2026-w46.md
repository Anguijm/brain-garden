Two yard periods the Navy booked will end before their ships come home, and the repair period last year's budget booked for a destroyer that lost power at sea in July is missing from this year's funded list. Keyport, meanwhile, has put a number on the parts it pulls from retired ships. This is a short issue: three dated items to watch, then the events calendar.

## On the watch

### Three things to watch

**Two booked yard periods end before their ships come home.** [The Navy's April 2026 operations budget book](https://web.archive.org/web/20260927025227/https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/27pres/OMN_Book.pdf) books the destroyer John Finn's repair period at its home port of Yokosuka, Japan, from Nov. 2 to Jan. 19, and the carrier George H.W. Bush's maintenance period at Portsmouth, Va., from Nov. 22 to Jan. 6. John Finn's families were told to expect the ship next March, [Defense One reported](https://www.defenseone.com/policy/2026/10/yearlong-deployments-navy-new-normal/416777/). Bush left Norfolk on March 31, [the Navy announced](https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/Press-Releases/display-pressreleases/Article/4451254/george-h-w-bush-carrier-strike-group-departs-for-deployment/). Sailors deploying with carrier groups are told to expect 10 to 11 months away, [USNI News reported on Oct. 29](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/29/long-deployments-to-be-navy-standard-for-next-couple-years-mcpon-cno-say). By our count, that puts Bush home in late January or later, after its booked window has closed.

**A destroyer that went dark in July has dropped off the funded repair list.** Benfold lost generator power on July 24 and was towed to Subic Bay, [USNI News reported in August](https://news.usni.org/2026/08/14/uss-benfold-was-stalled-in-the-south-china-sea-for-four-days-after-engineering-casualty). It returned to Everett, Wash., on Oct. 16, [Stars and Stripes reported](https://www.stripes.com/branches/navy/2026-10-17/ben-fold-returns-everett-23181392.html), and [USNI News reported on Nov. 5](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/05/uss-stout-loses-power-during-port-visit-due-to-engineering-casualty) that it "will now undergo maintenance." [The Navy's fiscal 2026 budget book](https://web.archive.org/web/20250709201504/https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/26pres/OMN_Book.pdf) booked Benfold a \$37 million private repair period on the West Coast from Oct. 26, 2026, to June 20, 2027. [The April 2026 book's list of funded repair periods](https://web.archive.org/web/20260927025227/https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/27pres/OMN_Book.pdf) does not include it, and lists \$2.1 million of Benfold's maintenance as unfunded and deferred. The record does not say whether the period was dropped, moved or paid for another way. [The same USNI News report said](https://news.usni.org/2026/11/05/uss-stout-loses-power-during-port-visit-due-to-engineering-casualty) the destroyer Stout lost power in port in Italy on Oct. 25 and had "full power restored within three days." The Navy named Benfold's generators; it has given no cause for Stout.

**One center has put a number on what retired ships supply: \$36 million a year.** [The Naval Undersea Warfare Center at Keyport said on Oct. 30](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/News/Article-View/Article/4638516/nuwc-division-keyport-saves-millions-through-parts-reclamation/), by its own count, that its teams have pulled parts from decommissioned ships generally and returned about \$36 million a year to fleet sonar programs since fiscal 2025. The teams "expect their workload to double over the next five years" as Ohio-class submarines retire. "Each boat can provide up to 200 backorder fulfillments," [project lead Bob Karp said in the release](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/News/Article-View/Article/4638516/nuwc-division-keyport-saves-millions-through-parts-reclamation/), meaning parts orders the supply system could not otherwise fill, and the parts ship "as-is" to Naval Supply Systems Command or the Defense Logistics Agency.

**Bottom line:** A repair period that drops off the funded list, as Benfold's did, can leave a ship home with work no funded schedule holds. Plan crews and pier time for that work against the windows long deployments are emptying, like Bush's and John Finn's.
