The weekly issue says what happened. This page is what a person running an availability
needs beside it: the rules a package is certified to, the plans and reports the queue is
read from, the vault's own explanations of how the maintenance system is organised, and
the studies the trade press never carries. Every line carries the date somebody here last
checked it. The dates on the standards are the record's own, read from DLA's ASSIST
database; nothing on this page is inferred.
